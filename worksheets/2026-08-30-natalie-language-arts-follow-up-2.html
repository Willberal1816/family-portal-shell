Natalie — Sentence types and paragraph development
Only the remaining skills from Follow-Up 1: distinguish compound from complex sentences, then finish a short paragraph with support and a conclusion.
1. Classify this sentence as compound or complex: “I finished my work, and I put the papers in the folder.” Explain how you know.
2. Classify this sentence as compound or complex: “Because the rain stopped, we went outside.” Explain how you know.
3. Complete this short paragraph by adding one supporting sentence and one concluding sentence: “Cooking is a useful skill because it helps people become more independent.”
