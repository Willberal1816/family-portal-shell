Heart Rate & Movement

Emma — Shared Health / Life Skills
Heart-rate movement lab. You need a timer/clock and enough safe space to move. Stop if you feel dizzy, painful, or unwell.

1. Find your pulse

After the short lesson, sit quietly for 2 minutes. Count heartbeats for 15 seconds and multiply by 4.
Resting heart rate: ______ beats per minute

2. Move

Do 1 minute of brisk movement you can do safely: marching, walking quickly, dancing, or similar.
Count again for 15 seconds and multiply by 4.
Heart rate after movement: ______ beats per minute
Difference: ______ beats per minute

3. Recover

Sit or stand quietly for 2 minutes, then measure one more time.
Recovery heart rate: ______ beats per minute

4. What happened?

a) How did movement change your heart rate?
b) Why do you think your body changes heart rate during exercise?
c) What happened during recovery?

5. Emma challenge

If you repeated the same movement tomorrow, what would you try to keep the same so the comparison is fair?
