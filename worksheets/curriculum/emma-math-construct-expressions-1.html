Build Numerical Expressions

Emma · Math · Independent Work
Directions: Write a numerical expression for each situation. Do not solve unless the question asks you to check it. Use parentheses when a quantity must be completed before the next operation.
1. Add 9 and 6, then multiply the result by 3.
2. Five bags hold 8 marbles each, then 7 extra marbles are added.
3. Start with 42 cards, remove 10, then split the rest equally among 4 players.
4. A baker makes 4 trays with 12 rolls on each tray and sells 9 rolls. Write the expression and explain what each part represents.
5. Which expression matches “double the sum of 7 and 5”: 2 × 7 + 5 or 2 × (7 + 5)? Explain why.
6. Error analysis: A student writes 36 − 12 ÷ 6 for “subtract 12 from 36, then divide the remainder by 6.” Correct the expression and explain the error.
7. Write words that match the expression 5 × (4 + 3). Your words must make the parentheses necessary.
8. Write a real-world situation for (24 − 8) ÷ 4. Then evaluate it and label the answer with a useful unit.
9. Create your own two-step situation and write a matching expression with parentheses. Explain how you checked that the expression tells the same story.
