Unit Rates & Proportions

Natalie · Math

Mini Lesson

A unit rate tells the amount for one unit. Once you know the unit rate, you can scale up or down to compare situations.

Practice

1. 5 notebooks cost $8.75. What is the cost per notebook?
2. A car travels 156 miles on 6 gallons of fuel. What is the unit rate in miles per gallon?
3. Store A sells 8 ounces for $3.20. Store B sells 12 ounces for $4.56. Which is the better price per ounce?
4. Write a proportion that represents 7 tickets costing $31.50 and use it to find the cost of 10 tickets.
