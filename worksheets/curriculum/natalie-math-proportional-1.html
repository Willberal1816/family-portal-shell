Unit Rates & Proportions

Natalie · Math
Directions: Show unit labels. Compare means calculate the same kind of unit rate for each choice, write both rates, and state which is greater, faster, or the better value as the question asks. When a question says explain, write the answer and one math reason.

Find unit rates

1. 42 miles in 3.5 hours: miles per hour?
2. $8.40 for 6 pounds apples: dollars per pound?
3. 135 pages in 9 minutes: pages per minute?
4. 5 cups paint cover 650 sq ft: square feet per cup?

Compare

5. Store A: 18 oz cereal $5.22; Store B: 24 oz $6.72. Compare cost per ounce.
6. Runner A travels 4.5 miles in 36 min; Runner B travels 6 miles in 51 min. Find the same unit rate for both runners, write both rates with units, and state which runner is faster.

Proportions

7. 2.5 cups rice for 8 servings. How much for 14?
8. Map: 3 cm = 45 km. What does 7.5 cm represent?
9. Table x:2,4,7; y:6,12,21. Is it proportional? Find constant.
10. Table x:1,2,3; y:5,8,11. Write Yes or No for proportional. Then write one sentence describing the pattern you checked and why it does or does not have a constant unit rate.

Reason and create

11. Why is “both quantities increase” not enough to prove proportionality? Give a counterexample.
12. Create a proportional cooking/travel/shopping situation with three equivalent pairs, unit rate, and equation.
13. Why is a $4 starting fee + $2/mile not proportional?
