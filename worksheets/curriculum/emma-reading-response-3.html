Independent Reading Response

Emma · Reading · Response Set 3
Read for 25 minutes first. Use details from today’s pages, not general facts about the whole book.
1. What problem or conflict mattered most in today’s reading? Explain what made it difficult.
2. Describe one setting detail and explain how it affected what a character could do, feel, or notice.
3. What is one thing the narrator or viewpoint lets you understand well? Give one story detail that proves it.
4. Choose one word or phrase whose meaning depends on context. Copy it, explain the meaning, and name the clue.
5. Summarize today’s reading in exactly 3 sentences: beginning situation, most important development, and where things stand now.
6. Write one question you still have. Then give one detail from the text that makes that question important.
