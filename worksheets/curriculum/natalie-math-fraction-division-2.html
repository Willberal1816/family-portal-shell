Fraction Division 2

Natalie · Math
Directions: For calculation questions, write the fraction setup and the steps you use. Predict means decide how large the answer should be before calculating. Explain means write your answer plus at least one math reason. Verify means check the answer with multiplication.

Meaning and setup

1. Write one ribbon-cutting sentence that shows what 5 14 ÷ 34 is asking: how many 34-foot pieces fit into 5 14 feet. Do not solve yet.
2. Convert 3 25 and 1 34 to improper fractions. Identify which fraction is flipped in the division expression.
3. Before calculating, choose: will 2 12 ÷ 56 be greater than or less than 2 12? Then write one sentence explaining why dividing by a number less than 1 changes the size that way.

Compute carefully

4. 4 12 ÷ 1 12
5. 3 34 ÷ 1 14
6. 2 23 ÷ 56
7. 5 16 ÷ 23
8. 78 ÷ 1 34

Applications

9. A 6 34-foot board is cut into 1 18-foot pieces. How many full pieces can be cut, and what does any leftover mean?
10. A recipe uses 23 cup yogurt per batch. You have 3 12 cups. How many full batches can you make and what remains?
11. A cyclist travels 8 14 miles in equal 1 12-mile segments. Calculate the answer. Then write one sentence saying what the answer tells you about the number of segments.

Reason and create

12. A student writes 2 14 ÷ 35 = 94 × 35. Circle or name the incorrect step, write the corrected multiplication setup, and solve it.
13. Without fully calculating, decide which is larger: 4 ÷ 23 or 4 ÷ 56. Write one sentence using this idea: smaller groups fit into 4 more times than larger groups.
14. Write a real-world mixed-number division problem whose answer is between 2 and 4. Then (1) write the division equation, (2) solve and simplify it, and (3) multiply to check that your answer works.
