Fraction Division 2

Natalie · Math
Directions: Show enough work to make your reasoning visible. Estimate or predict the size of the answer when asked.

Meaning and setup

1. Explain what 5 1/4 ÷ 3/4 means in a ribbon-cutting situation without solving yet.
2. Convert 3 2/5 and 1 3/4 to improper fractions. Identify which fraction is flipped in the division expression.
3. Predict whether 2 1/2 ÷ 5/6 is greater or less than 2 1/2 and explain.

Compute carefully

4. 4 1/2 ÷ 1 1/2
5. 3 3/4 ÷ 1 1/4
6. 2 2/3 ÷ 5/6
7. 5 1/6 ÷ 2/3
8. 7/8 ÷ 1 3/4

Applications

9. A 6 3/4-foot board is cut into 1 1/8-foot pieces. How many full pieces can be cut, and what does any leftover mean?
10. A recipe uses 2/3 cup yogurt per batch. You have 3 1/2 cups. How many full batches can you make and what remains?
11. A cyclist travels 8 1/4 miles in equal 1 1/2-mile segments. Interpret the quotient.

Reason and create

12. A student writes 2 1/4 ÷ 3/5 = 9/4 × 3/5. Diagnose and correct the exact error.
13. Without fully calculating, decide which is larger: 4 ÷ 2/3 or 4 ÷ 5/6. Explain using group size.
14. Create a mixed-number division word problem with a quotient between 2 and 4. Solve and verify by multiplication.
