Specific Supporting Details

Emma · Language Arts

Mini Lesson

A strong paragraph makes one clear point and supports it with specific details. Details should prove or explain the main idea, not just repeat it.

Practice

1. Write a topic sentence about why cooking is a useful skill.
2. Add one specific supporting detail.
3. Add a second different supporting detail.
4. Write a concluding sentence that wraps up the paragraph.
5. Underline the sentence that gives the strongest evidence for your main idea.
