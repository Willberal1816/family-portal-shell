Choose the Operation

Natalie · Math
Directions: For each application, name the operation and give a one-sentence reason before calculating. Label units.

Choose without solving

1. A 7.5-mile route is split into 5 equal sections. Name the operation and what the result means.
2. A $48 jacket is discounted to 3/4 of original price. Name the operation and explain.
3. A tank holds 12.8 gallons and 3.6 are used. Name the operation and explain.
4. Temperatures differ by 17.5°. One is -4.2° and the other is warmer. Describe an operation to find it.

Solve and check

5. 2/3 cup flour per loaf for 7 loaves. How much flour?
6. 5 1/4 liters poured into 3/8-liter bottles. How many full bottles?
7. $36.75 shared among 5 people. Each share?
8. 2.6 miles morning + 3 3/4 miles afternoon. Total?

Multi-step

9. A 9.6-mile trail has 4 equal stages. Natalie walks 1.25 stages. How many miles? Label each step.
10. 3/4 cup rice makes 4 servings. How much for 10 servings? Use a unit rate or scale factor.

Analyze

11. Give an example where the word “each” requires division, not multiplication.
12. Create a two-step problem using a fraction or decimal. Solve, label units, and explain operation order.
