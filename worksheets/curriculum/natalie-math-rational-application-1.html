Mini Lesson

Strong problem solving starts by deciding what the quantities mean before calculating. Circle or write the operation you chose, then solve.

Practice

1. A recipe needs 2/3 cup of oats per batch. You have 4 cups. How many full batches can you make?
2. A runner travels 6.75 miles in 1.5 hours at a steady pace. What is the average miles per hour?
3. A tank is 3/5 full. Then 1/4 of the tank capacity is added. What fraction of the tank is full now?
4. Explain why the first problem uses division instead of multiplication.
