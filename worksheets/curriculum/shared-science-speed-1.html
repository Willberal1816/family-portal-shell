Speed: Distance and Time

Shared Science

Mini Lesson

Speed compares distance traveled with the time taken. Average speed can be found with distance ÷ time. Always include units.

Practice

1. A cyclist travels 12 miles in 2 hours. What is the average speed?
2. A toy car moves 6 meters in 3 seconds. What is its average speed?
3. Two runners travel the same distance. Runner A takes less time. Who has the greater average speed and why?
4. A dog runs 150 meters in 30 seconds. Find the average speed.
5. Write one situation where average speed does not tell you every detail about the motion.
