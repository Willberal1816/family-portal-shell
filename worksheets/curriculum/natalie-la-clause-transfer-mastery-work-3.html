Kinds of Sentences — Clause Transfer Mastery

For 1–5: bracket each whole clause, label it I or D, then name the sentence type. Add any needed punctuation.

2. I finished the chapter and I wrote my response.
3. When the clouds cleared we went outside.
4. Although the trail was muddy we kept walking and we reached the lake.
5. Mia cooked dinner but I washed the dishes after everyone ate.
6. Write one compound sentence with exactly two independent clauses. Bracket both clauses and label each I.
7. Write one complex sentence with one dependent and one independent clause. Bracket and label both clauses.
