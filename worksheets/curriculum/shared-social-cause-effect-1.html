Cause, Effect & Chronology

Shared Social Studies

Mini Lesson

Historical events usually have more than one cause and more than one effect. Chronology helps us see what happened before, during, and after an event.

Practice

1. Put these in logical order: roads close, heavy rain falls, river rises, cleanup begins.
2. Name one immediate effect and one longer-term effect a flood could have on a town.
3. Why is “it happened first” not always enough to prove something caused another event?
4. Write one possible cause-and-effect chain with at least three steps.
5. What additional evidence would help you test whether your claimed cause really mattered?
