Coordinate Direction & Address — Independent Work

Complete all parts independently after Learn First. Keep horizontal x movement and vertical y movement separate.

Address rule: write (x,y). Right/east = +x, left/west = -x, up/north = +y, down/south = -y. If x or y is zero, check for an axis before naming a quadrant.
