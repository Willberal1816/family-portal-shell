Complete Sentences

Emma · Language Arts

Mini Lesson

A complete sentence needs a subject and a complete thought. A fragment may have useful words but still leave the reader waiting for the rest.

Practice

1. Is this complete or a fragment: Because the rain started suddenly.
2. Repair it if needed.
3. Is this complete or a fragment: The dog under the table barked at the vacuum.
4. Repair this fragment: While we were waiting for dinner.
5. Write one complete sentence that begins with Although.
