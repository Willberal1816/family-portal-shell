Fix the Sentence

Emma · Language Arts
Directions: Rewrite the entire corrected sentence. When more than one answer works, choose the clearest version.

Diagnose

1. Label Complete, Fragment, or Run-on: “The tiny kitten slept in the basket.”
2. Label: “Because the movie ended late.”
3. Label: “I packed my lunch I forgot my water bottle.”
4. Label: “The wind shook the windows, the dog hid under the table.”

Repair

5. Fix: “While Emma waited for the cookies to cool.”
6. Fix: “The trail was steep we kept hiking.”
7. Fix the comma splice: “I wanted the blue marker, Natalie was using it.”
8. Combine clearly: “The plant leaned toward the window. It needed more light.”

Choose punctuation

9. Join with a comma and a joining word: “The recipe looked easy. We read every step first.”
10. Rewrite as two sentences: “The puppy chased the ball it slid under the chair he barked at it.”

Edit in context

11. Rewrite: “Saturday morning. We planned to ride bikes the tire was flat. Because Dad found the pump. We fixed it, we left after lunch.”
12. Write a 5–6 sentence paragraph about a small problem. Include one sentence joined with and, but, or so. Reread and check every ending mark.
