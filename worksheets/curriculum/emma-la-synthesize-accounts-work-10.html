Synthesize Two Accounts — Independent Work

Directions: Read both short accounts. Use exact details from both. Your final synthesis must be neutral and must include what the accounts share plus one useful contribution from each.
Account A — park ranger: “At 2:10 p.m. the north trail was closed after water covered the wooden footbridge. Rangers placed barriers at both trail entrances and directed hikers to the ridge route.”
Account B — hiker: “We reached the footbridge a little after two and saw water moving over the boards. A ranger stopped us before the bridge and showed us a higher trail. The detour added about twenty minutes, but we stayed dry.”
1. List two facts supported by both accounts.
2. What useful detail does Account A add that Account B does not?
3. What useful detail does Account B add that Account A does not?
4. Explain how the writers’ roles affect what each account emphasizes.
5. Write a neutral 5-sentence synthesis: shared event, Account A contribution, Account B contribution, how the details fit together, and a final sentence describing the fuller picture.
6. Underline one exact detail from each account that you used in your synthesis. Explain why each detail was worth including.
