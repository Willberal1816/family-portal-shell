Clauses — Build the Connector Map

For each sentence: bracket every whole clause, circle the connector, label each clause I or D, then name the sentence type.
1. The dog scratched at the door, and Maya let him outside.
2. Because the sidewalk was icy, we walked slowly to the car.
3. I saved the last cookie because my sister had not eaten dessert.
4. The lights flickered, but the computer stayed on when the storm passed over us.
Tracking #
5. Connector contrast. Compare these two sentences. A: “The bell rang, so we packed our bags.” B: “When the bell rang, we packed our bags.” Label every clause I/D and explain in 2–3 sentences why A is compound but B is complex.
6. Find the hidden dependent clause. “We finished the game although the rain had started.” Explain why although the rain had started is dependent even though “the rain had started” sounds complete without the connector.
7. Error analysis. A student says, “Any sentence with three clauses is compound-complex.” Explain why that rule is wrong. Then make up one three-clause sentence that is not compound-complex and label its clauses.
Tracking #
8. Repair a fragment. “Although the movie ended late.” Add one independent clause to make a complete complex sentence. Bracket and label both clauses and punctuate correctly.
9. Build a compound sentence. Write two independent clauses about preparing for a trip. Join them with a coordinating conjunction and correct punctuation. Label I + I.
10. Build a complex sentence. Write one independent clause and one dependent clause about weather. Use because, when, although, if, while, or since. Label the clauses and punctuate correctly.
11. Build a compound-complex sentence. Write a new sentence containing at least two independent clauses and one dependent clause. Bracket each whole clause, label I/D, circle the connectors, and explain in one sentence why the result is compound-complex.
12. Transfer check. “While the bread baked, Dad washed the dishes, and I set the table.” Bracket each clause, label I/D, name the sentence type, and explain what each connector is doing.
