Use this reference while you work

Student recipe, one separate dough each: 100 g all-purpose or 00 flour + 1 large egg; a little extra flour to dust. Shared family total: 200 g plus dusting flour, 2 eggs. Tools: two bowls or clean stations, fork, clean board, kitchen scale, towel or wrap, rolling pin, bench scraper, adult-supervised knife or pasta cutter, two labeled trays. Wash hands and surfaces after raw egg/flour.

MinuteTask
0–5Wash hands, prepare two labeled stations.
5–15Measure flour; parent helps crack egg; mix with fork.
15–25Knead until a smooth, cohesive dough ball forms.
25–55Cover; rest dough about 30 minutes in refrigerator. Record measurements and explain method during rest.
55–70On floured board roll from middle outward, turn, dust lightly; adult supervises cutting into even ribbons.
70–80Label/stow dough cold if not cooked now, clean raw-egg/flour surfaces, scan records.

Safety: Do not taste raw dough; wash after touching uncooked eggs/flour. Keep knife flat on board, fingers away, adult within reach. No boiling water or Alfredo sauce in this class.

1. Write your actual flour weight, egg count and dough label; compare with the target amounts.
2. Write the starting time and ending time of the covered 30-minute rest.
3. List four actions in the correct order from mixing through the end of kneading.
4. Name two raw flour/egg safety actions you did and one adult-supervised knife rule.
