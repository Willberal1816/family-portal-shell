Newton’s First Law & Inertia

Shared Science

Mini Lesson

An object does not need a continuing unbalanced force just to keep moving at constant speed in a straight line. A net unbalanced force changes motion; inertia is the tendency to keep the current state of motion.

Practice

1. A hockey puck slides at steady speed in a straight line. Are the forces necessarily unbalanced? Explain.
2. A book rests on a table. Describe two forces that can balance.
3. A bus stops suddenly and a standing passenger lurches forward. Explain using inertia.
4. Give one everyday example of an object changing motion because of an unbalanced force.
5. Correct this statement: If an object is moving, an unbalanced force must be pushing it forward.
