Emma · Language Arts

Mini Lesson

Use the skills from this week together: complete sentences, exact text evidence, specific details, and careful editing.

Practice

1. Passage: Nora carried the plant to the brightest window and turned the pot so every leaf faced the light. What can you infer about Nora?
2. Quote or copy the exact clue that supports the inference.
3. Repair: Hoping the new leaves would grow quickly.
4. Write two connected sentences explaining one way people can help a houseplant grow.
5. Reread your last two sentences and make one editing improvement.
