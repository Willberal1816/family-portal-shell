Language Arts Review & Apply

Emma · Language Arts
Directions: Work independently first. This is a weekly evidence sample, not a high-stakes test. Complete every requested part. When asked to explain or reason, give your answer, name the clue/detail, and write one sentence connecting the two.

Sentences

1. Label and fix: “After the bus finally arrived.”
2. Fix: “The trail was muddy we took the longer path.”
3. Fix: “I found the missing page, it was under my notebook.”

Evidence

4. “Jon zipped his coat, checked dark clouds, and moved chairs under the porch.” Make an inference and give two clues.
5. Why is “Jon is worried because he is worried” not evidence-based reasoning?

Details and clarity

6. Revise “The party was fun and there was good stuff” with two specific details.
7. Clarify: “When Nora handed Lily her drawing, she smiled.”
8. Replace one vague verb in “The dog went to the door and got the toy.”

Combine skills

9. Lights flicker; Dad sets out flashlight and charges phone; branches bend in wind. Write 5-7 sentences about what the family may expect. Include exactly two clues from the situation and at least one sentence explaining how a clue supports your idea.
10. Write 7–9 sentences about a small challenge you solved, with complete sentences and three concrete details. Mark one clarity revision.
11. Which skill felt easiest this week and which needs more practice? Give a specific example.
