Food, Energy & Balanced Meals

Shared Health

Mini Lesson

Meals do different jobs: carbohydrates are a major energy source, protein supports growth and repair, fats support cells and energy storage, and fruits/vegetables provide vitamins, minerals, water, and fiber.

Practice

1. Build a balanced lunch with one protein food, one carbohydrate-rich food, and at least one fruit or vegetable.
2. What role does protein play in the body?
3. Why is “healthy” not the same as “eat as little as possible”?
4. Choose one familiar meal and name one way to add variety or balance.
5. Explain why water belongs in a discussion about nutrition.
