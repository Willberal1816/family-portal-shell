Clause Decision Recheck

For 1–5: bracket every whole clause, label each clause I or D, and name the sentence type. Under each D label, write 2–4 words explaining what makes the thought incomplete.

1. We packed the cooler, and Dad loaded the chairs.
2. Because the sidewalk was wet, I wore my old shoes.
3. The lights came on when the sun went down.
4. Although I wanted to stay, the store closed, so we headed home.
5. I called Natalie, but she was reading after dinner ended.
6. In one sentence, explain the stand-alone test. Your answer must say what happens when you read an independent clause alone and what happens when you read a dependent clause alone.
