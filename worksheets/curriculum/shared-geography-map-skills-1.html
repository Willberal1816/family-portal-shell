Map Skills

Shared · Geography
A map gives clues about where places are. Use direction words and the map key carefully.
1. Draw a small map of one room in your home. Add a north arrow.
2. Add a key with at least three symbols, such as a door, table, or window.
3. Write directions for getting from one place on your map to another.
4. Name one thing your map shows well and one thing it leaves out.
