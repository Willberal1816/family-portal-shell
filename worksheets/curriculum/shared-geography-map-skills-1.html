Map Skills

Emma + Natalie · Geography
Directions: Use the fictional map information in each problem. Show scale calculations. When asked to explain a map choice, name the map you chose and write one sentence naming the map feature that makes it useful for that question.

Read map tools

1. Legend: ★ hospital, ▲ park, ● school. What symbol matters for emergency care and why is the legend necessary?
2. North is up. The library is directly right of school. What direction is it?
3. Grid columns A-F, rows 1-6; museum C4. Write one sentence explaining how the letter-and-number grid helps someone locate one exact place.

Scale

4. 1 cm = 20 km; towns are 3.5 cm apart. Find real distance.
5. 2 inches = 50 miles; route is 5 inches. Find real distance.
6. Correct: 4 cm on a 1 cm = 12 km map equals 16 km.

Choose the map

7. Choose the best map for mountain ranges: political, physical, or population-density. Then write one sentence naming the feature that makes your choice useful.
8. Choose the best map for showing where corn is grown: road, physical, or crop-production thematic. Then write one sentence naming the feature that makes your choice useful.
9. Give one question a political map answers well and one it answers poorly.

Reason

10. Emma: Design an imaginary park map with title, north arrow, five symbols, legend, and scale.
11. Natalie: A “County Population, 2000” map is used to decide a 2026 clinic site. Identify two useful features and two limitations.
12. Write one sentence naming one way a map simplifies, selects, or changes real-world information instead of copying everything exactly.
