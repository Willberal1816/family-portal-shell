Fresh story: Six teams each receive six stickers, and the organizer keeps two spare badges outside all team bundles.

RepeatOne-time extra
6 equal groups of 62 extra items

Two different expressions: 6 × 6 + 2 and 6 × (6 + 2). They tell DIFFERENT stories.

1. Name exactly which amount repeats and which amount happens only once in the story. Explain in two sentences.
2. Draw and label the equal groups and the separate extra items. Use a small box/circle for each group.
3. Write the correct expression with ×, ÷, + and brackets if necessary. Evaluate every operation and state units.
4. Evaluate the OTHER printed expression and explain why it answers a different story.
