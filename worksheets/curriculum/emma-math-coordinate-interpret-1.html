Plot & Interpret Coordinates

Emma · Math

Mini Lesson

Coordinates describe position. Read x first, then y. When you compare points, notice which coordinate changes and what that means on the graph.

Practice

1. Plot A=(-4,2), B=(-1,2), C=(-1,-3), and D=(3,-3).
2. Which two points share the same y-coordinate? What does that tell you?
3. Which two points share the same x-coordinate? What does that tell you?
4. Starting at (-2,1), move 5 units right and 4 units down. What point do you reach?
5. Write a point in Quadrant II and explain how its signs prove it belongs there.
