Horizontal & Vertical Distance

Emma · Math
Directions: Use the printed coordinate planes for plotting and route questions. Show how you counted or subtracted. Distance is always a positive amount.

Same-line distances

1. Find the distance between (-4,2) and (5,2). Explain which coordinate stays the same and why the segment is horizontal.
2. Find the distance between (3,-6) and (3,4).
3. Find the distance between (-7,-3) and (-2,-3).
4. Find the distance between (0,5) and (0,-4).

Plot and reason

5. Plot A(-5,4), B(2,4), C(2,-2). Draw A→B→C. Find AB, BC, and total route distance.
6. Create and plot two points 8 units apart horizontally that are both in Quadrant III. Label the points and show the 8-unit segment.
7. Create and plot two points 6 units apart vertically on opposite sides of the x-axis. Label the points.

Situations

8. Treasure (-3,1) and key (4,1): how far apart are they and which coordinate stays the same?
9. A robot travels (-2,-4) to (-2,3), then to (5,3). Find total distance.
10. Correct the distance from x=-5 to x=2. Explain why distance is positive even when a coordinate is negative.

Challenge

11. Draw an axis-aligned rectangle with integer-coordinate vertices on the printed plane. Label all four vertices and find its perimeter.
12. Explain the difference between what a negative coordinate tells you about location and what a positive distance tells you about how far apart two points are.
