Fictional classroom source packet — Which Water Test Is Stronger?. Source A — two-day garden log: “Bed A: 11 seedlings upright on Monday and 12 on Tuesday; Bed B: 9 Monday and 9 Tuesday.” Source B — caretaker note: “Bed A looks happier.” Source C — weather record: “Rain fell between the two counts.”

1. Identify which source measures the central change and which gives a personal observation. Name the role of the unrelated source.
2. Copy TWO exact numerical facts with dates or conditions. Include units and do not replace them with “more” or “faster.”
3. Calculate the difference in the two counts. Show subtraction and state what that comparison does and does not measure.
4. Write a one-sentence claim limited to THESE observations. Do not use “everyone,” “always,” or “caused” without proof.
