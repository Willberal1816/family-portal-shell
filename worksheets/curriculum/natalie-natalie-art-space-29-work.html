Paper composition frame (use the boxes below; do not redraw a grid):

Frame A — simple leaf silhouetteFrame B — bottle silhouette
Outline a leaf shape; shade the object, leave background white.Outline a bottle; shade the space around it, leave object white.

Materials: pencil, eraser, black marker or dark pencil, optional colored pencil. This is about which SPACE gets attention, not repeating the earlier one-point-perspective hallway or the light/shadow sphere.

1. In Frame A, draw an original plant or leaf silhouette in dark fill. Keep the outer space unfilled.
2. Label the positive and negative spaces in Frame A with arrows or a compact key.
3. In Frame B, draw a different simple object and darken ONLY the surrounding negative space.
4. Write two sentences comparing the filled object and filled background.
