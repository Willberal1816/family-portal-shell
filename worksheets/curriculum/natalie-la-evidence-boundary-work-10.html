Claim: The new library checkout station moved students faster.
Source A: A student said, “The line felt quicker on Thursday.”
Source B: Wednesday: 31 students in 13 minutes. Thursday after the change: 34 students in 8 minutes.
Source C: Thursday’s book display featured mysteries.
1. Rank A, B, and C as strong, partial, or weak for the exact speed claim. Give one specific reason for each.
2. Write one careful claim supported by A and B together. Avoid always, everyone, and proves.
3. Explain the reasoning link: why do the exact numbers in B support a faster-checkout claim?
4. Name one real limitation involving time, cause, or scope. Explain why it matters.
