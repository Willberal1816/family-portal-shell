Viewpoint Evidence Practice

Emma · Language Arts · 55 minutes including Learn First

Scenario: A school garden was damaged during a storm. Account A is written by the student garden leader. Account B is written by the custodian who inspected the grounds the next morning.

Account A: “We tied the tallest tomato plants before the rain. By morning, several stakes had fallen, but the herb boxes were still covered. Our class wanted to replant immediately.”

Account B: “At 6:30 a.m., I found two broken stakes, standing water by the west fence, and loose branches near the walkway. I closed the garden until the path was cleared.”

1. What does each account emphasize? Cite one exact detail from each.
2. Explain how each writer’s role shapes the details selected.
3. Name one thing Account A cannot establish and one thing Account B cannot establish.
4. Which account best answers “Why was the garden closed?” Explain.
5. Write a neutral 4–5 sentence synthesis using evidence from both accounts.
