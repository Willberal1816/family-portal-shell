Make Writing Clear

Emma · Language Arts
Directions: Rewrite the full sentence or passage while preserving the intended meaning. When a question has two jobs, complete both. Explain means write one sentence saying exactly what makes the original wording unclear.

Clear pronouns

1. Rewrite: “When Mia called Emma, she was already outside.” Make clear who was outside.
2. Rewrite: “The dog chased the cat until it reached the porch.” Clarify which animal reached it.
3. Explain why “Ava put the book beside the folder because it was torn” is confusing and fix it.

Replace vague/repeated wording

4. Revise: “We went to the place and got some stuff for the project.”
5. Remove repetition: “The small little box was tiny and small enough to fit in my pocket.”
6. Strengthen verbs: “The squirrel went up the tree and then went across a branch.”

Sentence and order

7. Fix: “I checked the weather it looked clear, we packed our bikes.”
8. Put in logical order: hung wet coats; came in from rain; dried floor near door. Rewrite as paragraph.

Full revision

9. Revise: “Maya had a thing to show Liv. She put it by her chair, she forgot about it. Later she saw the box and remembered the surprise gift. Maya gave it to her and she smiled.”
10. Write 6–8 sentences about completing a task. Revise one pronoun, vague word, sentence boundary, and sequence/transition. Mark changes.
11. Name one revision you made that helped the paragraph most. Then write one sentence saying what became clearer for the reader.
