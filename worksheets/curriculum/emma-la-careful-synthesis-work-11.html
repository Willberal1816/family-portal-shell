Build a Careful Synthesis

Emma · Independent Work · two fresh accounts

Name ____________________ Date __________

Source A · Library repair log

October 3: Staff moved the children’s shelves away from the north wall after a ceiling leak. A repair crew replaced two damaged ceiling tiles on October 5. The shelves returned on October 7 after the wall and floor were dry.

Source B · Student interview

“When I visited that week, the children’s books were on rolling carts near the front desk. I could still borrow a book, but the reading corner was closed. The next week, everything was back where it belonged.”

  1. What can Source A establish? Give two exact details.
  2. What can Source B establish? Give one experience detail.
  3. How does each writer’s role shape the details selected?
  1. Which source is stronger for the exact repair date? Explain.
  2. Which source is stronger for how library use changed during the repair? Explain.
  3. Write a 4–5 sentence neutral synthesis with a claim, evidence from both sources, and one limitation.
  4. Circle one phrase in your synthesis and copy the exact source words that support it.
Turn-in check: claim · Source A evidence · Source B evidence · limitation · no invented facts.
