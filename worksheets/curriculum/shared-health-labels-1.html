Reading a Nutrition Label

Shared Health

Mini Lesson

A nutrition label gives information per serving. Before comparing foods, check serving size, then compare the nutrients that matter for the question you are asking.

Practice

1. Why should you check serving size before comparing two labels?
2. If one package has 2 servings and you eat the whole package, what happens to the listed nutrient amounts?
3. Name one nutrient you might compare when choosing a higher-fiber snack.
4. Why is one number on a label not enough to decide whether a food fits a balanced meal?
5. Write one question a nutrition label can answer and one question it cannot answer by itself.
