Remember: an object can move at steady speed with zero net force. A nonzero net force changes speed or direction. Name actual forces such as gravity, support/normal force, friction, tension, push, or pull.
1. The puck is already sliding right at a steady speed on nearly frictionless ice. Circle YES / NO: does it need a forward net force to keep moving? Explain in 1–2 sentences.
2. A cart moves east at a steady 2 m/s. Write zero or east for the net force, then explain what evidence in the sentence controls your answer.
3. The printed arrows on the moving box are equal: 5 N left and 5 N right. Find the net force and state what can happen to its velocity if no other unbalanced force acts.
4. A rolling marble slows on carpet. Name the force that changes the marble’s motion, state its direction compared with the motion, and explain how you know the net force is not zero.
5. A backpack rests on a car seat while the car is moving right. The car stops suddenly. Draw an arrow showing what the backpack tends to do at first, then explain using inertia.
