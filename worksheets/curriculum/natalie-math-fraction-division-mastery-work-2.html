Applications and transfer

9. Container problem.

You have 5 1/2 liters of juice. Each pitcher holds 1 3/8 liters. How many full pitcher-size groups fit in the juice? Show the conversions, division, and multiplication check. Finish with a sentence that labels the quotient.
