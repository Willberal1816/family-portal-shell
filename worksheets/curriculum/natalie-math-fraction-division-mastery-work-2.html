Fraction Division — How Many Groups Fit?

Complete this page independently after Learn First. For each problem, think about the amount you have and the size of one group before you calculate.

Use this routine: 1) What amount do I have? 2) How big is one group? 3) Should the answer be larger or smaller than the starting amount? 4) Calculate. 5) Say what the answer counts. 6) Check by multiplying the answer by the group size.
