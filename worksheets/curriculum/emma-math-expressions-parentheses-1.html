Expressions with Parentheses

Emma · Math · Independent Work
Directions: Parentheses tell which calculation belongs together first. For word problems, write the expression before evaluating it.
1. Evaluate both expressions and explain why they have different values: 4 × (6 + 3) and (4 × 6) + 3.
2. Write an expression for: “Add 8 and 7, then multiply the sum by 5.” Evaluate it.
3. Write an expression for: “Subtract 12 from 30, then divide the result by 3.” Evaluate it.
4. Maya buys 3 notebooks at $4 each and 3 pens at $2 each. Write one expression using parentheses to show the total cost, then solve.
5. Place parentheses in 6 + 2 × 5 so the value is 40. Then explain what the parentheses change.
6. Create a short real-world problem that can be represented by 4 × (9 + 3). Explain what each number and the parentheses mean in your situation.
