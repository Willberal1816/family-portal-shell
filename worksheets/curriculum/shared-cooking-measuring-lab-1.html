Kitchen Measuring Lab

Shared · Cooking / Life Skills
Use clean measuring cups and spoons. Water is enough for this lab. Ask a parent before using anything else.
1. Measure 1/2 cup of water twice. How much water do you have altogether?
2. Measure 3 teaspoons of water. Compare it with 1 tablespoon. What do you notice?
3. Show 3/4 cup using more than one measuring cup. Write which cups you used.
4. Clean and put away the tools. Write one measuring tip you want to remember.
