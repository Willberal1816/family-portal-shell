Make Pancakes from Scratch — Cooking Record

Emma + Natalie · Cooking / Life Skills
This is a cooking class. Complete the record while you actually prepare, cook, taste, and clean up. A responsible adult should be nearby while the skillet or griddle is hot.

Before heat

☐ Washed hands   ☐ Hair/clothes secured   ☐ Counter cleared   ☐ Recipe read all the way through
1. Write each person’s first job. Make sure both of you will measure ingredients and cook pancakes.
2. Which tool will you use for 1 cup flour? Which tool will you use for 1 cup milk? Why are they different?
3. Two teaspoons of baking powder equals how many one-teaspoon measures? How many 1/4-teaspoon measures make the 1/4 teaspoon salt?

Make the batter

☐ Dry ingredients measured and leveled   ☐ Wet ingredients measured   ☐ Wet and dry mixed separately first   ☐ Batter stirred only until combined
4. Describe the batter after mixing. Was it pourable, very thick, very thin, mostly smooth, or a little lumpy?

Cook

☐ Adult nearby for hot surface   ☐ Pan handle safely positioned   ☐ Used spatula   ☐ No raw batter tasted
5. What signs did you use to decide when the first pancake was ready to flip?
6. Each student cooks at least two pancakes. Record one thing Emma noticed while cooking and one thing Natalie noticed.
7. If one pancake browned too fast or stayed pale too long, what did you change about the heat or timing?

Taste and evaluate

8. Cut one pancake open. Is the center fully cooked? Describe the color and texture.
9. Rate the batch for browning, fluffiness, and taste. Name one thing that worked well.
10. If you made these again, what one change would improve the result or make the kitchen work smoother?

Kitchen math extension

11. Emma: If you wanted half a batch, what would happen to the 1 cup flour and 1 cup milk?
12. Natalie: Calculate the flour, milk, sugar, and baking powder needed for 1 1/2 batches.

Clean and close

☐ Heat turned off   ☐ Dishes washed   ☐ Counter wiped   ☐ Cooking surface cleaned when safe   ☐ Ingredients put away
13. What kitchen habit mattered most today: measuring, organization, heat control, teamwork, or cleanup? Explain briefly.
Turn in this cooking record after the kitchen is reset.
