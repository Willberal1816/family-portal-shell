1. Three boxes each contain 6 red and 4 blue pencils. Write one expression, evaluate it, and say what repeats.
2. Three boxes each contain 6 red pencils, plus 4 loose blue pencils. Write an expression, evaluate it, and explain why it differs from #1.
3. Thirty tickets shared equally among five teams; each team receives four more. Write the expression and final answer.
4. Two bags each contain 7 apples and 5 pears; 3 loose oranges are nearby. Find the total and explain which parts repeat.
5. Calculate 6 + 3 × (5 + 4) − 8. Write every intermediate step and final result.

6. Compare 5 × (8 + 2) with 5 × 8 + 2. Give both answers and a possible story for each.
7. Repair the wrong expression 48 ÷ (6 + 2) for 48 stickers shared among six friends, then each friend gets two more. Give the actual answer.
8. A student computes 7 + 2 × 4 − 3 as 33. State their order mistake and show the correct value.
9. Invent a story for 4 × (3 + 5). Explain what one group holds and find the total.
10. Invent a related story for 4 × 3 + 5. Explain what is added only once, calculate, and write one sentence comparing #9 and #10.
