Expressions in Context — Independent Work

Emma · Math
Directions: For every problem: (1) write one expression, (2) show each evaluation step, and (3) circle the operation that remains outside the parentheses after you simplify them.
1. Five boxes contain 7 markers each. Then 9 markers are used. Write and evaluate the expression.
2. A $48 bill is split equally among 6 people. Each person also pays a $3 fee. Write and evaluate the expression.
3. Four students each read 12 pages Saturday and 8 pages Sunday. Write one expression and evaluate it.
4. Evaluate 36 ÷ (10 − 4). After the parentheses are simplified, write the entire expression again before finishing.
5. Evaluate 7 + 5 × 3 and (7 + 5) × 3. Explain why the answers differ.
6. Error analysis: Ava changes 42 ÷ (9 − 3) into 42 − 6. Identify exactly what operation she lost and correct the work.
7. Write a short real-world situation represented by 6 × (4 + 2). Explain what the parentheses mean in your story.
8. Create two expressions using 2, 5, and 8 where adding parentheses changes the answer. Evaluate both.
