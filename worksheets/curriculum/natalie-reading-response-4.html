Independent Reading Response

Natalie · Reading · Response Set 4
Read for 25 minutes first. Use precise text evidence. Do not treat a reasonable guess as a fact unless the text supports it.
1. Compare two scenes, decisions, or moments from today’s reading. Explain one meaningful way they are connected and one important difference.
2. State one possible theme or larger idea developing in the book. Support it with two specific details from today’s pages.
3. Choose one claim that the text supports only partly. Explain what the text proves and what is still unknown.
4. Explain one character’s motivation. Give two pieces of evidence and identify which is stronger evidence and why.
5. Predict a likely next development. Support it with two clues and identify one uncertainty that could change the outcome.
6. Revise one answer above for greater precision by adding a missing detail, stronger evidence, or a clearer explanation of how the evidence supports your idea.
