Kinds of Sentences — Practice

Natalie · Language Arts
Use what you just learned. Show your thinking when a sentence is tricky. The goal is to understand how the pieces work, not to race through labels.

Part A — Classify and explain

1. The old bridge shook in the wind.
2. We packed lunch, and we left before sunrise.
3. Although the trail was muddy, we kept walking.
4. My brother and I carried the heavy cooler.
5. The clouds grew dark, so we hurried back to the car.
6. When we reached the top, the whole valley came into view.
7. The puppy with the red collar slept under the table.
8. I wanted to finish the puzzle, but dinner was ready.

Part B — Find the building blocks

9. In “Because the road was icy, school started late,” underline the dependent clause and circle the independent clause.
10. In “The power went out, but we found flashlights,” identify the two independent clauses and the joining word.
11. Explain why “While the soup was cooking” is not a complete sentence by itself.

Part C — Build stronger sentences

12. Combine: The rain stopped. We went for a walk. Make a compound sentence.
13. Combine those same ideas again, but this time make a complex sentence.
14. Combine: I finished my homework. I could watch a movie. Make a complex sentence using after, when, because, or since.
15. Combine: The recipe looked difficult. Natalie tried it anyway. Make a compound sentence.

Part D — Fix the writer's mistakes

16. Fix the fragment: Because the dog was scratching at the door.
17. Fix the comma splice: The movie ended, we talked about the ending.
18. Fix this so the meaning is clear: When I opened the box. The kitten jumped out.

Part E — Use the skill in real writing

19. Write a 6–8 sentence paragraph about a book, animal, meal, trip, game, or activity you know well. Include at least one simple sentence, two compound sentences, and two complex sentences. Underline the joining words in your compound/complex sentences. Then write S, CD, or CX beside each sentence.
20. Read your paragraph aloud. Choose one sentence you would revise for better flow and rewrite it here.
