Simple, Compound & Complex Sentences

Natalie · Language Arts

Mini Lesson

A simple sentence has one independent clause. A compound sentence joins two independent clauses. A complex sentence has an independent clause plus a dependent clause.

Practice

1. Classify: I finished the chapter before lunch.
2. Classify: I finished the chapter, and I wrote a response.
3. Classify: Because the chapter ended with a surprise, I kept reading.
4. Combine these into a compound sentence: The rain stopped. We went outside.
5. Write one complex sentence using although, because, when, or while.
