Compare Two Accounts

Emma · Language Arts · Independent Work
Directions: Two people can describe the same event differently because they notice different details or have different points of view. Compare the accounts using evidence from each.
Account A: The school garden workday was exciting. By 9:30, volunteers had filled three wheelbarrows with weeds. I worked near the tomato beds, and our group laughed while we tried to pull out one enormous root. By lunch, the garden looked brighter and easier to walk through.
Account B: Saturday’s garden cleanup took longer than expected. The soil was wet from Friday’s rain, so the wheelbarrows were difficult to push. Several roots had to be dug out with shovels. We finished the main path before lunch, but two garden beds still needed work.
1. What event are both writers describing? List one detail both accounts support.
2. What does Account A emphasize? Quote or copy one exact detail that supports your answer.
3. What does Account B emphasize? Give one exact detail.
4. Do the accounts actually contradict each other, or can both be true? Explain using evidence from both.
5. Which account would help answer “Was the cleanup enjoyable?” Which would help answer “What work remained?” Explain.
6. Write a short neutral account of the workday using information from both writers. Include at least three accurate details.
