Independent Reading Response

Natalie · Reading · Response Set 7

Read 25 minutes first. Separate what the text proves from what you infer. Link every claim to exact evidence.
1. State one claim about a character, conflict, or idea. Give two exact details and explain how each supports the claim.
2. Trace a two-step cause-and-effect chain: A caused B, and B led to C.
3. Explain how point of view limits what the reader knows in one moment. Cite the moment.
4. Choose one phrase that creates tone. Explain the tone and how the wording creates it.
5. Predict a likely next development using two clues, then name one uncertainty that could change the outcome.
6. Write a precise five-sentence synthesis: main development, two key details, one important author choice or character decision, and why it matters.
