Original short story: Mira saw the red weather flag rise beside the harbor office. The fishermen packed their nets, but her uncle stayed to check the mooring ropes. A second flag snapped in the wind, and a worker closed the narrow walkway. Mira wanted to hurry across with a forgotten lunch bag. Her uncle pointed to the notice: “Walkway closed until the gusts ease.” They waited in the shelter. From there Mira watched the last boat tie securely, and only after the worker lowered the flag did they return to the dock. She still did not know how strong the next gust might be.

1. Record the title of your own book and the pages or chapter read for 25 minutes. Identify one important event in one sentence.
2. State one focused claim about the passage. Copy TWO different exact clues supporting it.
3. Explain separately HOW clue 1 and clue 2 support that claim. Use two complete sentences.
4. Write the complete event chain with at least four labeled links. Do not skip the decision in the middle.
