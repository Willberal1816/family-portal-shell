Independent Reading Response

Natalie · Reading · Response Set 3
Read for 25 minutes first. Base every answer on today’s pages and distinguish what the text states from what you infer.
1. Explain how the central conflict or problem changed during today’s reading. Identify the event that caused the change.
2. Choose one author choice—description, dialogue, pacing, or point of view—that affected how you understood a scene. Give a specific example and explain its effect.
3. Make one inference about a character’s motivation or an important idea. Support it with two different details and explain the connection.
4. Choose a word or phrase with a strong or unusual meaning in context. Explain the meaning and how the surrounding language creates that meaning.
5. Write a 4-sentence precise summary of today’s reading. Include only details necessary to understand the main development.
6. Write one unanswered question the text raises. Explain what new evidence would help you answer it.
