Decimal Division

Natalie · Math

Mini Lesson

Estimate first so you know the size your quotient should have. For decimal divisors, scale both numbers by the same power of ten until the divisor is a whole number.

Practice

1. Estimate, then compute 8.4 ÷ 0.7.
2. Estimate, then compute 12.6 ÷ 0.3.
3. Compute 5.25 ÷ 1.5.
4. Four identical notebooks cost $13.80. What is the cost of one notebook? Explain how you know your answer is reasonable.
