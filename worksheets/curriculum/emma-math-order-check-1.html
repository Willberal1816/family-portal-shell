Expression Accuracy Check

Emma · Math · 50 minutes including Learn First

For every item, show one line per step and preserve the original operations.
1. 54 ÷ (15 − 9)
2. 8 + 6 × 5 − 7
3. (24 ÷ 6 + 3) × 4
4. Compare 7 + 5 × 3 with (7 + 5) × 3. Evaluate both and explain what changed.
5. A student wrote 63 ÷ (12 − 5) = 63 − 7. Identify the exact changed operation and correct the work.
6. Use 5, 8, and 4 to create two expressions with different answers because one uses parentheses. Evaluate both.
7. Write a two-step situation and its matching numerical expression. Explain why your grouping is correct.
