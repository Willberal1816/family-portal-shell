Culinary Lab Record — Heat Control

Shared cohort · Scrambled Eggs & Toast · 60 minutes

Watch the professional demo and read Learn First before heat begins. Adult nearby for stove work.
1. Setup check: hands washed; board/pan stable; handle inward; spatula, bowl, plate, and cleaner ready. Record who checked each item.
2. Plan: Write the sequence for eggs, toast, plating, and cleanup so the food is ready together.
3. Heat evidence: Record the starting heat, one sign it was correct, and any adjustment made.
4. Doneness: Describe the final curds, moisture, color, and evidence that no liquid egg remained.
5. Knife retrieval: State the full claw/flat-side rule and identify one ingredient or future task where it applies.
6. Reflection: What would you repeat next time? What one change would improve timing, texture, or cleanup? Use observable evidence.
