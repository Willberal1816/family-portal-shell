Inference + Exact Evidence

Emma · Language Arts

Mini Lesson

An inference combines what the text says with what you already know. A strong answer names an exact clue from the passage instead of inventing a detail.

Practice

1. Passage: Maya checked the clock twice, packed her bag before the bell, and waited beside the classroom door. What can you infer about Maya?
2. Copy the exact clue that best supports your inference.
3. Passage: The sidewalk was shiny, umbrellas dripped near the doorway, and Eli shook water from his jacket. What probably happened outside?
4. Which exact detail proves your answer?
5. Write one sentence explaining the difference between a clue from the text and a guess.
