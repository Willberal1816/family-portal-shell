Net Force & Changes in Motion

Shared Science

Mini Lesson

Net force is the combined effect of all forces. Balanced forces give a net force of zero; an unbalanced net force can change speed or direction.

Practice

1. A box has 12 N right and 7 N left. What is the net force and direction?
2. A cart has 9 N right and 9 N left. What is the net force?
3. Can an object with zero net force be moving? Explain.
4. A ball moving east curves north. What must have happened to its motion?
5. Draw or describe a force situation that changes direction without stopping the object.
