Sleep & Recovery

Shared Health

Mini Lesson

Sleep supports memory, learning, mood, growth, and physical recovery. A good sleep routine is about giving the body enough consistent opportunity to rest, not chasing a perfect number every night.

Practice

1. Name two things the body or brain does better with adequate sleep.
2. Why can a consistent bedtime routine help?
3. Name one screen-free activity that could help you wind down.
4. What is one sign that someone may not be getting enough rest?
5. Write one realistic change that could protect sleep without making the evening miserable.
