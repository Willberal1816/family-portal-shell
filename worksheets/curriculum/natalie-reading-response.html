Reading Response

1. Choose one decision, discovery, or turning point from today’s reading. In 3–4 sentences, explain what led to it and what changed because of it.
2. Compare two characters, ideas, or sides of a conflict from today’s pages. Give one specific detail for each side, then explain the most important difference.
3. Choose one detail that changed or deepened your understanding of the story. Copy or describe the detail, then explain exactly what it helped you understand.
4. Choose one interesting word or phrase. Copy it, explain what it means in this passage, and name the exact context clue that supports your meaning.
5. Make one prediction about what may happen next. Give two different pieces of evidence from the book and explain how each one supports your prediction.
6. Write one claim about a character, conflict, or important idea from today’s reading. Support the claim with two specific details from the text.
7. Reread your answers. Pick one that could be more precise and revise it by adding a missing detail, exact clue, or explanation.
