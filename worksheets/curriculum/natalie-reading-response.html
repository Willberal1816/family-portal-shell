Independent Reading Response

Natalie · Reading
First: read your current book for about 25 minutes without stopping to fill this out. Then spend time thinking and writing carefully enough that someone who did not read your pages can follow your answers.

Reading Response

1. In 3–4 sentences, explain one important event, decision, discovery, or change from today's reading. Include enough context that the event makes sense.
2. What can you figure out about a character, conflict, or situation that the author does not say directly? State your idea, then give one exact detail from the text that supports it.
3. Choose a second detail from today's reading. Explain why that detail matters instead of simply copying it.
4. Choose one interesting or unfamiliar word or phrase. Copy it, explain what you think it means in this passage, and name the context clue that helped you.
5. Make one prediction about what may happen next. Give at least two reasons from the book for your prediction.
6. Write one question you would ask the author or a character about today's pages. Explain why the answer would matter.
7. Pick your strongest answer above. Add one more specific detail that makes it clearer or more convincing.
