Independent Reading Response

Natalie · Reading

Mini Lesson

Read for about 25 minutes first. Then make your answers specific enough that someone who did not read the pages can understand what changed or mattered.

Practice

1. Name one specific event, decision, discovery, or change from today’s reading.
2. Make one inference and support it with an exact detail from the text.
3. What detail did the author include that helped you understand a character, place, or conflict?
4. Make one prediction or connection and explain the evidence behind it.
