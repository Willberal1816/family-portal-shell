Fresh case: Community Center Power Outage

Source A — front desk log: “2:16 p.m. — lobby computer shut off; emergency lights came on.”
Source B — utility alert: “2:18 p.m. — feeder fault affecting Pine Street area. Crews dispatched.”
Source C — visitor text: “The lights went out a little after 2:00 while we were in the gym.”
Source D — weather station: “2:00–3:00 p.m.: cloudy, light wind, no lightning detected.”
Source E — social post: “A lightning strike knocked out the power!”
1. What fact is supported by both Source A and Source C? Name both sources and write only the fact they both support.
2. What exact new detail does Source B add? Explain what that detail lets you say that A and C do not.
