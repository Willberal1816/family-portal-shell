Original short story: At the community fair, the path to the garden was dark. Finn wanted to follow a faint trail, but the volunteer pointed to three lanterns leading around a muddy patch. Finn counted each light and stayed on the marked path. At the end he found the garden gate and a sign showing where the return route began. He realized the lanterns were not decorations only: each one was a clue that helped visitors avoid the mud.

1. Write your own book title and pages or chapter read for 25 minutes. Give one event in a full sentence.
2. State the story’s first problem and copy one exact phrase that shows it.
3. Explain one important word by giving its meaning AND a nearby exact context clue.
4. Write the action the character takes; copy one phrase showing why that action makes sense.
