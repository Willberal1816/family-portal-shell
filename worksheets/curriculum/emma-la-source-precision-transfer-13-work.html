Source / data for these questions:
Source A — Museum maintenance record: On June 10 the north gallery closed after a water pipe leaked. Staff replaced a valve June 11. The gallery reopened June 12 after the floor dried. Source B — Visitor notice: During the closure, guests used the south route to reach the history room; the children's exhibit stayed open.
1. Copy two exact date-and-action facts from Source A.
2. Copy one exact visitor-access fact from Source B.
3. Explain in one sentence what Source A was written to record.
4. Explain in one sentence what Source B was written to tell visitors.
5. Which source establishes the valve replacement date? Name the exact date and explain why.
