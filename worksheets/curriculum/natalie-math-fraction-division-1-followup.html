Fraction Division 1 — Follow-Up

Natalie · Math
Three focused checks. These questions review the math that actually needs another look from Fraction Division 1. The earlier reasonableness prompt is not included because its directions were not clear enough to use as evidence of a skill gap.
1. Meaning of division. A 2-foot ribbon is cut into pieces that are each 2/3 foot long. Draw or describe the 2/3-foot groups. Then write the division equation and the number of pieces.
2. Mixed-number conversion. First convert 2 1/4 to an improper fraction. Then solve 2 1/4 ÷ 3/5. Show the reciprocal-multiplication setup and circle the simplified quotient.
3. Make the quotient mean something. Write a real-world word problem for 3 5/6 ÷ 1 2/3. Then (1) write the division equation, (2) solve and simplify it, and (3) write one sentence saying what the quotient means in your story.
