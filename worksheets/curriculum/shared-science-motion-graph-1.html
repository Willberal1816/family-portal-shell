Reading Motion Graphs

Shared Science

Mini Lesson

On a distance-versus-time graph, a rising line means distance is increasing. A steeper line means distance changes faster. A flat line means the object is not changing its distance from the start.

Practice

1. If a distance-time graph is flat for 5 minutes, what is the object doing relative to the start?
2. Which shows greater speed: a gently rising line or a steeply rising line? Explain.
3. Sketch a graph for: move quickly for 2 minutes, stop for 1 minute, then move slowly for 3 minutes.
4. What information must be on the graph axes?
5. Explain one difference between a graph of motion and a map of position.
