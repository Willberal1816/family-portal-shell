Rule Detective Transfer Check

Natalie · Math · 25 minutes independent evidence

For every item: write k, write b, write the whole rule, and check one value. For tables, show two change calculations. Answer every requested part.
1. A skating rink charges $6 to enter and $4 per hour for skate rental. Write the rule, find the cost at 0 hours and 3 hours, and state whether it is proportional.
2. Table: (0,5), (2,11), (6,23). Show two change calculations, find k and b, write the rule, and check x=6.
3. Error repair: A parking lot charges $12 to enter and $3 per hour. A student wrote y=3x+3. Name the incorrect part, write the correct rule, and check 4 hours.
4. Complete both tables for x=0, 2, and 5: A: y=7x; B: y=7x+4. Explain exactly which rule is proportional and why.
5. A table has (0,9), (3,21), and (8,41). Show two change calculations, write the whole rule, and verify all three points.
6. Create a new real-world situation with a rate of 5 and a nonzero start of your choice. Write the specific rule, calculate y at x=0 and x=4, and explain what both numbers mean in your situation.
