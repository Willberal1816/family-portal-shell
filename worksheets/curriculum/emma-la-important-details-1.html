Find the Important Details

Emma · Language Arts · Independent Practice
Directions: Read the passage, then decide what a reader truly needs in a summary. Write complete sentences where asked.
Passage: On Saturday morning, Elena and her grandfather went to repair a loose board on the small footbridge behind his house. First they checked which boards were solid and which one moved under pressure. Her grandfather showed her how to remove the old screws safely. Elena held the replacement board in place while he started the new screws, then she tightened them with a hand screwdriver. A robin landed on the fence while they worked. When they finished, they walked across the bridge twice to make sure the board stayed firm.
1. Write the main event in one sentence.
2. From the passage, list three details that are important to understanding the repair and one detail that can be left out of a summary.
