Independent Reading Response

Emma · Reading · Response Set 7

Read 25 minutes first. Check every part: exact detail count, exact context clue, and requested sentence count.
1. Name one character decision. Give one event that caused it and one result that followed.
2. Copy one exact detail that reveals a feeling or goal. Explain what the detail reveals and why.
3. Explain one thing the viewpoint character knows and one thing that character cannot know yet.
4. Copy one exact word or phrase, copy the nearby context clue, and explain the meaning.
5. Compare two moments. Give one similarity, one difference, and one detail supporting each.
6. Write exactly five sentences: main development, three supporting events/details, and why the section matters.
