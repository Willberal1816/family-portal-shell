Independent Reading Response

Natalie · Reading · Response Set 6
Read for 25 minutes first.
Evidence check: Claim stated? Two exact clues? Reasoning connects each clue? Any uncertainty clearly separated from what the text proves?
1. Make one claim about a character, conflict, or central idea. Give two exact clues and explain how each clue supports the claim.
2. Identify one cause-and-effect chain in today’s reading. Explain the cause, the immediate effect, and one later consequence.
3. Choose one moment where point of view or narration affected what the reader knew. Explain what information was available and what remained uncertain.
4. Choose a sentence, phrase, or detail that changes the tone. Explain the tone and cite the exact wording that creates it.
5. Predict what is likely next. Use two clues, then name one uncertainty that could make the prediction wrong.
6. Write a 5-sentence synthesis: main development, two key supporting details, one important author choice or character decision, and a final sentence explaining why this section matters to the larger story or topic.
