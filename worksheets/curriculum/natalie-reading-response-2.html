Independent Reading Response

Natalie · Reading · Response Set 2
Read for 25 minutes first. Use precise evidence from today’s pages. When a prompt asks for two details, include two different details.
1. Identify one turning point. Explain what caused it and one important effect it had on the character, conflict, or situation.
2. Compare two characters’ or sides’ perspectives on the same event. Give one text detail supporting each perspective.
3. Choose one conclusion you can make from today’s reading. Give the strongest evidence for it and explain why that evidence is stronger than a second possible clue.
4. Copy one interesting word or phrase. Explain its meaning in context and identify the exact clue that supports your interpretation.
5. Predict what may happen next. Give two different pieces of evidence and explain how each supports the prediction.
6. Write one claim about a character, conflict, or important idea. Support it with two specific details and one sentence explaining how the evidence connects to your claim.
