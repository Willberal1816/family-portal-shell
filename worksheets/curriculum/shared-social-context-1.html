Source Context & Reliability

Shared Social Studies

Mini Lesson

Before using a source, ask who made it, when, why, and what access the creator had to the event. Reliability depends on the question you are asking.

Practice

1. A newspaper article was written the morning after an event. What is one strength and one limitation?
2. A memoir was written 40 years later. What might it add, and what might memory change?
3. A government report lists official totals. What kind of question could it answer well?
4. Why can a biased source still be useful evidence?
5. Write two context questions you should ask before trusting a source claim.
