Tax, Tip & Discounts

Natalie · Math · Independent Work
Directions: Mark the original amount, percent rate, percent amount, and final amount. Estimate before calculating so you can catch unreasonable answers.
1. A meal costs $42.50 before tip. Find a 20% tip and the final total. Estimate first.
2. A $68 jacket is 25% off. Find the discount and sale price. Explain why multiplying by 0.25 gives the discount, not the final price.
3. A $34 purchase has 5.5% sales tax. Find the tax and final total to the nearest cent.
4. A store advertises 30% off a $90 item. Another gives a $24 coupon. Which deal saves more, and by how much?
5. A restaurant bill with an 18% tip totals $59.00. Find the original bill. Show an equation or proportional relationship and check your result.
6. Create a realistic two-step shopping problem involving either discount + tax or price + tip. Solve it and explain the order of your calculations.
