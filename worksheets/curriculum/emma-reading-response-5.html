Independent Reading Response

Emma · Reading · Response Set 5
Read for 25 minutes first. Use specific evidence from today’s reading and check that your answers are complete.
1. What was the most important event in today’s reading? Explain why it mattered to what happened next.
2. Evaluate one decision a character made. Was it a strong choice based on what the character knew? Give one detail supporting your answer.
3. What lesson or idea might the story be showing so far? Give two story details that support your thinking.
4. Choose one small detail that turned out to be important. Explain why it mattered.
5. Copy one interesting word or phrase, explain its meaning here, and identify the exact clue that helped you.
6. Write a 4-sentence summary of today’s reading using only the most important events and information.
