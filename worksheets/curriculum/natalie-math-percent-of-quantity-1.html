Percent of a Quantity

Natalie · Math · Independent Practice
Directions: For each problem, write a quick estimate or reasonable range before the exact calculation. Show the method you chose. Explanations should be one complete sentence unless the problem asks for more.
1. Estimate, then find 20% of 85. Explain why your answer is reasonable.
2. A backpack costs $64 and is discounted 25%. Find the discount and the sale price. Show both amounts.
3. A recipe normally makes 40 servings. A smaller event needs 65% of that amount. How many servings is 65% of 40? Show a method that does not begin with a calculator-style decimal multiplication.
4. Which is greater: 35% of 120 or 45% of 90? Calculate both and write one sentence comparing them.
5. A game score increased from 80 points to 120% of the original score. What is the new score? Explain why the result is greater than 80.
6. Error analysis: A student says 15% of 240 is 360 because 240 × 1.5 = 360. Identify the mistake and give the correct answer.
7. A store takes 30% off a $48 item, then adds $3 shipping. What is the final cost? Show the order of your steps and label the discount, sale price, and final cost.
8. Create a practical percent problem whose exact answer is between 20 and 40. Solve it and explain how you know your answer fits that range.
