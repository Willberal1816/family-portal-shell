Fraction Division — Independent Work

Complete this on your own after today’s teaching and guided practice. These questions are the independent evidence for today’s lesson.

1. Meaning of division

A 2-foot ribbon is cut into pieces that are each 2/3 foot long. Draw or describe the 2/3-foot groups. Then write the division equation and the number of pieces.

2. Mixed-number conversion

First convert 2 1/4 to an improper fraction. Then solve 2 1/4 ÷ 3/5. Show the reciprocal-multiplication setup and circle the simplified quotient.

3. Make the quotient mean something

Write a real-world word problem for 3 5/6 ÷ 1 2/3. Then: (1) write the division equation, (2) solve and simplify it, and (3) write one sentence saying what the quotient means in your story.
