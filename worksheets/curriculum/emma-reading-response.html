Independent Reading Response

Emma · Reading
The 25-minute reading period is part of the class, not a suggestion. Read your current book for a full 25 minutes before beginning the written response. Do not shorten the reading period because you read quickly. After 25 minutes, answer with specific details from the pages you actually read. The written response should normally take another 15–20 minutes when completed carefully.

Reading Response

1. In 2–3 sentences, describe one important event, discovery, or change from today's reading.
2. What do the clues tell you about a character? Write your idea and copy or describe one exact clue that supports it.
3. Write a second clue that supports the same idea—or a clue that makes you rethink it. Then write one sentence saying how that clue supports or changes your idea.
4. Choose one interesting or unfamiliar word. What do you think it means here, and what nearby words helped you decide?
5. Make one prediction about what could happen next. Give two details that led you to that prediction.
6. Name the most important detail in today's pages. Then write one sentence explaining what you would understand differently if that detail were removed.
