Reading Response

1. Choose one moment that changed what a character knew, wanted, or decided to do. In 2–3 sentences, explain what happened and what changed.
2. Pick one choice a character made. What does that choice show about the character? Give one exact story detail that supports your idea.
3. Find one place where the setting mattered to what happened. Describe the place, then explain in one sentence why that setting mattered.
4. Choose one interesting word or phrase from today’s pages. Copy it, tell what you think it means here, and name the nearby clue that helped you decide.
5. Make one prediction about what may happen next. Give two different details from today’s reading that support your prediction.
6. Which answer above is your strongest? Write one more specific story detail that makes that answer clearer or stronger.
