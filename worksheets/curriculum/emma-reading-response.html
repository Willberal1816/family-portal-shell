Independent Reading Response

Emma · Reading

Mini Lesson

Read for about 25 minutes first. Then answer briefly but specifically. Use an exact clue from the pages you read whenever the question asks for evidence.

Practice

1. Name one important event, discovery, or change from today’s reading.
2. What can you infer about a character? Copy one exact clue that supports your inference.
3. Choose one interesting or unfamiliar word. What do you think it means in this passage?
4. Make one prediction and explain what detail led you to it.
