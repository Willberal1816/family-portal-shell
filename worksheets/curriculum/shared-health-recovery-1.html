Exercise, Recovery & Fair Comparisons

Shared Health

Mini Lesson

Heart rate usually rises during exercise because working muscles need more oxygen-rich blood. Recovery time tells us how quickly the body moves back toward resting conditions. Fair comparisons keep important conditions the same.

Practice

1. Why does heart rate usually rise during exercise?
2. Name two conditions you would keep the same if comparing two movement trials.
3. Why should pulse measurements be taken at the same time after each trial?
4. What would make a comparison unfair?
5. Write one safe question you could investigate about movement and recovery.
