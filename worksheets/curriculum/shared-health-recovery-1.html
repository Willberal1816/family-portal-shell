Heart Rate Recovery

Emma + Natalie · Health
Directions: Use safe, comfortable movement and have a parent nearby if needed. Stop if you feel dizzy, painful, or unwell. Predict means state what you expect before measuring. Explain means give one reason based on the lesson. Describe the pattern means use at least three of your actual numbers, not only general words.

Plan

1. List four things you will keep the same during recovery measurements.
2. Name one safe 60-second movement. Then write one sentence explaining what you will keep the same so you can repeat it consistently.
3. Before measuring, write what you expect your pulse to do (1) immediately after movement, (2) after 1 minute, and (3) after 2 minutes. Then write one sentence explaining why you expect that pattern.

Collect data

4. Measure resting pulse. Record count interval and calculated beats per minute.
5. After 60 seconds of movement, record pulse as soon as practical.
6. Record pulse 1 minute after stopping.
7. Record pulse 2 minutes after stopping.

Interpret

8. Describe the pattern using your actual numbers. Do not just say “it went down.”
9. Calculate the change from immediately after movement to 2 minutes after.
10. Name one source of measurement error.

Extend

11. What must stay the same to compare a repeat trial tomorrow fairly?
12. Natalie: Write one sentence naming the two things that changed (exercise type and duration), then one sentence explaining why changing both makes it hard to know which one caused a different result.
13. Emma: Draw a line graph of the four measurements and label both axes.
