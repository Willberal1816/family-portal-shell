Independent Reading Response

Emma · Reading · Response Set 4
Read for 25 minutes first. Use exact evidence and complete every requested part.
1. Choose one character trait that mattered today. Give one action or line of dialogue that shows the trait and explain why it fits.
2. Identify one cause-and-effect chain with at least two steps: A caused B, and B led to C.
3. Find one sentence or description that helped you picture the scene. Explain which words made the picture clearer.
4. Compare two characters, places, or ideas from today’s pages. Give one similarity and one difference with a supporting detail.
5. Make a prediction and support it with two clues. Then name one fact that could make your prediction turn out wrong.
6. Reread your answers. Choose one and revise it to be more specific, clearer, or better supported.
