Compare Two Texts

Natalie · Language Arts · Independent Work
Directions: Compare what each author emphasizes, which evidence each chooses, and how those choices shape the reader’s understanding. Do not treat “different” as meaning one author must be dishonest.
Text A: The new community garden opened after volunteers spent six weekends clearing weeds, building beds, and installing a rain barrel. Organizers say the garden gives neighbors a place to grow food and learn from one another. Twenty families signed up for plots during the first week.
Text B: The community garden is open, but several questions remain. The site has only twelve parking spaces, and nearby residents are concerned about weekend traffic. The city plans to review parking and water use after the garden’s first month.
1. What does Text A emphasize? Name two details that show its focus.
2. What does Text B emphasize? Name two details.
3. Identify one fact or topic both texts share and one important difference in what they choose to highlight.
4. Which text would be more useful for answering “How was the garden created?” Which would be more useful for “What problems might the garden create?” Explain.
5. Write one question you would ask before deciding whether either account gives a complete picture. Explain what evidence would answer it.
6. Write a 4–5 sentence synthesis that combines useful information from both texts without taking either author’s tone as fact.
