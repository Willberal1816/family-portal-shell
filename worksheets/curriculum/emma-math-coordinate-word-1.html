Coordinate Plane Word Problems

Emma · Math
Directions: Plot or sketch on the printed coordinate planes whenever the problem describes a location or route. Label points and route arrows before calculating.

Map locations

1. Slide (-4,3), swings (2,3): how far apart?
2. Fountain (5,-2), gate (5,4): find distance and name whether the segment is horizontal or vertical.
3. A tree is 3 units west and 5 units north of the origin. Plot the tree, then give its ordered pair and quadrant.

Routes

4. Robot: (-2,1) → (4,1) → (4,-3). Plot and draw the route, then find each segment and the total distance.
5. Game route: (3,-5) → (3,2) → (-1,2) → (-1,6). Plot the route and find each segment and total.
6. Create a three-segment horizontal/vertical route totaling exactly 15 units. Plot and label all four stops, then show the segment lengths.

Scale and compare

7. 1 grid unit = 2 meters. Path (-3,0) to (5,0): what is the real distance?
8. Benches (-6,4), (1,4); fountain (-6,-1). Which location is closer to the first bench? Show both distances.
9. Explain what additional coordinate information you need, besides x=-8, to find the distance to a particular point on the y-axis.

Design

10. Design a coordinate map with a home base and four locations in at least three quadrants. Plot and label them, then write and solve two route questions using your map.
11. Explain the difference between a coordinate sign (which side of the origin) and distance (how far apart).
