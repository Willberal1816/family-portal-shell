Coordinate Plane Word Problems

Emma · Math
Directions: Sketch each situation before solving when the route has more than one segment. When a question says explain, give the answer and one sentence naming the coordinate or distance fact that supports it.

Map locations

1. Slide (-4,3), swings (2,3): how far apart?
2. Fountain (5,-2), gate (5,4): find distance and name horizontal/vertical.
3. A tree is 3 west and 5 north of origin. Give ordered pair and quadrant.

Routes

4. Robot: (-2,1) → (4,1) → (4,-3). Draw route and find total.
5. Game route: (3,-5) → (3,2) → (-1,2) → (-1,6). Find each segment and total.
6. Create a three-segment route totaling exactly 15 units.

Scale and compare

7. 1 grid unit = 2 meters. Path (-3,0) to (5,0): real distance?
8. Benches (-6,4), (1,4); fountain (-6,-1). Which is closer to first bench? Show both distances.
9. Write one sentence explaining what additional coordinate information you need, besides x=-8, to find the distance to a particular point on the y-axis.

Design

10. Design a coordinate map with home base + four locations in at least three quadrants. Write and solve two route questions.
11. Write one sentence explaining the difference between a coordinate sign (which side of the origin) and distance (how far apart).
