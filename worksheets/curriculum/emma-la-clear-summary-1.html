Write a Clear Summary

Emma · Language Arts · Independent Practice
Directions: Read the passage. Plan the summary before writing. Your final summary should be 4–5 complete sentences, stay faithful to the passage, and include only details needed to understand the main event and result.
Passage: During a week of heavy rain, a shallow drainage ditch beside a neighborhood trail filled with leaves and small branches. Water began spreading across part of the path. A volunteer trail group inspected the ditch and found that debris was blocking the narrow opening under a footpath. Wearing gloves, they removed the leaves and branches and placed the debris in yard-waste bags. Then they watched the water level for twenty minutes. Once the opening was clear, water began moving through the ditch again and the flooded section of trail slowly drained. The group later posted a sign reminding walkers not to dump yard waste near the drainage area.
1. Write the main event or main idea in one complete sentence.
2. Choose the three most important supporting details and write them in the order they belong in a summary.
