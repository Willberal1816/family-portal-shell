Independent Reading Response

Emma · Reading · Response Set 6
Read for 25 minutes first.
Before you finish, check: Did I answer every part? Did I give the exact number of details requested? Did I copy an exact context clue where asked? Did I follow the sentence-count direction?
1. Name one event that changed what a character could do next. Explain the cause and the result.
2. Choose one character. Give two details that show what the character wanted or worried about, then explain what those details reveal.
3. Compare the beginning and end of today’s reading. What changed, and what stayed the same?
4. Copy one exact word or phrase you figured out from context. Copy the exact context clue that helped, then explain the meaning.
5. Make a prediction about the next section. Give two story clues that support it.
6. Write exactly 5 sentences: sentence 1 names the main development, sentences 2–4 give the three most important supporting events/details, and sentence 5 explains why the section matters.
