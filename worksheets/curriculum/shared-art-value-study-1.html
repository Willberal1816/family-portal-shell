Shading: Light to Dark

Shared · Art
You only need paper and a pencil. The goal is to control how light or dark your marks become.
1. Draw five boxes in a row. Shade them from very light to very dark.
2. Draw a simple ball or fruit. Pick where the light is coming from and shade the opposite side darker.
3. Circle the part that should be brightest. Put an X on the darkest part.
4. Write one thing you changed to make the drawing look more three-dimensional.
