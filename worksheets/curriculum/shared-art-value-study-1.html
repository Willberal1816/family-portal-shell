Shading: Light to Dark

Emma + Natalie · Art
Directions: Use pencil and plain paper. Work from observation when possible. This is a drawing lab, not a writing worksheet.

Control

1. Draw a five-box value scale from paper white to your darkest controlled value.
2. Make a second 5-step scale using hatching or cross-hatching.

Form study

3. Draw a large circle; show light from upper left; shade into a sphere with highlight, middle values, core shadow, cast shadow.
4. Label highlight, light side, shadow side, and cast shadow.
5. Compare with a real round object under a lamp/window. Name one match and one revision.

Observe an object

6. Choose a mug, ball, box, or fruit. Make a 10-minute value sketch using at least four values.
7. Circle darkest dark and lightest light. Do they match your chosen light direction? Explain.

Extend

8. Emma: Redraw one section with a different shading technique. Which gives better control?
9. Natalie: Add subtle reflected light on the shadow side and explain why it stays darker than the highlight.
10. What changed most between your value scale and final object drawing? What would you practice next?
