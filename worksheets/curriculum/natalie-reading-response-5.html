Independent Reading Response

Natalie · Reading · Response Set 5
Read for 25 minutes first. Support interpretations with exact evidence and separate what the text proves from what you infer.
1. State one important claim about today’s reading and support it with two pieces of evidence. Explain the reasoning connecting the evidence to the claim.
2. Analyze one author choice—dialogue, description, pacing, structure, or point of view. Explain how it shaped the reader’s understanding or reaction.
3. Compare two characters, ideas, or moments. Explain one similarity, one difference, and why that difference matters.
4. Choose one word or phrase whose meaning or tone matters. Explain its meaning in context and the evidence that supports your interpretation.
5. Predict what is most likely to happen next. Use two clues and explain one uncertainty that prevents the prediction from being certain.
6. Write a precise 4–5 sentence summary of today’s reading. Include the main development and omit details that do not change the central understanding.
