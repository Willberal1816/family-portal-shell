Fictional classroom case: Riverton, 1912. This is the complete source packet for the questions. Do not assume details that the records do not show.
DateEvent
May 3Heavy rain recorded.
May 4Inspector reports split north bridge support; recommends no wagons.
May 5Town posts bridge closure notice.
May 8Temporary ferry service begins.
May 11Shopkeeper reports a delayed flour delivery.
May 20Bridge repair begins.
A · Inspector, May 4: “The north support has a visible split. Do not allow wagons across until it is inspected and repaired.”
B · Town notice, May 5: “Bridge closed to all wagons while the north support is inspected. Use the temporary riverside route.”
C · Shopkeeper letter, May 11: “Our flour arrived two days later than usual. I used the ferry but did not see the bridge support myself.”
D · Museum caption, 1940: “A storm destroyed the bridge and stopped all trade.” No supporting records are named.
1. Put three linked events in date order. State exactly what happened on each date.
2. Which source directly observes the support? Which reports a merchant's experience? Explain why those roles matter.
3. Use two sources to explain why the town closed the bridge. Quote or copy one exact detail from each.
