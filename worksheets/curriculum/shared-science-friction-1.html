Friction Investigation

Shared Science

Mini Lesson

Friction opposes relative motion between surfaces. The amount of friction can change with the surfaces and how strongly they are pressed together.

Practice

1. Predict which will slide farther: the same object on smooth tile or on a rough towel. Explain.
2. Name one variable you would keep the same for a fair test.
3. Name one variable you would deliberately change.
4. Record or imagine two trials and state what result would support your prediction.
5. Explain how friction can be useful, not just something that slows motion.
