Friction Test

Emma + Natalie · Science
Directions: Use a safe parent-approved setup. Use the same object and setup for every surface. Do at least three trials per condition.

Plan

1. Question: How does surface material affect slide distance? Identify independent variable, measured outcome, and four controls.
2. Choose 3 safe surfaces and predict which will resist motion most. Explain.
3. Describe exactly how you will release/move the object the same way each time.

Collect

4. Surface 1: record three distances with units and average.
5. Surface 2: record three distances with units and average.
6. Surface 3: record three distances with units and average.

Analyze

7. Rank surfaces from shortest average travel to longest. What does the pattern suggest?
8. Identify an unusual trial, if any, and a possible experimental reason.
9. Write a claim and support it with at least two actual measurements.

Extend

10. Emma: Draw a bar graph of average travel distance for the three surfaces. Label axes/units.
11. Natalie: Explain why shorter travel is indirect evidence of greater motion-resisting friction but not a direct newton measurement.
12. Connect friction to net force while the object slides and slows.
