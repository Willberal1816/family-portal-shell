Heat Control: Scrambled Eggs & Toast — Lab Record

Before heat: Watch the assigned professional Food Network egg demonstration and read the Learn First page. Complete questions 1–2 before turning on the stove.
1. Safety retrieval. Write two board/knife safety rules and two stovetop/raw-egg safety rules you will use today.
2. Plan. Starting heat setting:
How will you know the eggs are fully cooked?
3. During cooking. What change did you notice first after the eggs hit the pan? What did you do with the spatula?
4. Heat evidence. Decide: cooked too fast / controlled pace / cooked too slowly. Give two observations that support your judgment.
5. Product check. Describe the final egg texture and browning. What one heat-control change, if any, would improve the next batch?
6. Toast timing. Explain when you started the toast and why that timing helped both foods reach the plate warm.
7. Cleanup. List the cleanup/reset steps you completed, including raw-egg contact surfaces.
8. Reflection. What did today’s cooking show about the relationship between heat level, cooking speed, and texture?
