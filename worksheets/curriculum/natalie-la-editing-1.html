Editing Compound & Complex Sentences

Natalie · Language Arts

Mini Lesson

Editing longer sentences means checking clause boundaries. Compound sentences need a correct join; introductory dependent clauses usually need a comma before the main clause.

Practice

1. Edit: Although the road was icy we left early.
2. Edit: I wanted to finish the book, however I needed to sleep.
3. Edit: We packed the cooler and Dad loaded the car.
4. Edit: When the timer rang I checked the bread but it needed five more minutes.
5. Choose one sentence and explain the punctuation rule you used.
