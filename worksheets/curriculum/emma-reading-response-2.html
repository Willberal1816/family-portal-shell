Independent Reading Response

Emma · Reading · Response Set 2
Read for 25 minutes first. Use exact details from today’s pages. Answer every part before turning this in.
1. Put one important event from today’s reading in sequence: what happened just before it, what happened, and what happened because of it. Write 3 sentences.
2. What did one character want in today’s pages? Give one exact story detail that shows that goal.
3. Name one clear cause-and-effect relationship from today’s reading. Explain how the cause led to the effect.
4. Copy one unfamiliar or interesting word/phrase. Explain its meaning here and name the nearby clue that helped you.
5. Compare how a character felt or what a character knew at the beginning and end of today’s reading. Give one detail for each.
6. Predict what may happen next and support the prediction with two different details from today’s pages.
