Different Points of View

Emma + Natalie · Social Studies
Directions: Use the fictional Harbor Festival source set. Treat each source as evidence with strengths and limits.

Source set

1. A — Vendor: rain after noon; signs ruined; organizers told vendors to pack; lost sales.
B — Parks report: lightning observed at 12:42; staff cleared field and closed festival for safety.
C — Visitor: left before big storm because lines were long; saw people running toward parking lot from car.
D — Next-day promotion: “Despite yesterday’s weather interruption, Harbor Weekend continues today with a full schedule!”

Compare

2. What do A and B agree on, and what concerns differ?
3. Why is C weak for establishing exact official closing time?
4. What is D’s likely purpose/audience and how may that shape wording?

Build claims

5. Claim: “Safety concerns, not only heavy rain, led organizers to close the field.” Best source and why?
6. Write one claim supported by at least two independent sources.
7. Name one question none of the sources can answer confidently and a new source that could help.

Perspective and synthesis

8. Emma: Explain how vendor and visitor could both be truthful while remembering differently.
9. Natalie: Compare B and D by purpose, audience, timing, and emphasis; choose best for safety reason.
10. Why is “Source A cared about money so ignore it” too simple?
11. Write a 6–8 sentence account using at least three sources, separating well-supported facts from uncertainty.
12. Why is perspective useful information, not merely a problem?
