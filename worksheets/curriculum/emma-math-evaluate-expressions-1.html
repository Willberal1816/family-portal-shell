Evaluate Multi-Step Expressions

Emma · Math · Independent Work
Directions: For every problem, circle or write the operation you will do first. Show one line for each step. Do not skip directly to the answer.
1. 5 × (12 − 7)
2. 8 + 6 × 4
3. (27 + 9) ÷ 6
4. 42 − 18 ÷ 3
5. 36 ÷ 6 × 4
6. Compare: 3 × (7 + 5) and 3 × 7 + 5. Evaluate both and explain why the answers differ.
7. Error analysis: Mia says 10 + 2 × 6 = 72 because she added first. Correct her work and explain the rule she missed.
8. A game awards 6 points for each of 5 challenges, then removes 8 penalty points. Write and evaluate the expression.
9. Create two expressions using the numbers 4, 6, and 10 that give different answers because one uses parentheses. Evaluate both.
