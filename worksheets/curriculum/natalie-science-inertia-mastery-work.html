Force reminder: an object does not need a forward net force just to keep moving at a steady speed. A net force changes motion.
1. The hockey puck below is already sliding right at a steady speed on nearly frictionless ice. Circle YES / NO: does it need a forward net force to keep moving? Then explain why.
2. A car is moving right with a loose backpack on the seat. The car stops suddenly. Draw an arrow on the diagram showing what the backpack does for the first moment after the stop. Then explain using the word inertia.
3. The passenger below is moving with the car. The car stops quickly and the seat belt tightens. Draw the belt’s force arrow on the passenger. Then write one sentence that names inertia and the force from the belt.
4. Complete the force table. Draw and name the important force arrows, then decide whether the forces are balanced or unbalanced.
