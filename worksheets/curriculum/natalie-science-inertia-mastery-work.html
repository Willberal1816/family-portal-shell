Why Things Keep Moving — Independent Work

1. A hockey puck is sliding straight at a steady speed on nearly frictionless ice. Does it need a forward net force to keep moving? Circle YES / NO, then explain in one sentence.
2. A car is moving forward. A loose backpack is moving forward with the car. The car stops suddenly. Why does the backpack keep moving forward for a moment? Use the word inertia.
3. A passenger is moving forward with a car. The car stops quickly. What does the seat belt do to the passenger’s motion? Write one sentence that names inertia and the force from the belt.
4. Name the important force(s) and write BALANCED or UNBALANCED for each situation.

b) A rolling marble slows on carpet.
