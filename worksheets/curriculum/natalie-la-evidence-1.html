Inference with Text Evidence

Natalie · Language Arts

Mini Lesson

An inference is strongest when you can point to a specific phrase or action in the text that supports it.

Practice

1. Passage: Lena reread the directions, erased two answers, and checked the example one more time before turning in the page. What can you infer about Lena?
2. Copy the exact clue that best supports your inference.
3. Passage: The porch light was still on at sunrise, the newspaper was wet, and no footprints crossed the fresh snow. What can you infer?
4. Name the detail that most strongly supports your answer.
5. Explain why evidence makes an inference stronger than a guess.
