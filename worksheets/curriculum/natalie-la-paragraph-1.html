Paragraph Development

Natalie · Language Arts

Mini Lesson

A complete paragraph develops one main idea with support and then gives the reader a sense of closure. One strong supporting sentence is better than several vague ones.

Practice

1. Write a topic sentence explaining one benefit of learning at home.
2. Add one specific example or supporting reason.
3. Add a second sentence that explains why that example matters.
4. Write a concluding thought that does not simply repeat the topic sentence.
5. Circle the sentence that does the most work supporting your main idea.
