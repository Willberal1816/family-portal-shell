Practice cards, not personal medical advice: (A) A student sits reading in a cool room. (B) A student hikes on a warm day and sweats. (C) A student finishes active play and sees a refill station. (D) A student feels unusually dizzy or unwell. Decision pattern: Notice thirst/activity/heat → choose a reasonable drink or water break → recheck; tell a parent for unusual or ongoing symptoms.

1. Explain two body or activity cues that could change the timing of an ordinary water break.
2. For cards A and B, write a different sensible action AND recheck step for each.
3. Build a three-column plan for before/during/after a safe outdoor activity. Include access to water.
4. Explain why no single fixed bottle count suits every age, body, weather condition and activity.
