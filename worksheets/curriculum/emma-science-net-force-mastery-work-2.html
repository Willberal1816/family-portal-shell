Why Things Keep Moving — Net Force Mastery

For each situation, state: (1) whether velocity changes, (2) whether net force is zero or not zero, and (3) the force(s) causing or balancing the motion.

1. A scooter travels north at a steady 4 m/s in a straight line.
2. A picture frame hangs still. Gravity pulls down while the hook/cord supports it upward. Explain the net force.
3. A rolling ball slows across carpet.
4. Two equal 20 N forces push opposite directions on a cart that is already moving steadily left.
5. A runner keeps the same speed but turns around a corner. Did velocity change? What does that tell you about net force?
6. A backpack slides forward when a bus stops. Explain inertia and name the force that eventually changes the backpack's motion.
7. Correct this statement: “If something is moving, there must be an unbalanced force pushing it forward.”
