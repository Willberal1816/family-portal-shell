Garden Packs: What Repeats?

Emma · Math · Thursday, October 22, 2026 · 50 minutes

Goal: Model identical groups accurately and keep one-time extras outside parentheses.

One class: Read this Learn First lesson, work through both examples, complete all six items on TWO printable pages, scan both pages, and Finish Class. Learn First and Printable are not separate assignments.

Learn First

A group is a box of items that repeats. The expression number of boxes × (items in one box) + one-time extras matches the drawing. Put the exact number of each item inside every box before you add. A one-time extra is not multiplied.

Two worked models

Model 1. Two art kits each contain 1 brush and 3 paints. There are 2 loose clips. Write 2 × (1 + 3) + 2 = 10 items. The two clips stay OUTSIDE both kits.
Model 2. Three travel bags each hold 2 maps and 1 flashlight, plus 4 spare batteries carried once: 3 × (2 + 1) + 4 = 13. Check by counting 9 inside the bags plus 4 outside.
Check for mistakes: Common mistake: adding the extra items inside each bag would count them several times. Say which objects repeat before writing an expression.
