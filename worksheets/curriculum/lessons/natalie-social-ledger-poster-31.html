Ledger, Poster, and What Follows

Natalie · Social Studies · Thursday, October 15, 2026 · Learn First

Goal: classify economic records and promotion, distinguish sequence from cause, and bound a claim to the available evidence. Read this teaching page before opening the separate Independent Work printable.

Worked model

A mill ledger records transactions; a festival poster promotes attendance. If the ledger shows flour purchases before the festival, that chronology does not prove the festival caused the purchases. A cause claim needs a source connecting the order to the event.

Reliable method

  1. Identify each source’s role before using it.
  2. Use exact dates and quantities.
  3. Separate “happened before” from “caused.”
  4. Limit conclusions to the people, place, and period represented.

Before independent work

Explain the goal in your own words. On the printable, answer every numbered part, show the requested evidence or calculation, scan both pages, and then finish class.
