Goal: separate dates and times from quantities, classify sources, and write a claim with causation and place limits. Plan about 50 minutes. Read this teaching page before the Independent Work section.

Learn First

A schedule records planned times. A tally records amounts. A larger number is not a later date, and two nearby facts do not automatically show cause.

Worked model

Schedule: train at 9:15. Tally: 24 crates. One is time; one is quantity. Neither alone proves why freight changed.

Today's fresh source or situation

Station schedule: Train 6 arrived in Mill Creek at 10:20 a.m. on May 4. Freight tally: Mill Creek received 18 flour sacks on May 3 and 31 flour sacks on May 5. Handbill: Market Day in Mill Creek, May 5.
