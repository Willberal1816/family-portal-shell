Check Multi-Step Expressions

Goal: Evaluate accurately, preserve every operation, and prove that parentheses change grouping.

Learn First

Use this loop: group → multiply/divide left to right → add/subtract left to right → compare with the original expression. Circle the operation outside a group before simplifying it.

Model 1: 42 ÷ (18 ÷ 3). First 18 ÷ 3 = 6. The outside sign is still division, so 42 ÷ 6 = 7.
Model 2: (6 + 10) × 4 = 16 × 4 = 64, while 6 + 10 × 4 = 6 + 40 = 46. The parentheses changed which operation happened first.
Model 3: 30 − 12 ÷ 3 = 30 − 4 = 26. Do not work left to right before division.
Error check: If a rewritten line changes ÷ into −, × into +, or drops a number, stop and copy the original structure again.

Before independent work

  1. Underline each group.
  2. Circle the operation that remains outside the group.
  3. Estimate the answer range.
  4. Evaluate and compare to the estimate.
