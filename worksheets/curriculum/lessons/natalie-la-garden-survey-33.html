Learn First

Every survey has a population that was invited and a smaller group that actually responded. If 14 of 20 respondents answered yes, the supported claim is about those 20 respondents, not every person invited. A flyer describes what is offered, not whether the offer caused a result.

Use exact numbers, dates, and source purposes. Say what a source cannot prove.

Worked example

Forty neighbors were invited; 12 answered; 9 preferred Saturday. Safe claim: 9 of the 12 respondents preferred Saturday. Unsupported claim: Three quarters of all 40 neighbors prefer Saturday.

Today's information

Fictional community garden source packet:

Source A — volunteer list: 80 people are registered for garden updates.

Source B — short survey: 20 of those 80 returned the survey. Of those 20 respondents, 14 said evening watering hours would help them.

Source C — notice: “Evening watering now available on Tuesdays.”

Source D — sign-in record: Three evening watering sessions occurred in the first week. The record does not say why people attended.
