Paper Whirlybird: Build, Drop, Test Weight

Emma · Wednesday, October 28, 2026 · 60 minutes

What you will make

The finished object is a small paper whirlybird with two blades folded in opposite directions, a narrow hanging stem, and an optional paper clip. When dropped, the blades spin and air slows the fall. Adding clips changes weight. Change ONLY the clip count between trials so you can compare the same whirlybird. An observation is what you actually saw, not what you expected.

Materials and safety check

2 sheets of printer paper, child-safe scissors, pencil, ruler, 3 identical paper clips, two colored pencils, stopwatch or clock optional. Use a safe open floor area and a consistent adult-approved hand-release height. Adult handles scissors or high placements if needed; NEVER stand on a chair or railing.

Watch and do — Science Buddies demonstration

Video from Science Buddies. If the player fails to load, ask an adult to open the official demonstration video or illustrated source instructions. Do not substitute an unrelated video.

Learn First and worked model

Example record (illustrative, not your result): 0 clips: spin, 3.2 seconds; 1 clip: spin, 2.8 seconds; 2 clips: less spin, 2.2 seconds. The observations suggest the heavier model fell faster in these trials, but your own results may differ.

Six steps to follow

  1. Watch the Science Buddies demonstration. Pause when it shows the completed whirlybird; compare yours to the drawing.
  2. Draw two joined blades at the top of a strip, with a hanging stem beneath. Cut and fold the two blades in opposite directions as demonstrated; paper clip goes at the bottom.
  3. Predict the effect of 0, 1, and 2 clips. Keep the model, starting height, and release method unchanged.
  4. Drop the SAME model three times with 0 clips, then three with 1, then three with 2. Catch/collect it safely. Record spin and time if available.
  5. Choose the condition that fell slowest, support with your observations, and make one new design sketch, not a second unsafe high drop.
  6. Clean up all clips and paper scraps; scan BOTH paper pages before Finish Class.

Time plan: watch & learn 10 min; gather/check 5 min; make and test 25 min; compare/write 15 min; cleanup/scan 5 min. Print TWO pages, record what YOU observed, scan BOTH pages, then Finish this ONE class.
