Evidence Chain Audit

Goal: answer every requested part with two exact clues, a complete A→B→C chain, and exactly five synthesis sentences.

Read the fresh passage

Mara held two ferry tickets, but the storm flag snapped above the dock. “The last boat leaves at four,” the clerk warned. Mara checked the darkening sky, returned one ticket, and called her sister. “We’ll travel tomorrow,” she said, relieved that safety had won.

Model

Claim: Mara chooses safety over speed. Clue 1: the storm flag snaps. Clue 2: she returns a ticket. Together, the danger and her action prove the claim.

Chain: storm warning → Mara changes the plan → the sisters delay the trip.

Exact-five structure

  1. Make the claim.
  2. Give clue one.
  3. Give clue two.
  4. Explain how the clues prove the claim.
  5. State why the decision matters next.

Audit before submitting: two quoted or precisely copied clues; all three chain links; all prompt parts; five numbered synthesis sentences.
