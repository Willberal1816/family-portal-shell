On a normal north-up map: right = east, left = west, up = north, down = south. Northeast means both up and right.

Choose a map for the job

Physical map: shows land and water features such as mountains, rivers, and elevation.

Political map: shows human boundaries and places such as states, countries, capitals, and cities.

Thematic map: shows one topic or pattern, such as rainfall, crops, population, or temperature.

Worked choice: “Which states share a border with Wisconsin?” → political map, because the question is about state boundaries.

Worked choice: “Where are the highest mountains?” → physical map, because the question is about land/elevation.

Maps simplify

A map cannot show everything. The mapmaker chooses information that helps answer the map's purpose. A road map may leave out crop data because crop data would make the road information harder to use.
