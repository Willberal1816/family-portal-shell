From Interview Notes to a Fair Feature Story

Natalie | Language Arts | Wednesday, November 25, 2026 | 55 minutes

What you are learning: Write an attributed feature article without converting signups into attendees.

Learn First

A feature story can be engaging AND accurate. Attribute a quote to its source, e.g. 'Jordan said'. One person's experience does not represent every resident. Before publishing, check whether a number counts signups, participants or survey respondents.

Two worked examples

Accurate: 'Jordan said six attended.' Not supported: 'Everyone volunteered.'
Eight people signed up but six attended; the actual attendee denominator is SIX.

Source and materials

FICTIONAL COMMUNITY GARDEN NOTES
Jordan: 'Eight signed up; six attended Saturday.'
Ari: 'We planted four raised beds.'
Maya: 'I want to learn how the beds will be watered.'
Observer: event lasted two hours.
No full survey, cost figures or later harvest data.
