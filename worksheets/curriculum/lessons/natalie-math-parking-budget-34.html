Learn First

Total cost equals fixed fee plus rate times number of days. To compare plans, use the SAME number of days. A budget limit means cost must be less than or equal to the budget. Solve, then test an allowed whole-number day count.

Worked example

Plan P costs $5 + $3d and Plan Q costs $11 + $2d. Tie: 5 + 3d = 11 + 2d, so d = 6. Check P(6) = $23 and Q(6) = $23. Under a $20 budget, P allows at most 5 whole days because 5 + 3(5) = $20.
