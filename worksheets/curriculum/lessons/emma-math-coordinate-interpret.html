Reading Coordinate Movement

Math · Learn First
What you are learning: Use x and y coordinates to describe where points line up and how they move.

Key ideas

  • Same x-coordinate means the points line up vertically.
  • Same y-coordinate means the points line up horizontally.
  • Changing x moves left or right; changing y moves up or down.

Example

Example: (2,5) and (2,-1) have the same x-coordinate, so they are on the same vertical line.
