Potential & Kinetic Energy: Track the Transfer

Emma and Natalie · Science · 45 minutes · Learn First
What you are learning: identify stored potential energy and motion energy, track energy changes through a system, and use evidence instead of saying energy “disappeared.”

Two useful energy stores

Gravitational potential

Energy stored because an object is elevated in Earth’s gravitational field. More height usually means more stored energy when the object stays the same.

Kinetic

Energy of motion. A moving object has kinetic energy; faster motion means more kinetic energy when mass stays the same.

Worked model: A ball rests at the top of a ramp: high gravitational potential, zero kinetic. It rolls down: potential decreases while kinetic increases. At the bottom it slows because energy spreads to the surroundings as thermal energy and sound; it did not vanish.

Fair comparison

To test height, change only the starting height. Keep the same ball, ramp surface, release method, and measuring method. Release without pushing. Repeat at least three trials.
