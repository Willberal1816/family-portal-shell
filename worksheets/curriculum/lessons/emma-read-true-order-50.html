Read Your Book: Actions in Real Order

Emma · Reading · Wednesday November 18 · 45 minutes

What you are learning: Read your own book 25 active minutes, then show three supported actions in order and their outcome.

Read your REAL current book for 25 active minutes BEFORE writing.

Learn First

  1. Read for 25 ACTIVE minutes before writing. Record the title, author and exact pages you actually read.
  2. An action is something a character DOES, not something they only wish or plan.
  3. Find three separate actual events and list them first, next and then with exact page citations.
  4. Explain the middle action in a complete sentence—what happened because it occurred?
  5. Report the exact outcome confirmed by the pages, not a guessed later event.
  6. If your pages do not include three related actions, choose three distinct events and explain the connection without inventing facts.

Worked models

Model 1. Fictional model: p8 Lee checks a box; p10 Lee moves books; p12 Lee returns the list.
Model 2. Fictional limit: 'The list was returned' may be proven, but 'the teacher accepted it' may still be unconfirmed.
Common mistake to avoid: Never invent page numbers, dialogue, or a completed outcome the text hasn't yet shown.
