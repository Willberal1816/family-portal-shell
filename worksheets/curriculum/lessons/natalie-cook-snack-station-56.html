Kitchen Foundations: Organize and Portion a Snack

Natalie | Cooking/Life Skills | Friday, November 27, 2026 | 50 minutes

What you are learning: Prepare a safe no-heat snack, verify the measure, document actual portions and complete cleanup.

Learn First

Mise en place is the professional habit of preparing ingredients, tools and workspace before starting. Wash hands, clean the counter, set out a clean bowl and spoon, check the ingredients with an adult, and choose the correct dry measures. Work with only safe on-hand food.

To measure dry ingredients, fill the proper measure and level it. Two level quarter cups equal one level half cup; a heaped scoop is not equivalent. If you overfill, level the amount into a separate clean bowl, not the original container. Measure each ingredient before mixing.

Prepare one portion using one half cup adult-approved ready-to-eat cereal or crackers and one quarter cup adult-approved ready-to-eat fruit or dry snack. Verify the half cup using two level quarter-cup measures in a separate clean bowl, then combine. No sharp tools, heat, unsafe substitutions or purchasing. Record actual evidence, not intended actions.

Two worked examples

Model checklist: clean hands and counter; approved ingredients; two clean bowls and spoon; half-cup and quarter-cup measures; planned cleanup. If an item is unavailable, stop and ask the adult before beginning.
Model equivalence: level quarter cup + level quarter cup = level half cup. A half-cup measure filled by the two quarter cups should reach the rim. If not, re-level and check tools before adding food.

Source and materials

ONE SERVING - NO-HEAT SNACK
Adult-approved ready-to-eat cereal or crackers: half cup.
Adult-approved ready-to-eat fruit or dry snack: quarter cup.
Two clean bowls, spoon, half-cup and quarter-cup dry measures, clean towel.
Use only on-hand safe foods; no knives, heating, purchases or unfamiliar allergens. If supplies are missing, ask a parent.
