Goal: connect two exact setting details to mood and explain a safe choice using only printed evidence. Plan about 50 minutes. Read this teaching page before the Independent Work section.

Learn First

Setting shapes mood through specific details. A safety inference must point to evidence in the passage rather than inventing extra danger.

Worked model

If fog hides the far rail and the path is unusually quiet, both details can support an uneasy mood. A safe choice might be to slow down or wait where visibility is better.

Today's fresh source or situation

Fog pressed low over the creek, hiding the far end of the footbridge. The usual bicycle bells were absent, and even the reeds made no sound. Lila stopped beside the bright trail marker. She could see only the first three boards, each slick with mist. Instead of stepping onto the bridge, she called the ranger station from the marked post and waited where the path was wide.
