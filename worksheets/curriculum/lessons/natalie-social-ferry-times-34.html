Learn First

A schedule states PLANNED departures and arrivals; it does not prove a trip ran on time. To find travel time, subtract departure from arrival. Compare routes only when the place, day, and time window are the same. Published schedules do not establish the reason for delays.

Worked example

A fictional ferry leaves at 9:20 AM and arrives at 10:05 AM. Travel time is 45 minutes. That supports 'the timetable plans 45 minutes,' not 'every ferry takes exactly 45 minutes.'
