Fix the Sentence — Repair & Punctuation

What you are learning

First decide what is broken. Then choose the repair that matches the sentence.

Fragment: an unfinished thought. Add the missing subject, verb, or complete idea.

Run-on: two complete sentences pushed together. Separate them, or join them correctly.

Compound sentence: complete thought + comma + joining word + complete thought.

Worked fragment: “Because the bus was late.” This starts with because and leaves us waiting. Repair: “Because the bus was late, we called Dad.”

Worked run-on: “I packed my bag I forgot my book.” Repair: “I packed my bag, but I forgot my book.”

Decision check

  1. Can each side stand alone?
  2. If yes and you join them with and/but/so/etc., put a comma before the joining word.
  3. If the sentence starts with because/when/although/if and that opening clause comes first, put a comma after it.
  4. Read the repaired sentence aloud once to check that it is complete.
