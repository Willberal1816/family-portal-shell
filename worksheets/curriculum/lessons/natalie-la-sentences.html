Kinds of Sentences

Language Arts · Learn First
What you are learning: Recognize simple, compound, and complex sentences and understand how their parts fit together.
School word: independent clause = a group of words that can stand alone as a sentence; dependent clause = a group that cannot stand alone

Key ideas

  • A simple sentence has one complete thought.
  • A compound sentence joins two complete thoughts, often with and, but, or, so.
  • A complex sentence has one complete thought plus a part that cannot stand alone, often beginning with because, although, when, or while.

Example

Example: “I read.” is simple. “I read, and I took notes.” is compound. “Because I had time, I read.” is complex.
