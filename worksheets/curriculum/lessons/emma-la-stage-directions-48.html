Stage Crew: Write Directions Someone Can Follow

Emma · Language Arts · Monday, November 16, 2026 · 55 minutes

What you are learning

Turn exact stage setup facts into complete, orderly instructions without adding unsupported claims.

Learn First

  1. Directions help a reader do tasks in a sensible order. Each instruction should tell WHO does WHAT, using a clear verb.
  2. The source below is an INVENTED backstage checklist. Follow its specified order; do not add extra steps that the checklist never requested.
  3. A fragment such as 'On the left table' has a place but no complete instruction. Write 'Place the four red folders on the left table.'
  4. Use sequence words FIRST, NEXT, THEN and LAST; they explain order but do not replace a subject or action.
  5. A fact is supported if it appears in the checklist. A reason beginning 'because' is only justified when the source names that reason.
  6. An instruction should name important quantities and locations exactly. Four folders are not five, and the left table is not the back shelf.
  7. When directions say six sentences, count all six and check the opening capital letter and end punctuation.

Two worked models

Model 1: Model A: 'On the back shelf.' is a fragment. Better: 'Put the two cardboard signs on the back shelf.' This tells an action, quantity and place.
Model 2: Model B: A precise sequence starts 'First, clear the center aisle.' It next places props, then checks the stage. You may not say an aisle was cleared because of a fire drill unless the source says so.
A mistake to avoid: Writing a list of noun phrases is not the same as complete instructions. Keep exact quantities and do not invent reasons.

Independent work — after teaching

Complete all six tasks on three paper work pages. For drawing tasks, use the provided large panels. Scan all THREE numbered pages, then return to your same active School Portal class and Finish Class. Do not claim you performed measurements or reading you did not actually do.

Time plan: 15 minutes teaching and models, 30 minutes independent practice, 5 minutes checking.
