Learn First

A rain gauge measures rainfall depth in inches. The horizontal graph axis can show the observation day and the vertical axis can show inches. Bars are useful for separate daily amounts. To find the total, add measured amounts. A missing reading is unknown—not automatically zero—and a graph cannot prove why rain changed.

Two worked examples

Worked example 1. Example three measured days: Monday 0.2 in, Tuesday 0.5 in, Wednesday 0.1 in. Total is 0.2 + 0.5 + 0.1 = 0.8 in.
Worked example 2. If Thursday's gauge was not read, write 'not measured', not 0.0 in. An empty bar is not a measured dry day. Label what was truly observed.
Common mistake to avoid: Do not connect distinct daily bars as if intermediate rainfall was measured. Clearly mark missing observations; distinguish an actual 0.0-in reading from no reading.
