Snack Boxes: Which Extras Repeat?

Emma · Math · Wednesday November 18 · 50 minutes

What you are learning: Model repeated groups and once-only extras without mixing them up.
Model drawing

Learn First

  1. Every box holds the same number. The group size repeats, but the loose objects outside all boxes are added only once.
  2. Draw every box before writing an equation. Order A has three boxes of four and two loose crackers.
  3. Count inside groups as 3 × 4 = 12; add two once: 12 + 2 = 14 crackers.
  4. Writing 3 × (4 + 2) means two extra inside EVERY box; that is a different story and totals 18.
  5. A complete proof includes a labeled picture, repeated-addition check, multiplication expression and units.
  6. Read directions before moving numbers: 'outside all boxes' is not the same as 'inside each box'.

Worked models

Model 1. A: [4][4][4] plus two outside: 3 × 4 + 2 = 14 crackers.
Model 2. B: five equal groups of three plus one loose: 5 × 3 + 1 = 16 pencils, not 5 × 4.
Common mistake to avoid: Do not put loose extras inside every group.

Independent practice

Complete all SIX questions on FOUR printed pages. Scan all pages with their Tracking numbers, then return to the same class to Finish Class.

Time: 15 minutes learning, 30 minutes practice, 5 checking.

Your Six Questions

  1. Order A: draw three labeled boxes with four dots each and exactly TWO dots outside. Write a repeated-addition equation AND multiplication equation.
  2. Order B: draw five groups of three pencils and one outside; calculate the total and label which number is multiplied.
  3. Solve order C. Draw a clear group picture, give the full expression and check your answer by recounting.
  4. Error detective: explain in THREE sentences why 3 × (4 + 2) = 18 is the wrong solution for A, and write the corrected equation.
  5. Invent a different four-group packing problem with exactly two extras outside. Draw the model, solve, and write a sentence checking the total.
  6. Write FIVE complete sentences explaining how you tell what repeats, what is added once, and why your drawing proves the result.

The four printed pages provide writing space. Scan all four pages after completing your work.
