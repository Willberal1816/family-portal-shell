County Records: Place, Time, and Source Limits

Natalie · Social Studies · Tuesday, October 27, 2026

What you are learning: A source is strongest when a claim stays inside its place, time, and purpose. A county table cannot prove what happened in every county. A dated newspaper notice can describe what was announced, but not automatically what every person did.

Fully worked model

A 1924 Lake County school table supports a claim about Lake County schools in 1924. It does not prove a statewide trend or explain why attendance changed.

Source and materials

Source A — Lake County school table, 1924: North School 118 students; River School 96; Hill School 74. Source B — Lake County Gazette, September 3, 1924: “The county board announced a new bus route for North and River schools beginning September 8.”
