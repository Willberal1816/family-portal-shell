Measure the Exhibit: Feet and Inches

Natalie · Math · Wednesday November 18 · 50 minutes

What you are learning: Convert customary lengths, compare equal units, and check calculations in an exhibit plan.
Customary length model

Learn First

  1. One foot contains 12 inches and one yard contains 3 feet. Learn the unit relationship before calculating.
  2. To compare lengths, express both in the same units. Five feet eight inches = 5 × 12 + 8 = 68 inches.
  3. Banner B is 70 inches, so it exceeds banner A by 2 inches.
  4. Three yards two feet = 3 × 3 + 2 = 11 feet = 132 inches.
  5. Seven feet nine inches = 7 × 12 + 9 = 93 inches; add the nine extra inches only once.
  6. Reverse-check 93 inches: seven complete feet use 84 inches, leaving nine. Always label inches, feet or yards.

Worked models

Model 1. A: 5 ft 8 in = 68 in; 70 − 68 = 2 in, so B is 2 inches longer.
Model 2. Rope: 3 yards × 3 feet/yard = 9 feet; add 2 more feet to get 11 feet.
Common mistake to avoid: Do not compare bare numbers in different units or multiply the leftover inches by the feet.
