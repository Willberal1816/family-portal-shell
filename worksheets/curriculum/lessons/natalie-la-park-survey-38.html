Park Survey: Name the Respondents and Bound the Claim

Natalie · Language Arts · Tuesday, October 27, 2026

What you are learning: A survey claim applies to the people who actually answered, not automatically to everyone. Name the respondent group, report exact counts or percentages, and avoid inventing a cause the survey did not test.

Fully worked model

If 18 of 30 Saturday visitors chose shade trees, write: “Among the 30 Saturday visitors surveyed, 18 preferred more shade trees.” Do not write “The whole town wants trees because summers are hotter.”

Source and materials

On Saturday, volunteers asked 40 visitors entering River Park. Twenty-four chose more shade trees, 10 chose another drinking fountain, and 6 chose more benches. The survey did not ask why people chose an option. No residents outside the park entrance were surveyed.
