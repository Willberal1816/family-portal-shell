Choosing the Operation

Math · Learn First
What you are learning: Decide what a word problem is asking before choosing an operation.

Key ideas

  • Do not choose an operation from one keyword alone.
  • Ask what quantities you know and what relationship the question asks about.
  • Estimate what kind of answer should make sense before calculating.

Example

Example: If 3/4 of a pan serves 6 people, finding the number served by one whole pan is a division/scaling question, not just a keyword hunt.
