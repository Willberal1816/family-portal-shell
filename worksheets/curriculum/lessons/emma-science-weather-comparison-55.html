Weather Watch: Make a Fair Comparison

Emma | Science | Tuesday, November 24, 2026 | 50 minutes

What you are learning: Interpret temperature data and identify conditions in a fair test.

Learn First

Measurements need a time, place and unit. A shaded thermometer is not necessarily comparable to one in direct sun if we aim to test time alone.

A fair comparison changes one important factor while holding others steady. To calculate an observed temperature increase, subtract the first reading from the later reading.

Two worked examples

Shade sensor reads 68 F at 9 a.m. and 74 F at noon: the increase is 6 F.
Shade at noon is 74 F while direct sun is 83 F. The 9-degree difference alone cannot establish every cause.

Source and materials
