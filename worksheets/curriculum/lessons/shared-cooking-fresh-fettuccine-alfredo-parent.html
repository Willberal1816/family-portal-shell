Parent Prep: Fresh Fettuccine & Homemade Alfredo

What you are learning

This parent page supports the students’ learning about kneading, covered dough rest, fresh-pasta doneness, and heat-and-moisture control in Alfredo sauce.

Model checkpoints: dough should become smoother and spring back slowly; finished sauce should look glossy and coat the noodles.

One-glance ingredient list

RequiredOptional
200 g all-purpose flour (about 1¾ cups total)
2 large eggs
4 Tbsp butter
1 cup very finely grated Parmesan
Salt
Black pepper
Tap water; reserve ½–¾ cup hot pasta water		Up to ½ cup heavy cream for the American-style extension

Equipment

Scale or measuring cups; two bowls/work areas; forks; bench scraper; rolling pin or pasta machine; two labeled covered containers or wrap; large pot; tongs; colander; heat-safe cup; grater; large warm bowl or skillet; two printed lab records.

Split-day timeline

Adult availability

Be nearby during raw-egg cleanup and throughout the 1:30 hot-work session for boiling water, reserving pasta water, draining, and stovetop safety. The students perform the measuring, kneading, rolling, cutting, sauce work, evidence recording, and cleanup.
