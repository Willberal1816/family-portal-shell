Goal: translate a new two-step story into a grouped expression, solve it, and check that the expression matches every quantity. Plan about 50 minutes. Read this teaching page before the Independent Work section.

Learn First

Parentheses show what repeats in every group. A one-time amount stays outside the multiplication.

Worked model

Four museum teams each receive 6 visitor tickets and 2 guide tickets. Three staff passes are added once. Expression: 4 × (6 + 2) + 3 = 4 × 8 + 3 = 35 passes. Check: there are four equal groups; the 3 staff passes do not repeat.

Today's fresh source or situation

A festival makes 5 equal welcome packs. Each pack has 7 maps and 3 coupons. Two coordinator badges are added once.
