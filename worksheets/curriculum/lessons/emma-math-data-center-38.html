Community Garden Data: Median and Range

Emma · Math · Tuesday, October 27, 2026

What you are learning: A data set can be described by its center and spread. To find the median, order the values and locate the middle. With an even number of values, average the two middle values. The range is largest minus smallest. A conclusion must name the statistic and the values that support it.

Fully worked model

For 4, 7, 7, 10, the middle values are 7 and 7, so median = (7 + 7) ÷ 2 = 7. Range = 10 − 4 = 6.

Source and materials

Garden harvest weights in pounds: Bed A 8, Bed B 12, Bed C 10, Bed D 6, Bed E 9, Bed F 15.
