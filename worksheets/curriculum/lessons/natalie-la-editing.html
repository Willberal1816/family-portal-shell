Edit Your Sentences

Language Arts · Learn First
What you are learning: Use sentence structure and punctuation to make compound and complex sentences clear.

Key ideas

  • Find the complete thought or thoughts first.
  • When two complete thoughts are joined with and, but, or, or so, use the punctuation the sentence needs.
  • When a dependent clause comes first, a comma often separates it from the complete thought that follows.

Example

Example: “Because the storm passed, we went outside.” The opening part cannot stand alone, so the comma helps show where the complete thought begins.
