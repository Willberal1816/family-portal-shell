Sort the Story Before You Solve

Emma · Math · Wednesday, October 14, 2026 · Learn First

Goal: decide whether an amount belongs inside every group or outside the repeated groups, then build and defend the matching expression. Read this teaching page before opening the separate Independent Work printable.

Worked model

A display has 6 shelves with 4 models on each shelf and 3 models on one table. Draw six shelf boxes with 4 inside each, and put 3 outside. The expression is 6 × 4 + 3 = 27. If every shelf has 3 labels as well, the expression changes to 6 × (4 + 3) = 42.

Reliable method

  1. Underline the number of groups.
  2. Box what is inside each group.
  3. Circle any one-time extra outside the groups.
  4. Draw, write the expression, solve, and name the exact misgrouped number in an incorrect expression.

Before independent work

Explain the goal in your own words. On the printable, answer every numbered part, show the requested evidence or calculation, and scan both pages before finishing class.
