Aquarium Update: Semicolons and Supported Claims

Emma · Language Arts · Tuesday, October 27, 2026

What you are learning: A semicolon joins two complete, closely related sentences. Each side must stand alone, and the first word after the semicolon is lowercase unless it is a proper noun or I. When using a source, keep sequence separate from cause: one event happening before another does not prove it caused the second.

Fully worked model

The filter was cleaned; the water reading improved the next morning. This sentence reports sequence. It does not claim the cleaning caused the change unless the source proves that link.
