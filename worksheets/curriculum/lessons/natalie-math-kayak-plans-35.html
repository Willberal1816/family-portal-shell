Kayak Plans: Compare the Real Costs

Natalie · Math · Thursday, October 22, 2026 · 50 minutes

Goal: Compare two linear pricing rules with substitutions, a tie point, and a budget check.

One class: Read this Learn First lesson, work through both examples, complete all six items on TWO printable pages, scan both pages, and Finish Class. Learn First and Printable are not separate assignments.

Learn First

A pricing rule has a starting fee and a cost per hour. Total = starting fee + hourly rate × hours. Compare plans at the SAME number of hours. To find when they tie, set their totals equal and verify the answer in both rules.

Two worked models

Model 1. Plan C costs $5 + $2h. Plan D costs $1 + $4h. At h=2: C=5+2(2)=$9 and D=1+4(2)=$9. They tie at 2 hours.
Model 2. At h=4: C=5+2(4)=$13 and D=1+4(4)=$17. Plan C is $4 cheaper. The start fee is the cost at zero hours.
Check for mistakes: A lower starting fee does not always mean the lower total. Show substitutions and label dollars and hours.
