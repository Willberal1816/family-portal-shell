Goal: quote exact source facts, name each source's job, and write a connection that stays inside the evidence. Plan about 50 minutes. Read this teaching page before the Independent Work section.

Learn First

A receipt records a transaction. A notice announces or invites. Two sources may be related without proving that one caused the other.

Worked model

Receipt: 10:18 a.m.; 4 rolls; $8.00. Notice: Bread Share, 11:00 a.m. A safe claim is: The receipt shows a purchase before the announced Bread Share. It does not prove why the rolls were bought.
