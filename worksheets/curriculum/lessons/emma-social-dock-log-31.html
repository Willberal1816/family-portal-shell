Dock Log vs. Storm Story

Emma · Social Studies · Thursday, October 15, 2026 · Learn First

Goal: separate exact chronology from supported causation by using a fresh log, notice, and later account. Read this teaching page before opening the separate Independent Work printable.

Worked model

Dock log: 7:10 ropes inspected; 8:05 wind increased; 8:20 ferry delayed. The sequence proves inspection came before stronger wind and delay. It does not prove the inspection caused the delay. A cause claim needs evidence connecting wind or safety conditions to the decision.

Reliable method

  1. Label creator, date/time, and source purpose.
  2. Copy exact facts before explaining them.
  3. Build a timed sequence with no invented event.
  4. Call something a cause only when a source states or demonstrates the connection; otherwise write “the sources do not prove.”

Before independent work

Explain the goal in your own words. On the printable, answer every numbered part, show the requested evidence or calculation, scan both pages, and then finish class.
