Use a Clue from the Text

Language Arts · Learn First
What you are learning: Support an idea about a passage with a precise detail from the text.
School word: text evidence = words or details from the text that support an answer

Key ideas

  • State what you think the text shows.
  • Choose the detail that most directly supports that idea.
  • Explain how the detail connects to your answer.

Example

Example: If you say a character is nervous, “she reread the message three times and kept tapping her foot” is stronger evidence than a general detail about the room.
