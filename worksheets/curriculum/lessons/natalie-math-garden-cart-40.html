Learn First — follow these three steps

  1. Every rental plan has a fixed starting charge plus a charge PER day. Formula: total = start fee + (daily rate × number of days).
  2. A tie means both plans give the SAME total for the same number of days. Do the arithmetic for both sides, not just one.
  3. To prove one plan is cheaper, write the chosen input AND the TWO exact totals. The lower starting fee does not always win after many days.

See the exact worked examples

Worked example A: Plan Red = 5+4d; Plan Blue = 17+2d. At d=6, Red=29 and Blue=29, so the plans tie at 6 days.
Worked example B: At d=9, Red=5+36=41 and Blue=17+18=35; Blue is $6 cheaper. At d=2, Red=13 and Blue=21; Red is cheaper. BOTH totals prove each claim.
Check this common mistake: Common mistake: 'Blue is cheaper after the tie.' This is not a proof until you show one exact day count and BOTH correctly calculated totals.
