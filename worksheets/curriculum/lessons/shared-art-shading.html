Shading: Light to Dark

Art · Learn First
What you are learning: Use pencil pressure and light direction to make a flat shape look more three-dimensional.
School word: value = how light or dark a color or mark is

Key ideas

  • Light pressure makes a lighter value; heavier pressure makes a darker value.
  • Choose where the light is coming from before you shade.
  • The side facing the light is usually lighter and the side turned away is darker.

Example

Example: On a drawn sphere with light coming from the upper left, keep the upper-left area light and gradually darken toward the lower right.
