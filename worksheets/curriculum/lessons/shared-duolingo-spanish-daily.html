Spanish — One Hour on Duolingo

Today’s goal: Spend 60 minutes actively learning Spanish with your own Duolingo profile. This is a real Spanish class, not a worksheet or a race for points. Your progress in Duolingo stays with your own account.

Step 1 — Get ready

  1. Start your Spanish class in the School Portal first. Open Duolingo on your iPad (or use the website below).
  2. Check that your profile is yours, not your sister’s. Select the Spanish course. If you are new, start at the beginning or follow Duolingo’s placement/start choices.
  3. Turn sound on if possible, allow speaking exercises when appropriate, and find a comfortable place to focus.

Open Duolingo — Spanish

If the browser asks you to log in, use your own family-plan Duolingo account. If the installed app opens instead, continue there. Ask a parent for help if you cannot reach your Spanish course.

Step 2 — Spend 60 active minutes learning

About 30 minutes

Follow your personalized Spanish path and finish new lessons at a pace where you understand the words.

About 20 minutes

Use your available review, mistakes practice, stories, or listening exercises. Repeat anything you did not understand.

About 10 minutes

Practice listening and speaking. Repeat sentences aloud and check pronunciation using Duolingo’s lessons or available practice.

You can move these parts around to match the activities available in your account. Active learning time matters; streaks, XP, and number of lessons are not the goal. If you need a short rest, pause and continue until you reach 60 minutes of actual learning.

Step 3 — Finish your Spanish class

  1. Return to the School Portal after approximately 60 active minutes. Finish today’s Spanish class only.
  2. If a parent asks, show your Duolingo daily activity or the part of the course you reached. No screenshot, paper worksheet, quiz, or scanning is required.
  3. One short spoken sentence you remember is a useful way to show what you learned, but it is not an extra written assignment.

Parents: The School Portal can record class status and its own start/finish time, but this page does not sync Duolingo’s actual usage minutes or learning results. Verify active practice if an exact total is needed.
