Museum Memberships: Test the Linear Model

Natalie · Math · Tuesday, October 27, 2026

What you are learning: A linear cost rule has a starting value and a rate. Write C = start + rate × visits. A counterexample is one exact input where a broad claim fails. After two plans tie, compare a value beyond the tie before recommending a plan.

Fully worked model

Plan A: C = 18 + 4v. Plan B: C = 6 + 6v. At v = 6, both cost $42. At v = 8, A costs $50 and B costs $54, so A is cheaper after the tie.

Source and materials
