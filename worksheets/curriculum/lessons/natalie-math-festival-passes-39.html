Festival Passes: Tie Point and Post-Tie Proof

Natalie · Math · Wednesday, October 28, 2026 · 50 minutes

What you are learning: Model two start-plus-rate plans, find their tie point, and prove which plan is cheaper after the tie with one exact numerical counterexample.

Learn First

Write each plan as total = start fee + rate × uses. A tie occurs when the totals are equal.

A post-tie claim needs one exact input and both totals. A general statement without the two costs is not proof.

Fully worked model

Plan A = 8 + 2r; Plan B = 14 + 1r. At r = 6, both cost 20. At r = 9, A = 26 and B = 23, so B is cheaper after the tie.

Source and materials

River Festival: Blue Pass costs $6 plus $3 per ride. Gold Pass costs $18 plus $1 per ride. Use whole-number rides.
