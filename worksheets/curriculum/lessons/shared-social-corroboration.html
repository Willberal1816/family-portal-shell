Check Another Source

Social Studies · Learn First
What you are learning: Learn why historians check more than one source before deciding what probably happened.
School word: corroboration = checking one source against other independent evidence

Key ideas

  • One source can contain useful evidence without telling the whole story.
  • A strong second source is independent—it was not simply copied from the first source.
  • Compare what the sources agree on, disagree on, or add.

Example

Example: If a diary says a flood began before sunrise, a newspaper report, another diary, a photograph, or an official record could help check that claim.
