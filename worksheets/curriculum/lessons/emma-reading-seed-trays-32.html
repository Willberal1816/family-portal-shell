Learn First

A strong summary names the problem, the clues in order, and how the problem is solved. An inference must use at least two printed details.

Worked model

If a character finds wet wheel marks and an open shed latch, a supported inference connects both clues; it does not invent a new event.

Today's fresh source or situation

At 8:10, Mina had twenty minutes before the garden club arrived, but two seed trays were missing from the greenhouse bench. A thin line of soil led toward the tool shed. The shed door was closed, yet its lower hinge was muddy. Mina heard the cart's loose wheel click inside. She opened the door, found both trays balanced on the cart, and returned them to the sunny bench before 8:30.
