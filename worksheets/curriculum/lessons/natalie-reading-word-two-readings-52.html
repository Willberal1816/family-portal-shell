Reading: Words and Two Possible Meanings

Natalie · Reading · Saturday November 21, 2026 · 45 minutes

What you are learning: Support an analysis of an author's word choice with a real page and alternate interpretation.

Learn First

Context surrounds a word. A synonym is a close substitute; an alternate reading is another text-supported meaning.

Two worked models

Model 1: FICTIONAL model: 'The hallway swallowed footsteps' suggests silence; 'swallowed' creates a strong image.
Model 2: A different reading is that a thick rug muffled the sound; neither interpretation proves a supernatural cause.
Check for this mistake: Quote only what you actually read and cite the exact page.

Source and facts

  • Read your OWN book for 25 active minutes.
  • Choose a meaningful word or short phrase from a REAL page read today.
  • Find two nearby context clues; mark unsupported readings uncertain.

Read the models, then complete your six questions using your source. Write only what the source supports.

All six independent-work questions

These exact questions are visible on screen and appear in the matching four-page PDF. Write on paper, scan all pages and finish this class in the School Portal.

Question 1. Log title, starting and ending pages, 25 active minutes, and the chosen word with its page.

Question 2. Copy one short exact phrase and identify two real nearby context details.

Question 3. Give two synonyms, comparing how each changes the tone or precision in three sentences.

Question 4. Describe two DIFFERENT plausible interpretations supported by exact page-cited details.

Question 5. Identify one tempting but unproved interpretation and name the missing evidence.

Question 6. Write six analytical sentences: word, page, short quote, mood effect, synonym contrast, two readings and careful conclusion.
