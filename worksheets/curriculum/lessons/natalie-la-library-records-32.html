Library Log, Poster, and Bounded Claim

Goal: classify source roles, preserve the exact population, and avoid turning sequence into cause. Plan about 50 minutes. Read this teaching page before the Independent Work section.

Learn First

A circulation log records activity; a poster promotes an event. A survey describes only the people surveyed. Earlier-then-later is not automatically cause.

Worked model

A log shows checkouts rose after a poster appeared. That sequence supports order, not the claim that the poster caused the rise unless stronger evidence rules out alternatives.

Today's fresh source or situation

Circulation log: 42 mystery books checked out Sept. 1-7 and 58 checked out Sept. 8-14. Poster: Mystery Night, Sept. 8. Exit slip: 16 attendees responded; 11 said the display helped them choose a book.
