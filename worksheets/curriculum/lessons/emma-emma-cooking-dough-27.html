Fresh Pasta: Make, Rest, Roll and Cut

Emma · Cooking · Sunday, September 27, 2026 · Learn First

What you are learning: make YOUR OWN pasta dough, document a real rest, roll and cut evenly, and leave the workspace clean without repeating the earlier sauce lesson. Read the examples, then use the separate Independent Work printable for all answers. This teaching page has no written work to turn in.

Learn the idea

This is a new dough/roll/cut continuation. The earlier class produced sauce but did not establish individual dough evidence. Today each student measures a separate 100 g flour and one-egg dough. Mixing combines ingredients; kneading brings the dough together; a covered, refrigerated 30-minute rest helps the dough relax before rolling. This lesson uses its own 30-minute-rest method and does NOT open the earlier three-hour-rest video.

Put a modest dusting of flour on a clean work surface, press the dough into a disk, roll from center outward, turn and repeat until evenly thin. With adult supervision, fold lightly or use a safe guide to cut even ribbons and separate strands. Do not taste uncooked flour/egg dough; wash hands, utensils and surfaces with soap and water. Cooking and sauce are not part of today’s class. Keep shaped pasta refrigerated if not cooking promptly with an adult.

Words that help

  • knead: press, fold and turn dough until cohesive
  • rest: covered time for dough to relax before rolling
  • even thickness: dough rolled without a large thick center or thin tear-prone edge
  • cross-contamination: germs transferring from raw ingredients to other food or surfaces

Worked example 1

Measurement model: each student needs 100 g flour and one egg. For two individual doughs, the shared totals are 100+100=200 g and 1+1=2 eggs. Label the doughs so evidence cannot be confused.

Worked example 2

Technique model: a thick dough disk rolls unevenly. Press and roll center-out, rotate a quarter-turn, dust lightly only when sticky, and keep strips similar in width. A 30-minute rest begun at minute 25 finishes near minute 55; the timer is part of the evidence, not a skipped instruction.

Use this reference while you work

Student recipe, one separate dough each: 100 g all-purpose or 00 flour + 1 large egg; a little extra flour to dust. Shared family total: 200 g plus dusting flour, 2 eggs. Tools: two bowls or clean stations, fork, clean board, kitchen scale, towel or wrap, rolling pin, bench scraper, adult-supervised knife or pasta cutter, two labeled trays. Wash hands and surfaces after raw egg/flour.

MinuteTask
0–5Wash hands, prepare two labeled stations.
5–15Measure flour; parent helps crack egg; mix with fork.
15–25Knead until a smooth, cohesive dough ball forms.
25–55Cover; rest dough about 30 minutes in refrigerator. Record measurements and explain method during rest.
55–70On floured board roll from middle outward, turn, dust lightly; adult supervises cutting into even ribbons.
70–80Label/stow dough cold if not cooked now, clean raw-egg/flour surfaces, scan records.

Safety: Do not taste raw dough; wash after touching uncooked eggs/flour. Keep knife flat on board, fingers away, adult within reach. No boiling water or Alfredo sauce in this class.
