Dividing Decimals

Math · Learn First
What you are learning: Use place value and estimation to divide decimals.

Key ideas

  • Estimate first using nearby whole numbers.
  • If the divisor has a decimal, move its decimal to make it a whole number and move the dividend decimal the same number of places.
  • Put the decimal in the quotient directly above its new place in the dividend.

Example

Example: 7.2 ÷ 0.6 can be rewritten as 72 ÷ 6, so the answer is 12.
