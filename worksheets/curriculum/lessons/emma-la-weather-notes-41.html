Weather Station Notes: Two Clauses, Three Evidence Sentences

Emma · Language Arts · Friday, October 30, 2026 · 55 minutes

What you will be able to do: Use a semicolon only between complete clauses and write exactly three sentences: fact, fact, evidence limit.

Learn First

  1. A semicolon joins two complete clauses. Each side must work as a sentence.
  2. Do not put a semicolon after an opening time phrase such as “At 2:00 p.m.”
  3. An evidence report can use exactly three sentences: fact 1; fact 2; what the record cannot prove.

Worked examples

Correct: “The display showed 62°F; the later reading was 70°F.” Both sides are complete clauses.
Correct three-sentence model: “The first reading was 62°F. The later reading was 70°F. These two observations do not prove what caused the change.”

Class source

Source/data for this class: Practice station log: At 2:00 p.m., the outdoor display read 62°F. At 3:00 p.m., it read 70°F. A worker cleaned the sensor cover at 2:20 p.m. Clouds moved away after 2:30 p.m. No controlled test was performed.
