The Workshop Key: Follow the Evidence

Goal: advance reading inference and mood using two exact story details, ordered events, and a complete summary.

Plan: 45 minutes. Read Learn First, examine the worked example, complete all six questions on the two printable pages, scan both pages, then Finish Class. The Learn First and Printable tabs are TWO PARTS OF ONE CLASS.

Learn First

Read the passage twice. A strong inference cites the exact details that support it. To explain mood, name TWO details from the setting and connect each to how the scene feels. A complete summary includes the problem, the action that changes it, and the ending.

Worked example

Example: 'The hallway was empty and the lights flickered' supports an uneasy mood because both details suggest uncertainty. 'Maya found the switch and turned on the lights' completes the outcome; stopping at the search would be incomplete.

The Workshop Key

The community workshop opened early for a Saturday repair fair. Lila arrived with a toolbox and a list of bicycles that needed new reflectors. Rain tapped against the high windows, and the empty room echoed when she called for the volunteer coordinator.

At the storage cabinet, Lila discovered that the small brass key was missing from its usual hook. Without it, she could not reach the reflectors. She checked the desk but found only a sign-in sheet and a folded note that read, 'Moved supplies before the roof inspection. Check the labeled blue box.'

She remembered that the coordinator had moved several boxes away from a ceiling leak. Lila walked to the dry back corner and found three boxes, but only one had a blue label. Inside were the cabinet key, a spare lock, and a pencil. She carried the key back without opening the other boxes.

When the coordinator arrived, Lila showed him the note and explained how she had found the correct box. He thanked her for following the label rather than guessing. Together they opened the cabinet, placed the reflectors on the repair tables, and welcomed the first visitors on time.
