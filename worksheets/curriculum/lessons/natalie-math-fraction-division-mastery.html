Fraction Division — Make the Meaning Solid

Today is not a new topic. We are making sure the important parts of fraction division are solid before moving on.

1. What division is asking

When you see 2 ÷ 2/3, one useful question is: How many groups of 2/3 fit inside 2?

The answer is 3 because three groups of 2/3 make 2.

2. Mixed numbers first

Before dividing a mixed number, turn it into an improper fraction.

Example: 2 1/4 = 9/4. Then divide by multiplying by the reciprocal of the divisor.

Do not flip the first number. Only take the reciprocal of the number you are dividing by.

3. A quotient needs meaning in a story

If a problem asks what your answer means, do not write only the number. Write one complete sentence that names the groups, pieces, containers, trips, or other thing being counted.

Example: If 3/4 cup goes into each batch and you have 3 cups, the quotient 4 means you can make 4 batches.

Now do your mastery check

  1. Read each question once.
  2. Circle or underline what the quotient is counting.
  3. Convert any mixed number carefully before dividing.
  4. When asked what an answer means, write one complete sentence with the context.
