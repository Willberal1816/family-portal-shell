2. Division means groups

One useful meaning of division is “How many groups of this size fit in that amount?”

For 2 ÷ 2/3, picture two wholes split into thirds. There are six thirds total. Each group of 2/3 uses two thirds, so three groups fit.

2 ÷ 2/3 = 3 because three groups of 2/3 make 2.

3. Definitions

  • Dividend: the amount being divided.
  • Divisor: the size of each group; the number you divide by.
  • Quotient: the result of division.
  • Reciprocal: a fraction with numerator and denominator switched.

4. Worked example — mixed number and meaning

2 1/4 ÷ 3/4

  1. Convert the mixed number: 2 1/4 = 9/4.
  2. Interpret the question: how many 3/4-sized groups fit in 9/4?
  3. Only the divisor gets the reciprocal: 9/4 × 4/3.
  4. Simplify: 9/3 = 3.
  5. Interpret the quotient: three groups of 3/4 fit in 2 1/4.

5. Worked example — quotient larger than the starting amount

3 1/2 ÷ 2/3 becomes 7/2 × 3/2 = 21/4 = 5 1/4.

This is reasonable because the divisor, 2/3, is smaller than 1. More than three whole groups of that smaller size can fit inside 3 1/2.

6. More fully worked examples

Meaning: 3 ÷ 1/2 asks how many one-half-sized groups fit in 3. The answer is 6 groups.

Mixed-number conversion: 1 3/5 = 8/5.

Setup: 1 3/5 ÷ 4/5 becomes 8/5 × 5/4. Only the divisor is reciprocated.

Size check: 4 ÷ 3/4 must be greater than 4 because groups smaller than 1 fit into 4 more than four times.

Story meaning: 5 feet ÷ 1 1/4 feet counts how many 1 1/4-foot pieces fit in 5 feet.

7. Common mistakes and corrections

Mistake: 2 1/2 ÷ 3/5 = 5/2 × 3/5. Correction: the divisor must be reciprocated, so the setup is 5/2 × 5/3.

Mistake: 3 ÷ 1/2 = 1 1/2. Correction: dividing by 1/2 should produce a quotient larger than 3; the correct quotient is 6.

Mistake: a story answer is written only as “4.” Correction: the context belongs in the answer, such as “4 batches.”
