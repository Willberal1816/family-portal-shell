2. Division means groups

One useful meaning of division is “How many groups of this size fit in that amount?”

For 2 ÷ 23, picture two wholes split into thirds. There are six thirds total. Each group of 23 uses two thirds, so three groups fit.

2 ÷ 23 = 3 because three groups of 23 make 2.

3. Words you will use

  • Dividend: the amount being divided.
  • Divisor: the size of each group; the number you divide by.
  • Quotient: the result of division.
  • Reciprocal: a fraction with numerator and denominator switched.

4. Worked example — mixed number and meaning

2 14 ÷ 34

  1. Convert the mixed number: 2 14 = 94.
  2. Interpret the question: how many 34-sized groups fit in 94?
  3. Only the divisor gets the reciprocal: 94 × 43.
  4. Simplify: 93 = 3.
  5. Interpret the quotient: three groups of 34 fit in 2 14.

5. Worked example — quotient larger than the starting amount

3 12 ÷ 23 becomes 72 × 32 = 214 = 5 14.

This is reasonable because the divisor, 23, is smaller than 1. More than three whole groups of that smaller size can fit inside 3 12.

6. More fully worked examples

Meaning: 3 ÷ 12 asks how many one-half-sized groups fit in 3. The answer is 6 groups.

Mixed-number conversion: 1 35 = 85.

Setup: 1 35 ÷ 45 becomes 85 × 54. Only the divisor is reciprocated.

Size check: 4 ÷ 34 must be greater than 4 because groups smaller than 1 fit into 4 more than four times.

Story meaning: 5 feet ÷ 1 14 feet counts how many 1 14-foot pieces fit in 5 feet.

7. Common mistakes and corrections

Mistake: 2 12 ÷ 35 = 52 × 35. Correction: the divisor must be reciprocated, so the setup is 52 × 53.

Mistake: 3 ÷ 12 = 1 12. Correction: dividing by 12 should produce a quotient larger than 3; the correct quotient is 6.

Mistake: a story answer is written only as “4.” Correction: the context belongs in the answer, such as “4 batches.”
