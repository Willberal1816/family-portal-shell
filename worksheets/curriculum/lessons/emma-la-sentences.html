Complete Sentences

Language Arts · Learn First
What you are learning: Tell the difference between a complete sentence and a fragment, then fix fragments.
School word: fragment = an incomplete sentence

Key ideas

  • A complete sentence has a subject and tells what that subject does, is, or has.
  • A complete sentence expresses a full thought.
  • A fragment is only part of a sentence and needs something added.

Example

Example: “Because the rain stopped.” is a fragment. “We went outside because the rain stopped.” is complete.
