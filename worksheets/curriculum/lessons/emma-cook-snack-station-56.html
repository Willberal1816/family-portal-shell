Kitchen Foundations: Prepare a Snack Station

Emma | Cooking/Life Skills | Friday, November 27, 2026 | 50 minutes

What you are learning: Prepare one no-heat snack with clean tools, a complete setup, a checked level measure and cleanup.

Learn First

Mise en place means setting everything in its place before you begin. Wash hands with soap and water, clean the work surface, gather a bowl, a clean spoon, dry measuring cups and the adult-approved ingredients. Read the recipe before touching food.

A dry measuring cup is filled to the rim and leveled with a clean straight edge. Two level quarter cups equal one level half cup. If you overfill, remove the extra into a separate clean bowl before adding the ingredient; do not return handled food to its original container.

For this snack, put one half cup of ready-to-eat cereal or crackers and one quarter cup of parent-approved ready-to-eat fruit or other dry snack into a clean bowl. Use only ingredients already safe for you and approved by an adult. No cutting, cooking, heating or sharp tools. Clean up after preparing; record what you actually did.

Two worked examples

Example mise en place: wash hands, wipe counter, put bowl and spoon on clean towel, select half-cup and quarter-cup tools, get ingredients, check adult approval. This avoids hunting for tools while food is open.
Example measurement: a quarter cup is overfull. Level it over a separate clean bowl. The remaining amount in the cup is the intended quarter cup, not a heaped measure. If the ingredient spills, stop, clean up, measure again.

Source and materials

ONE SERVING - NO-HEAT SNACK
Adult-approved ready-to-eat cereal or crackers: half cup.
Adult-approved ready-to-eat fruit or dry snack: quarter cup.
Clean bowl, spoon, half-cup and quarter-cup dry measures, clean towel.
Use only on-hand safe foods. No knives, heat or purchases. If supplies are missing, ask a parent before starting.
