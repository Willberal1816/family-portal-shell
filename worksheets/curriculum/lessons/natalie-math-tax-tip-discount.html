Tax, Tip & Discounts

Natalie · Math · Guided Teaching
What you are learning: Use percent to calculate discounts, tax, and tips, and keep track of whether a percent is subtracted from or added to the original amount.

Decide what the percent does

  • Discount: subtract the percent amount from the original price.
  • Tax: add the percent amount to the price after any discount that applies first.
  • Tip: add a percent of the bill amount used as the tip base.
Discount example: 25% off $80 means a $20 discount, so the sale price is $60.
Tax example: 6% tax on $50 is $3, so the total is $53.
Tip example: 18% of a $40 bill is $7.20, so the total with tip is $47.20.

Multi-step order matters

If a $100 item is 20% off and then taxed 5%, first reduce the price to $80. Then find 5% of $80, which is $4. The final total is $84.

Estimate before calculating

Ten percent gives a fast benchmark. A 20% discount should be about twice 10%; an 18% tip should be a little less than 20%. Estimation helps catch decimal mistakes.
