The Clinic Supply Log: Evidence, Action, Result

Natalie · Reading · Friday, October 30, 2026 · 45 minutes

What you will be able to do: Identify exact evidence, order actions, distinguish later events, and summarize without inventing a cause.

Learn First

  1. Use the log’s exact time for each event.
  2. Name the evidence that led to the action.
  3. Keep the immediate verified result separate from a later unrelated event.

Worked examples

A check at 10:04 is not the same as a move at 10:09.
A complete summary states the problem, evidence, actions in order, and verified result.

Class source

Source/data for this class: Practice log: 10:02 — Lena found an unlabeled box on the clinic cart. 10:04 — The packing sheet listed the box as bandages for Room 3. 10:07 — Lena wrote Room 3 on the label. 10:09 — She placed the box on the Room 3 shelf. 10:15 — A nurse checked the shelf and signed the log. 11:00 — New gloves arrived.

Directions and timing

Read Learn First and both examples (15 minutes). Complete all six numbered items on the TWO printable pages (25–30 minutes). Check your work, scan BOTH pages with the Tracking number visible, then Finish this ONE class (5 minutes). Materials: printed pages and pencil only.
