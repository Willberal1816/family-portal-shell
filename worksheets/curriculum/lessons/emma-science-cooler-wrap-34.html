Learn First

Thermal energy moves from a warmer object toward cooler surroundings. Insulation can slow energy transfer. Compare the SAME starting temperature and the SAME time interval. Temperature drop is START minus FINISH in degrees Fahrenheit (°F). A difference in one demonstration does not prove every material always works the same.

Worked example

A drink begins at 140°F and ends at 128°F after 10 minutes: the drop is 140 − 128 = 12°F. If another drink falls 19°F, the first lost 7°F LESS in that observation. Say what the measurements show, not what they prove forever.
