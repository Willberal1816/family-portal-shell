Energy Data Detective: Convert, Compare, Conserve

Goal: turn mixed feet-and-inches measurements into one unit, compare exact averages, and explain where motion energy goes.

1. Convert before averaging

Use total inches = 12 × feet + inches. Example: 4 ft 7 in = 12×4+7 = 55 in.

Worked example

Trials: 3 ft 10 in, 4 ft 2 in, 4 ft 0 in.

Convert: 46 in, 50 in, 48 in. Average: (46+50+48)÷3 = 48 in = 4 ft 0 in.

2. Compare exact evidence

State both averages and the difference. “The high release averaged 68 in and the low release averaged 52 in, so the high release traveled 16 in farther.” A single unusual trial does not replace the average.

3. Energy changes form

When the ball stops, its motion energy does not disappear. Friction and deformation transfer much of it to thermal energy; some becomes sound.

Fair follow-up

Change one different variable, such as surface type. Keep release height, ball, ramp, start line, and measuring method constant.
