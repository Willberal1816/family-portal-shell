Make Writing Clear

Language Arts · Learn First
What you are learning: Edit sentences so the reader can understand them easily.
School word: run-on = two complete thoughts joined incorrectly

Key ideas

  • Check that every sentence is complete.
  • Fix two complete thoughts that run together by using punctuation or a joining word.
  • Use commas to separate items in a list.

Example

Example: “I packed a book a snack and a jacket” becomes “I packed a book, a snack, and a jacket.”
