Unit Rates and Proportions

Math · Learn First
What you are learning: Compare quantities by finding an amount for one unit, then use that relationship to solve problems.
School word: unit rate = an amount for one unit; proportion = an equation showing two equal ratios

Key ideas

  • A rate compares two different kinds of quantities.
  • A unit rate tells how much for 1.
  • Equivalent ratios can be used to scale up or down.

Example

Example: 180 miles in 3 hours is 60 miles per 1 hour. The unit rate is 60 miles per hour.
