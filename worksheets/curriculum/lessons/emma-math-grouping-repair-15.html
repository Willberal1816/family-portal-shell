Groups or Extras? Explain the Story

Emma · Math · Friday, September 25, 2026 · Learn First

What you are learning: decide which amounts are repeated, write the exact expression, then finish every calculation in order. Read these worked examples before opening the separate Independent Work page. There is no written work to complete on this teaching page.

Two similar stories with different answers

Four trays each have 3 sandwiches and 2 apples. All five items repeat: 4 × (3 + 2) = 20. Four trays each have 3 sandwiches, but 2 apples are loose on a counter: 4 × 3 + 2 = 14. The words each and loose decide what repeats.

Share, then add

Thirty-six pencils are shared equally among six desks. Each desk then gets two more pencils. Divide first: 36 ÷ 6 = 6. Add 2 to each share: 36 ÷ 6 + 2 = 8. Do not multiply the share by two; the story says receives two more.

Finish the full expression

For 9 + 3 × (4 + 2) − 5: parentheses 4 + 2 = 6; multiplication 3 × 6 = 18; then 9 + 18 − 5 = 22. Do not stop at 18 or do 9 + 3 first.

Fix an incorrect grouping

If 40 counters are split among 5 groups and each group receives 3 extra, the correct expression is 40 ÷ 5 + 3 = 11. The incorrect 40 ÷ (5 + 3) changes the number of groups from 5 to 8 and does not match the story.
