The Seed Tray Mix-Up

Emma · Reading · Wednesday, October 14, 2026 · Learn First

Goal: track the problem and solution in a fresh passage while completing an exact own-book log and numbered responses. Read this teaching page before opening the separate Independent Work printable.

Fresh passage

Mara reached the greenhouse early and found three seed trays with no labels. She remembered that the blue tray had been placed nearest the warm window, but all three trays now sat on the center table. Instead of guessing, Mara checked yesterday’s photo, matched a chipped corner on the blue tray, and replaced each label. Before leaving, she added a simple map to the clipboard so the next helper could check the trays without relying on memory.

Reliable method

  1. Log your own-book title and exact pages first.
  2. State the problem in one direct sentence.
  3. Use one exact clue to explain Mara’s decision.
  4. Number every response to match the prompt.

Before independent work
