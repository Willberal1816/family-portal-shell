Energy Path and Fair-Test Audit

Natalie · Science · Wednesday, October 14, 2026 · Learn First

Goal: trace an energy path and design a fair test with a measurable outcome. Read this teaching page before opening the separate Independent Work printable.

Worked model

A cart released from a higher ramp begins with more gravitational potential energy. As it rolls, energy transfers mainly to motion, sound, and thermal energy. A fair test changes one variable—ramp height—while holding cart, surface, release point method, and measuring tool constant. “Went better” is not a measured outcome; distance in centimeters is.

Reliable method

  1. Name the starting energy store.
  2. Trace where energy goes during motion and impact.
  3. Choose exactly one changed variable.
  4. Name a measurable outcome with units and at least four controls.
