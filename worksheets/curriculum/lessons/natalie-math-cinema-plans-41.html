Cinema Plans: Prove the Break-Even Day

Natalie · Math · Friday, October 30, 2026 · 50 minutes

What you will be able to do: Compare two linear cost rules, prove the tie with a full equation, and choose a plan beyond the tie.

Learn First

  1. Write start fee + daily rate × days for each plan.
  2. Set the two expressions equal, solve, then substitute the answer into both rules.
  3. A tie proof is incomplete until both totals are shown.

Worked examples

Plan A: 10+6d. Plan B: 26+4d. Tie: 10+6d=26+4d, so 2d=16 and d=8.
Check: 10+6(8)=58 and 26+4(8)=58. Both totals match.

Class source

Source/data for this class: Practice membership plans: Plan Red costs $15 plus $7 per day. Plan Blue costs $35 plus $5 per day. Use whole-number days.

Directions and timing
