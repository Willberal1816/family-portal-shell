Heart Rate Recovery

Health · Learn First
What you are learning: Learn what happens to heart rate after exercise and how to compare recovery fairly.
School word: recovery = the period when the body returns toward its resting state after activity

Key ideas

  • During activity, muscles need more oxygen and nutrients, so the heart usually beats faster.
  • After activity stops, heart rate gradually moves back toward its resting level.
  • For a fair comparison, keep exercise time, rest time, and pulse-measuring method consistent.

Example

Example: Compare heart rate 1 minute after two activities only if both activities lasted the same amount of time and you measured the pulse the same way.
