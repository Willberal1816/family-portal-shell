The Quiet Generator

Natalie · Reading · Wednesday, October 14, 2026 · Learn First

Goal: use exact clues to build an ordered event chain and explain how setting changes the mood. Read this teaching page before opening the separate Independent Work printable.

Fresh passage

At dusk, the clinic’s generator sputtered once and went silent. Lani heard only rain tapping the metal roof. She checked the fuel gauge: half full. Then she noticed a loose cable trembling near the battery. After switching off the main breaker, she tightened the connection and called the technician before restarting anything. The waiting room stayed dim, but the nurse’s steady instructions replaced the earlier panic with cautious focus.

Reliable method

  1. Quote or precisely paraphrase the clue.
  2. Explain what that clue proves—not more.
  3. Build the decision chain in order.
  4. Connect a setting detail to a specific mood effect.
