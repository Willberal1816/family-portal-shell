Source Role and Synthesis Audit

What you are learning: select exact details, explain what each source’s role makes it strong for, and write a neutral synthesis with a limitation.

Source A — maintenance record

On May 6, staff closed the east garden after wind broke two fence panels. A crew replaced both panels on May 8. The garden reopened May 9 after the path was cleared.

Source B — visitor note

During the closure, visitors used the west gate and could still reach the greenhouse. The children’s planting beds were roped off. By the following weekend, the usual route was open.

Worked model

Use Source A for an exact repair date because a maintenance record tracks work and dates. Use Source B for how visitors experienced the closure because it describes access. A neutral synthesis can combine them, but neither source proves how every visitor felt.

Audit every factual phrase: Is it copied exactly or safely paraphrased? Did I name both sources and one limitation?
