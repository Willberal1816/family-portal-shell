Equation Audit: Pair, Build, Prove

Natalie · Math · 20 minutes Learn First + 30 minutes independent evidence

Your job

Today you are an equation auditor. You will keep each y-change paired with its matching x-change, build the whole rule y=kx+b, and prove the rule by substitution.

1. Pair the same two points

When you find a rate, the top and bottom of the fraction must use the same point pair. Label the points before subtracting.

Worked model:
x037
y82036

Pair A: (0,8) to (3,20): (20-8)÷(3-0)=12÷3=4.

Pair B: (3,20) to (7,36): (36-20)÷(7-3)=16÷4=4.

Both give k=4. At x=0, y=8, so b=8. Whole rule: y=4x+8.

Audit failure: (20-8)÷(7-0) mixes the y-values from the first pair with x-values from a different pair. Circle the two points first so the coordinates cannot drift apart.

2. Prove, do not only say “it works”

Verification means substitution. Write the rule, replace x, calculate, and compare with the table.

For y=4x+8:

  • x=0: 4(0)+8=8 ✓
  • x=3: 4(3)+8=20 ✓
  • x=7: 4(7)+8=36 ✓

3. Translate a real situation completely

Example: A bike shop charges $11 to begin and $6 per hour.
  • k=6: $6 for each hour.
  • b=11: $11 before any hours.
  • Rule: y=6x+11.
  • At x=4: y=6(4)+11=35.

Final audit checklist

  1. I paired the same two points in each rate calculation.
  2. I wrote k and b.
  3. I wrote the whole rule.
  4. I substituted every requested x-value.
  5. I explained what k and b mean with units.
