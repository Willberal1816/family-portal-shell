Add Strong Details

Language Arts · Learn First
What you are learning: Build one clear paragraph with details that actually support the main idea.

Key ideas

  • Start with one main idea.
  • Add specific details, examples, or reasons that explain that idea.
  • End with a sentence that wraps up the same idea instead of starting a new topic.

Example

Example: Instead of “The park was fun,” add details such as what you did, what you noticed, and why those details made it fun.
