Camp Supply Crates: Groups and One Extra

Emma · Math · Tuesday, October 20, 2026

Goal: show exactly what repeats in every group and what is added only once; correct a fresh grouping error.

Plan: 50 minutes. Read the example, study the fresh information, then complete the two printable pages. This is one class, not two assignments.

Learn First

Draw one box for every group. Put only the repeated objects inside each box. Write one-time extras outside the boxes. Parentheses surround the contents of ONE group.

Never multiply an extra item unless the story says each group gets it.

Worked example

Three teams each carry 2 flashlights and 4 maps. One extra first-aid kit is carried by the leader. The expression is 3 × (2 + 4) + 1 = 19 items. The kit stays outside the groups because there is only one.

Today's information

A camp prepares 4 hiking groups. Each group receives 3 trail maps and 2 water bottles. The leader brings 4 extra first-aid stickers once, not four per group.

Second situation: 3 birdwatching teams each get 5 notebooks and 1 pencil; the guide carries 2 spare straps once.

Ready check: Explain the worked example in your own words before answering. Work independently and show your reasoning.
