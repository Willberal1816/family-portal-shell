Find Clues in the Story

Language Arts · Learn First
What you are learning: Use details from a story to figure out something the author does not say directly.
School word: inference = an idea you figure out from clues plus what you know

Key ideas

  • First, notice an exact clue in the text.
  • Then ask what that clue suggests when you combine it with what you already know.
  • A strong answer points back to the clue instead of making a random guess.

Example

Example: “Maya checked the clock twice and stood by the door before the bell.” A reasonable answer is that Maya is eager to leave, because those actions are the clue.
