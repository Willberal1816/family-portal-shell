Inside or Outside? Correction Lab

Emma · Math · Thursday, October 15, 2026 · Learn First

Goal: correctly place a quantity inside every equal group or outside as a one-time extra, then prove the expression with a model. Read this teaching page before opening the separate Independent Work printable.

Worked model

A club packs 5 kits. Each kit has 3 pens and 2 pencils, and there is 1 teacher marker. The grouped items are inside: 5 × (3 + 2). The marker is outside: + 1. Total: 5 × 5 + 1 = 26 supplies.

Reliable method

  1. Name the number of equal groups.
  2. List what repeats inside each group.
  3. List any one-time extra outside.
  4. Draw the groups, write parentheses when needed, solve, and label units.

Before independent work

Explain the goal in your own words. On the printable, answer every numbered part, show the requested evidence or calculation, scan both pages, and then finish class.
