Value Shapes to Form: Light Direction

Natalie · Art · Tuesday, October 27, 2026

What you are learning: Value means lightness or darkness. A form looks three-dimensional when the light side, middle value, core shadow, and cast shadow agree with one light direction. Even shading and clean edges make the form easier to read.

Fully worked model

Place an arrow showing light from the upper left. Keep the upper-left of a sphere light, darken the lower-right core shadow, and place the cast shadow down and right.

Source and materials

Materials: pencil, eraser, ruler, and one sheet of plain paper. Use pressure changes only; no blending tool is required.
