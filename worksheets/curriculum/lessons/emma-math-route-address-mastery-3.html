Coordinate Plane — Route to Address

What you are learning

An ordered pair is an address: (x, y). Move left/right for x first. Move down/up for y second.

Direction ledger

MoveChangesSign
Right / Eastx+
Left / Westx−
Up / Northy+
Down / Southy−

Worked example: Start at (−2, 1). Move 5 east and 3 south.

x: −2 + 5 = 3.   y: 1 − 3 = −2.

New address: (3, −2).

Check your answer by reading it backward: (3, −2) means 3 right and 2 down from the origin.
