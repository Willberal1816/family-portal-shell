Make a Measured Vegetable Wrap

Emma · Cooking/Life Skills · Friday, November 20 · 50-minute learning block

What you are learning: Prepare one real no-heat vegetable-and-cheese wrap while accurately measuring its ingredients and cleaning the workspace.

Learn First

This is a real-food measuring repair, not a new advanced culinary lab.

Work with an adult who confirms the ingredients, allergies and food-safe tools first.

Wash your hands, wash ready-to-eat produce and work with clean utensils.

A wrap needs one tortilla, one half-cup shredded lettuce, one quarter-cup shredded cheese and two tablespoons of plain yogurt spread.

For two wraps, double EACH ingredient before dividing the mixture evenly.

Use a dry measuring cup for shredded ingredients and a spoon measure for yogurt.

Keep the ingredients clean, wrap safely, taste only if the adult approves, and clean up.

Important words: A half-cup equals two quarter-cups. A quarter-cup is half of a half-cup. Measure level when using a dry cup, and use two actual tablespoon measures for the spread.

Worked models — read before independent work

Model 1: One wrap uses one tortilla, a half-cup lettuce, a quarter-cup cheese and two tablespoons yogurt. For TWO wraps, that becomes two tortillas, one full cup lettuce, one half-cup cheese and four tablespoons yogurt.
Model 2: To make a half-cup lettuce without a half-cup tool, fill a clean quarter-cup measuring cup TWO times and combine the amounts. A quarter-cup cheese uses ONE level quarter-cup; do not mistake it for a full cup.
Common mistake: Do not use raw meat, unknown allergens or spoiled food. An adult must approve any substitutions and handle unsafe or sharp tasks. If ingredients are missing, do not invent that the recipe was cooked: tell the adult before starting.

Independent Work — all six questions

Every assigned question is shown on this screen and included on your four-page printable. Write on your worksheet, not on the Learn First page.

Question 1. Before touching ingredients, record the adult-approved ingredient list, available clean measuring tools, any substitutions and the exact clean work area. If supplies are missing, write 'not prepared' and notify the adult rather than pretending the food was made.

Question 2. Show TWO methods to measure one half-cup lettuce: one using a half-cup tool and one using a quarter-cup tool. Draw or label the actual measures and record what you truly poured.

Question 3. With adult approval, prepare ONE real wrap using the source recipe. Record the measured amount of EACH ingredient, how you verified its level, and any spill or adjustment. Do not record a step as completed unless you performed it.

Question 4. Check the two-wrap total recipe. If a helper pours one full cup cheese instead of the intended half-cup, name the extra amount and describe a safe correction before mixing. Then describe how the two wraps can share the prepared ingredients fairly.

Question 5. After assembly, record a real taste/texture observation if a safe tasting was approved; otherwise record why no tasting occurred. Explain what you changed in placement or quantity to keep the filling from spilling.

Question 6. Write a SIX-sentence lab record stating what you actually prepared, exact measures, one check for accurate cups, food-handling safety, the real cleanup completed and one specific change you would make next time. Include actual task and cleanup minutes if measured; do not invent times.
