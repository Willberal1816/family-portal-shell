Learn First

  1. Sort first. For six values, median is the average of positions 3 and 4.
  2. Mean is total ÷ number of values. After removing a value, subtract it from the total and divide by the new count.
  3. A recommendation must name the statistic, give the before-and-after values, and explain why the high day matters.

Worked examples

Example A: 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 30 has total 90, mean 15, and median (12+14)÷2 = 13.
Example B: Remove 30. The new total is 60 and there are 5 values, so mean = 60÷5 = 12. The remaining median is 12.
