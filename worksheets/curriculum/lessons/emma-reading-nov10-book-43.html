Independent Reading: Trace a Decision

Emma · Reading · Tuesday, November 10, 2026 · 45 minutes

What you will learn: Read for 25 minutes and explain one character decision using specific evidence from the pages you read.
READ FOR 25 MINUTES BEFORE completing the response. Your book is the main source. You do not need to stop after a short passage. This is real reading time, not worksheet time.

Learn First

  1. Read your current book for about 25 ACTIVE minutes, continuing from where you ended on November 9. Record the pages you actually read.
  2. A character's decision is an action they CHOOSE. An author may state a reason directly, or you may infer a reasonable motive using a clue.
  3. Separate the decision and result: what was tried and what actually changed? If a result is not shown yet, it is not confirmed.
  4. After reading, complete the concise response. Use the exact page number for at least one clue, and write a short summary in your own words.

Two fully explained models

Model: A character checks a map before leaving. The action is 'checked the map.' A reasonable reason is to avoid getting lost. The book would need additional evidence to prove the reason.
Model summary: 'A child heard an unusual noise, chose to ask for help, and found an adult nearby. The source does not yet reveal what made the noise.' This preserves uncertainty.
Check this mistake: A guess such as 'the noise was a bear' is not confirmed just because a character was frightened. Cite a page detail and say what you really know.

Do the actual lesson
