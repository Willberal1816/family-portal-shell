Learn First

A line graph shows a measurement changing over time. Put time on the horizontal axis, temperature (°F) on the vertical axis, plot EACH measured pair, and connect adjacent measured points with straight segments. A connecting line is a visual trend, not an actual thermometer reading between observations. The data do not prove what caused the change.

Worked example

If a station reads 40°F at 8 AM and 48°F at 10 AM, plot (8 AM, 40°F) and (10 AM, 48°F) and connect them. The measured increase is 8°F. You cannot claim a directly measured 9 AM temperature without a 9 AM observation.
