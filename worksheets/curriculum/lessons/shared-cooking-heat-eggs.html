Heat Control: Scrambled Eggs & Toast

What you are learning: Use low-to-medium heat, cook eggs safely, judge texture and doneness, prepare toast, and reset the station.

Watch First

Watch the assigned professional demonstration before turning on the stove. Notice safe pan placement, gentle stirring, heat adjustment, and when the eggs come off the heat.

Safety and setup

  • Adult nearby for stove work.
  • Wash hands before and after handling raw egg; keep shell and raw egg away from ready-to-eat food.
  • Turn pan handles inward. Use a dry oven mitt if a handle becomes hot.
  • Cook until no liquid egg remains; do not taste raw egg.
Model sequence: Gather → crack into bowl → inspect for shell → whisk → preheat pan on low-medium → add butter → add eggs → push/fold slowly → lower heat if browning → remove when softly set → toast and plate → turn off stove → clean.
Heat evidence: Slow soft curds mean controlled heat. Fast browning, sticking, or rubbery eggs mean the pan was too hot or the eggs cooked too long.
Knife-safety retrieval: fingertips curled, guide with knuckles, make a flat side when cutting, and use one target size.
