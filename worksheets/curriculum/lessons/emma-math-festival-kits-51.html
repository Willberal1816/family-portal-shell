Festival Kits: Count What Repeats

Emma · Math · Friday, November 20 · 50-minute learning block

What you are learning: Use a labeled drawing, repeated groups, and a once-only extra to solve a new assembly task.

Learn First

An item INSIDE each kit repeats for every kit.

An item OUTSIDE all kits is added just once to the entire order.

First mark the groups, then write the expression, then check your total in units.

A statement about the total must name the thing counted.

Important words: Group size means the number of items in one identical kit. Once-only extra means loose items added to the whole order, not placed in every kit.

Worked models — read before independent work

Model 1: A crew makes four bags of three name tags and leaves two spare tags on the desk. The four bags contain 4 × 3 = 12 tags; the two loose spares make 12 + 2 = 14 tags.
Model 2: If two stickers are put INSIDE every one of four bags of three tags, each bag has five items: 4 × (3 + 2) = 20 items. Four groups of five differs from four groups of three plus two loose items.
Common mistake: A drawing that places loose extras inside every kit contradicts a correct once-only expression. Check the words 'inside each' and 'outside all' before calculating.

Independent Work — all six questions

Every assigned question is shown on this screen and included on your four-page printable. Write on your worksheet, not on the Learn First page.

Question 1. Order A: Draw five kit rectangles and put four tag marks INSIDE each. Put the three spare-sticker marks OUTSIDE every kit. Write a multiplication-plus-addition expression and the total with units.

Question 2. Order B: Show the two pencils and one card inside EACH of four kits; put the two ribbons outside all kits. Give a repeated-addition check, a multiplication expression and the combined total. Explain in ONE complete sentence what repeats.

Question 3. A helper writes 5 × (4 + 3) = 35 for Order A. Identify exactly what the helper wrongly repeated. Write the correct equation, state the actual total, and explain in TWO sentences how the diagram proves it.

Question 4. Order D has the stickers INSIDE each kit. Draw three labeled kits, write the parenthesized expression and compare its 18 items with the result from adding only two stickers outside. State the numerical difference and why the stories differ.

Question 5. For Order C, create a three-column work table labeled group count, each group and once-only extras. Fill it from the source, calculate the exact total, then write TWO sentences explaining how the table and your drawing agree.

Question 6. Invent TWO different festival-kit stories using four kits, three basic items and two additional items: one story where two are outside all kits, the other where two are inside EACH kit. Draw both, write both equations and totals, and explain in FIVE complete sentences which description changes the answer.
