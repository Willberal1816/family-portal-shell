Same units before average

12 inches = 1 foot. Convert 2 ft 5 in into 2 × 12 + 5 = 29 in. For 29, 31, 33 inches, average (29 + 31 + 33) ÷ 3 = 31 in = 2 ft 7 in.

A fresh fair test

A toy car starts from two track heights. Low-height distances are 29, 31, 33 in. High-height distances are 40, 42, 44 in. Both have three trials with the same car, track, floor, and release method. The only changed variable is start height.

A conclusion must cite the two means

Low average = 31 in; high average = 42 in; difference = 11 in. The higher start gave the car more gravitational potential energy, which changed to motion energy downhill. When it stops, energy transfers to thermal energy via friction and a little sound.

Change a truly different variable next

A follow-up could compare two floor surfaces while holding car, start height, track, and measuring method constant. Repeating the height change is not a different-variable follow-up.
