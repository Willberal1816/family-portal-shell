Desk Organizer Prototype: Measure, Build, Test, Revise

Emma · Project · Wednesday, October 28, 2026 · 60 minutes

What you are learning: Use measurements and design constraints to build a stable recycled-paper organizer, test it with real school supplies, and document a meaningful revision.

Learn First

A prototype is a testable first version. Translate each constraint into a measurable check before building.

Change one feature after the first test so the second test shows whether the revision helped.

Fully worked model

Constraint: hold 6 pencils upright in a 12 cm by 12 cm footprint. Trial 1 tips with 4 pencils. Revision: widen the base flap by 2 cm. Trial 2 holds 6 pencils for 30 seconds.

Source and materials

Materials: recycled cereal-box cardboard or two sheets of heavy paper, ruler, pencil, tape, and 6 pencils or markers. No cutting tool is required; fold and tape. Constraints: footprint no larger than 12 cm by 12 cm; hold 6 items; stay upright for 30 seconds; include one labeled pocket.
