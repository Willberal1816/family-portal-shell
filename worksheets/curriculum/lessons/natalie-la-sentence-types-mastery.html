2. The clause test

Before choosing S, CD, or CX, put a mental box around every group of words that has a subject and a verb.

I packed the cooler. → can stand alone → independent clause.
because the storm was coming → leaves the reader waiting → dependent clause.

Simple sentence: one independent clause. It can still have a compound subject or compound verb.

Compound sentence: two independent clauses joined correctly.

Complex sentence: one independent clause plus at least one dependent clause.

3. Worked examples

Example A — not compound

My sister and I packed the cooler.

There is only one complete clause. Two subjects do not create two clauses. This is simple.

Example B — compound

The clouds grew dark, so we hurried to the car.

“The clouds grew dark” can stand alone. “We hurried to the car” can stand alone. Two independent clauses + comma + so = compound.

Example C — complex

Although the trail was muddy, we kept walking.

“Although the trail was muddy” cannot stand alone. “We kept walking” can. Dependent + independent = complex.

4. Guided sorting — mark whole clauses first

  1. Because the bus was late, I called home.
    CheckDependent: “Because the bus was late.” Independent: “I called home.” Complex.
  2. The dog barked and scratched at the door.
    CheckOne subject with two verbs; one independent clause; simple.
  3. I finished the puzzle, but my brother kept working.
    CheckTwo independent clauses; compound.
  4. When the timer rang, the cookies were ready.
    CheckDependent + independent; complex.
  5. Maria washed the dishes, and she wiped the counter.
    CheckTwo independent clauses joined with comma + and; compound.

5. Repair the punctuation

  1. The rain stopped we went outside. Write two correct repairs: one with a period and one with a joining word.
  2. Because I finished early I read my book. Add the punctuation that clearly separates the opening dependent clause.
  3. I was tired, I finished the chapter. Explain why the comma alone is not enough and repair the sentence.
Check after you repair all three
  1. The rain stopped. We went outside. / The rain stopped, so we went outside.
  2. Because I finished early, I read my book.
  3. It is a comma splice; use a period or comma + conjunction.

6. Mistake detective

A student labels “My mom cooked dinner and washed the pans” as compound because it has two verbs. Explain the mistake in one sentence.

A student underlines only “clouds” when asked to underline a clause. Explain what should be underlined instead.

Check

The first sentence has one subject doing two actions, so it is one independent clause. A clause mark must include the whole subject-and-verb thought, not one word.
