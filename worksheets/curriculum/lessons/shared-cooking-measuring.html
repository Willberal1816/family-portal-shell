Kitchen Measuring Basics

Cooking / Life Skills · Learn First
What you are learning: Use common measuring cups and spoons accurately.

Key ideas

  • Read the label on the measuring tool before filling it.
  • Set a liquid measuring cup on a flat surface and check the level at eye height.
  • For a dry ingredient, fill the correct cup and level the top unless the recipe says otherwise.

Example

Example: Two 1/2-cup measures make 1 cup. Three teaspoons make 1 tablespoon.
