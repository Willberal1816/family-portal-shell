Goal: separate the one changed variable from true controls in a simple fair test. Plan about 45 minutes. Read this teaching page before the Independent Work section.

Learn First

The independent variable is the one planned change. Controls are conditions kept the same. The dependent variable is what is measured.

Worked model

Question: Does wrapping material affect melt time? IV: wrapping material. DV: minutes until the ice cube melts. Controls: same cube size, room, plate, start time, and timer.

Today's fresh source or situation

Compare a paper towel wrap with an aluminum foil wrap. Measure minutes until each same-size ice cube melts.
