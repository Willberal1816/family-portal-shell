Ramp Distance Trial: Control, Measure, Conclude

Natalie · Science · Wednesday, October 28, 2026 · 40 minutes

What you are learning: Compare repeated measurements, keep the changed variable separate from controls, and make a conclusion that does not claim more than the trial shows.

Learn First

Change one variable at a time. Here, ramp height changes; the same ball, start point, surface, and release method are controls.

Repeated trials show variation. Compare averages, but do not claim the height alone caused every possible result without more testing.

Fully worked model

Low-ramp distances 84, 88, 86 cm average to 86 cm. High-ramp distances 122, 118, 120 cm average to 120 cm. In these trials, the high ramp averaged 34 cm farther.
