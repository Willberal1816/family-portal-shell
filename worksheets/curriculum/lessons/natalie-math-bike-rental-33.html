Learn First

For a plan C = start fee + hourly rate × hours, the starting fee is paid once and the hourly rate repeats. Two plans tie when their costs are equal at the SAME number of hours. Solve the equation, then substitute the answer into both original rules.

Always label money in dollars and time in hours. A table can check your algebra.

Worked example

Plan X: $8 + $3h; Plan Y: $14 + $2h. Solve 8 + 3h = 14 + 2h, so h = 6. Check X = 8 + 18 = $26 and Y = 14 + 12 = $26. The point (6 hours, $26) belongs to both plans.
