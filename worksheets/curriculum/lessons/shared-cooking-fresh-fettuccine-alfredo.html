Cooking Lab 3: Fresh Fettuccine & Homemade Alfredo

Today’s challenge: each cook makes one small egg-pasta dough. After a full 3-hour covered rest, the team rolls, cuts, cooks, and sauces fresh fettuccine. This is one assignment completed in two sessions.

Today’s split schedule

  • Part 1 — 10:00–10:30: watch the pasta demonstration, measure, mix, and knead both doughs; cover by 10:30.
  • Rest — 10:30–1:30: dough rests covered for three hours while Math, Language Arts, and Health are completed.
  • Part 2 — 1:30–2:30: watch the Alfredo demonstration, roll, cut, cook, sauce, taste, record evidence, and clean.
  • Reading — 2:35: begins after Cooking cleanup.

Safety gates

  • An adult must be nearby for raw egg cleanup, boiling water, draining, and stovetop work.
  • Turn pot handles inward and keep the floor and work area dry.
  • Do not taste raw egg dough. Wash hands, tools, and the counter after Part 1.
  • Use a bench scraper or dinner knife for cutting when possible.
  • Reserve pasta water with a heat-safe cup before draining; an adult assists with the hot pot.

Ingredients

For each student’s doughShared Alfredo
100 g all-purpose flour (about ¾ cup + 1 Tbsp)
1 large egg
Pinch of salt
Up to 1 tsp water only if needed		 4 Tbsp butter
1 cup very finely grated Parmesan
½–¾ cup reserved hot pasta water
Black pepper
Small pinch salt only if needed
Optional: up to ½ cup heavy cream

Equipment

Scale or measuring cups, two bowls or clean work areas, forks, bench scraper, rolling pin or pasta machine, wrap or covered containers, large pot, tongs, colander, heat-safe cup, grater, large warm bowl or skillet, and one printed lab record per student.

Part 1 — make and rest the dough (10:00–10:30)

1. Watch and set up — about 7 minutes

Watch the pasta video in the portal. Each student names one dough technique on her lab record. Wash hands and label two covered resting containers.

2. Measure and mix — about 8 minutes

  1. Put 100 g flour on the work surface or in a bowl and make a wide well.
  2. Add one egg and a pinch of salt.
  3. Beat the egg with a fork, drawing in a little flour at a time so the wall does not break.
  4. When the mixture becomes thick, use the bench scraper and hands to gather it into one dough.

3. Knead — 8–10 minutes

  1. Press the dough away with the heel of the hand, fold it back, rotate, and repeat.
  2. If dry after several minutes, add water only a few drops at a time. If sticky, dust with the smallest useful amount of flour.
  3. Stop when the surface is smoother and the dough springs back slowly when pressed.

4. Cover, time, and clean

  1. Wrap or cover each labeled dough so no air reaches it.
  2. Record the rest start time. The dough must rest at least three hours.
  3. Wash hands; sanitize the raw-egg surface and tools before leaving Part 1.

Part 2 — roll, cut, cook, and sauce (1:30–2:30)

5. Watch sauce video and prepare

  1. Watch the Alfredo video in the portal and record one sauce technique.
  2. Finely grate Parmesan. Put butter in the large warm bowl or skillet. Start a pot of lightly salted water.

6. Roll and cut

  1. Flatten one rested dough; keep the other covered.
  2. Roll from the center outward, turning often, until thin enough to see a shadow through it.
  3. Dust very lightly, fold loosely, cut even fettuccine strips, and separate them. Repeat with the second dough.

7. Boil safely

  1. When the water is at a full boil, add the fresh pasta and stir gently.
  2. Cook about 2–4 minutes. Taste one strand safely; it should be tender with a slight bite and no raw center.
  3. Before draining, reserve ¾ cup pasta water in a heat-safe cup.

8. Make Alfredo off strong heat

  1. Move hot pasta to the butter and toss until coated.
  2. Add Parmesan gradually while tossing continuously.
  3. Add hot pasta water one tablespoon at a time until the sauce is glossy and coats the noodles.
  4. If clumpy or greasy, remove from heat and add a little warm pasta water while tossing. If thin, keep tossing and add a small amount of Parmesan.
  5. Serve immediately; then finish the lab record and clean the kitchen.
