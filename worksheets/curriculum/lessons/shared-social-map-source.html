Maps as Historical Evidence

What this lesson teaches: treat a map as a source made for a purpose. Maps can reveal location, routes, boundaries, distance, and change, but they also select and omit information. All required map analysis is on the printable.

Definitions

Historical map: a map created in or about an earlier time.

Legend/key: explains symbols and colors.

Scale: connects map distance to real distance.

Context: who made the map, when, for whom, and why.

Teaching map example

Town ATown Briver

This simplified map could support questions about route direction, the river crossing, and relative locations. It cannot by itself tell why people traveled, how difficult the trip was, or what individuals experienced.

Old map versus modern map

Comparing maps from two dates can show changes in roads, boundaries, settlement, land use, or place names. Differences may reflect real change, but they can also come from different map purposes or levels of detail.

Creator and purpose matter

A military map, tourist map, land-sale map, and school atlas may all show the same region differently because each mapmaker chooses information for a different audience and purpose.
