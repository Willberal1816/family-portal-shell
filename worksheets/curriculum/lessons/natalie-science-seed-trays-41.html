Seed Trays: Compare Repeated Growth and Preserve Controls

Natalie · Science · Friday, October 30, 2026 · 45 minutes

What you will be able to do: Average repeated trials, state a bounded comparison, and design a next test that preserves controls.

Learn First

  1. Average = total of trials ÷ number of trials.
  2. Say “greater in these trials,” not “always better.”
  3. A next test must name the new changed variable and explicitly preserve controls.

Worked examples

Heights 12, 15, 18 mm total 45 mm; average = 15 mm.
If another average is 21 mm, the difference is 6 mm. A fair next test keeps seed type, soil, water, light time, and measuring day the same.

Class source

Source/data for this class: Practice data after 7 days: Tray A under 8 hours of light grew 14, 16, and 15 mm. Tray B under 10 hours of light grew 20, 19, and 21 mm. Same seed type, soil, water, tray size, temperature, and measuring day.
