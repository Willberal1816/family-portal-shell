Build a Strong Paragraph

Language Arts · Learn First
What you are learning: Develop one focused idea with specific support and a meaningful ending.

Key ideas

  • A topic sentence tells the paragraph’s main idea.
  • Supporting sentences should add examples, reasons, facts, or details—not just repeat the topic.
  • A concluding sentence should leave the reader with a final thought about the same idea.

Example

Example: If the topic is why a garden is useful, strong support might explain food, pollinators, and learning rather than repeating “gardens are useful.”
