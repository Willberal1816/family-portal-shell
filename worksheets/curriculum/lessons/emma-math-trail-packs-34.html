Learn First

Each group gets exactly the same repeated items. Draw one box per group and put the precise number of each item inside it. Anything issued just once stays outside. Count objects before calculating.

Worked example

Two teams each receive 1 lantern and 2 batteries. A leader carries 3 extra flags once. Draw TWO boxes, each with 1 lantern and 2 batteries, then 3 flags outside. The expression is 2 × (1 + 2) + 3 = 9 objects.

Today's information

A hiking center prepares 3 trail packs. EACH pack contains exactly 2 whistles and 4 bandages. A guide carries 5 trail stickers only once, outside the packs. Another group makes 2 nature kits with 3 pencils and 1 notebook in EACH kit, plus 2 spare clips once.
