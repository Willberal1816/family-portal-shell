Garden Bulletin: Complete Sentences and Semicolons

Emma · Language Arts · Monday, October 26, 2026

What you are learning: Repair because-fragments and join two complete related sentences with a semicolon and a lowercase following word.

Learn First

A sentence must express a complete thought. Because the ground was wet. is a fragment: it tells a reason but does not tell what happened. Repair it by adding a complete main idea: The tables moved inside because the ground was wet.

A semicolon (;) can connect TWO related complete sentences: The rain stopped; the workers opened the path. Each half must make sense on its own. The word after a semicolon normally starts with a lowercase letter unless it is a proper name or the word I.

When writing from a notice, use only facts actually stated. Do not turn a possible explanation into a proven cause.

Fully worked example or model

Fragment: Because the wind was strong. Complete: The flags came down because the wind was strong. Semicolon: The wind slowed; the flags went back up.

  1. Rewrite this fragment as ONE complete sentence using only the bulletin: Because the mulch was wet after rain.
  2. Fix capitalization and punctuation: The path remained open; Visitors could still walk through the garden.
  3. Join these TWO complete sentences with a semicolon, keeping the facts: The art tables moved inside. The walking path stayed open.

The garden gate opens at 9 a.m. Volunteers found wet mulch after overnight rain. Museum workers moved the outdoor art tables inside. The walking path remained open. The public craft demonstration starts at 11 a.m. in the east room.

  1. Explain why Because the public craft demonstration starts at 11 a.m. is NOT a complete sentence. Then repair it without inventing a new fact.
  2. Write TWO complete factual sentences about the bulletin, using a semicolon between them. Check that each side could stand alone.
  3. A child writes The workers moved the tables because the path was closed. Identify the unsupported claim and replace it with a sentence supported by the bulletin.
