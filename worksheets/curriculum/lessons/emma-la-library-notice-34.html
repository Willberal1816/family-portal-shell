Learn First

A complete sentence has a subject and a verb and expresses a full thought. A comma alone cannot join two complete sentences. Use a period, semicolon, or a comma plus a joining word such as AND. A singular subject such as 'the list' takes IS, not ARE.

Worked example

Incorrect: 'The doors opened, the visitors entered.' Correct: 'The doors opened, and the visitors entered.' Incorrect: 'The schedule are posted.' Correct: 'The schedule is posted.' Keep both original ideas.
