Trail Workshop: Check Every Unit

Natalie · Math · Saturday November 21, 2026 · 50 minutes

What you are learning: Convert feet, inches and yards; compare only common units and check remainders.

Learn First

One foot = 12 inches; one yard = 3 feet = 36 inches. Remainder inches must be below 12.

Two worked models

Model 1: A 3-foot 8-inch sign is 3 x 12 + 8 = 44 inches; reverse-check 36 + 8 = 44.
Model 2: A 2-yard 2-foot rope is 2 x 36 + 2 x 12 = 96 inches, or 8 x 12 = 96.
Check for this mistake: Before subtracting, convert both quantities to one unit.

Source and facts

  • Small sign: 3 feet 8 inches. Backing board: 70 inches.
  • Rope: 2 yards 2 feet. Marker strips: 19 inches and 28 inches.
  • Two support rails: 2 feet 7 inches EACH. The fence perimeter is NOT recorded.

Read the models, then complete your six questions using your source. Write only what the source supports.

All six independent-work questions

These exact questions are visible on screen and appear in the matching four-page PDF. Write on paper, scan all pages and finish this class in the School Portal.

Question 1. Convert the small sign to inches with a multiply-add equation, labeled unit and reverse-check.

Question 2. Convert the board to feet and leftover inches. Compare it with the sign in inches, showing the correct subtraction and check.

Question 3. Convert the rope to feet AND inches by two different paths. Explain why the record cannot prove there is enough for the fence.

Question 4. Convert ONE support rail to inches and BOTH combined. Give the combined feet and remaining inches, then check.

Question 5. Add both marker strips end to end in inches and feet-plus-inches. A helper writes 3 feet 11 inches; diagnose and fix the error.

Question 6. Write a five-sentence workshop note with sign, board, rope, a supported comparison and the exact missing fence measurement.
