How to Read a Map

Geography · Learn First
What you are learning: Use map tools to understand where places are and how they relate to each other.
School word: legend = the part of a map that explains its symbols

Key ideas

  • A map key or legend explains what symbols mean.
  • A compass rose shows directions such as north, south, east, and west.
  • Use landmarks, symbols, and direction words together instead of guessing from position alone.

Example

Example: If the library symbol is east of the park, you would move right on a north-up map from the park to the library.
