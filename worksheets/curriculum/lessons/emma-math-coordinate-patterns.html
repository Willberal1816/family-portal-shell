Coordinate Patterns

Math · Learn First
What you are learning: Notice a rule in a table of x and y values and use it to predict points.

Key ideas

  • Look at how x changes from row to row.
  • Look at how y changes at the same time.
  • Describe the rule in words before predicting the next pair.

Example

Example: (1,2), (2,4), (3,6) follows “y is twice x,” so when x=4, y=8.
