Harbor Records: Exact Place, Date, Supported Claim

Emma · Social Studies · Friday, October 30, 2026 · 45 minutes

What you will be able to do: Attach exact place and date to facts, revise unsupported claims, and ask what a new source must answer.

Learn First

  1. A usable citation names the source, place, and date with the fact.
  2. Sequence does not automatically prove a cause or a prevented accident.
  3. A comparison should state what each source shows, one limit, and one precise question for a new source.

Worked examples

Supported: “The East Dock ledger dated May 6, 1915 records 22 crates unloaded.”
Unsupported: “The rule prevented accidents” when neither source reports any accident or prevention result.

Class source

Source/data for this class: Source A — East Dock cargo ledger, May 6, 1915: 22 fruit crates and 14 tool boxes unloaded. Source B — Harbor Gazette, May 8, 1915: workers were told to stack heavy boxes on the lower shelf. The article does not report any accident count or outcome.

Directions and timing
