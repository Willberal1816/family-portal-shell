Learn First

Heat energy moves from warmer objects toward cooler surroundings. A towel or oven mitt can slow that transfer; it does not make heat disappear. °F is a normal temperature unit in our home. A temperature drop is the starting temperature minus the later temperature.

Safety: this is a paper-only analysis. Do not heat water, touch hot cookware, or conduct a heat experiment without an adult.

Worked example

Example: a covered cup begins at 110°F and later reads 98°F. Its drop is 110 − 98 = 12°F. That describes the change in the provided readings; it does not prove all covered cups always behave that way.
