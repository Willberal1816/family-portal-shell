Goal: use two different historical records to order events, test a cause claim, and state each source's limit. Plan about 50 minutes. Read this teaching page before the Independent Work section.

Learn First

A dated record can establish sequence. A weather report can support a condition. Sequence alone does not prove cause.

Worked model

Toll record: boat entered at 2:10. Weather report: heavy rain began at 1:45. These sources support that rain began first. They do not prove rain caused every delay.

Today's fresh source or situation
