Different Points of View

Social Studies · Learn First
What you are learning: Understand why people can describe the same event differently.
School word: perspective = the point of view from which someone sees or explains something

Key ideas

  • People notice different details depending on where they were and what they experienced.
  • A person’s role, goals, background, and access to information can shape an account.
  • Different accounts do not automatically mean someone is lying.

Example

Example: A firefighter and a shop owner may describe the same city fire differently because they saw different parts and had different responsibilities.
