Record, Promotion, Sequence—not Cause

Natalie · Language Arts · Thursday, October 15, 2026 · Learn First

Goal: classify record, survey, and promotional sources and state exact chronology without turning sequence into causation. Read this teaching page before opening the separate Independent Work printable.

Worked model

Record dated June 2: filter replaced. Advertisement ran June 4–10: “Cleaner water!” The dates show replacement before the ad. They do not prove the replacement caused later opinions; the ad is promotional evidence, not a comparison test.

Reliable method

  1. Classify each source by what it was created to do.
  2. Record dates and facts exactly.
  3. Use “before/after” for chronology.
  4. Use “may” only when evidence supports a possibility; explicitly say what causation or population claim is not proven.

Before independent work
