Depot Records: Exact Place, Exact Dates, Clear Limits

Natalie · Social Studies · Friday, October 30, 2026 · 45 minutes

What you will be able to do: Identify each record precisely, combine dated facts, and write a complete place-and-time-bounded report.

Learn First

  1. Every fact needs its record, building or town, and date.
  2. Two records can show a change across their dates, but not what happened everywhere or always.
  3. A bounded report states two dated facts and an explicit limit about other places or dates.

Worked examples

Exact: “Record A is the Pineville Depot ledger dated June 3, 1938.”
Bounded: “These records show Pineville Depot on June 3 and June 4 only; they do not describe other depots or dates.”

Class source

Source/data for this class: Record A — Pineville Depot passenger ledger, June 3, 1938: 31 morning passengers and 17 afternoon passengers. Record B — Pineville Depot passenger ledger, June 4, 1938: 28 morning passengers and 25 afternoon passengers.

Directions and timing

Read Learn First and both examples (15 minutes). Complete all six numbered items on the TWO printable pages (25–30 minutes). Check your work, scan BOTH pages with the Tracking number visible, then Finish this ONE class (5 minutes). Materials: printed pages and pencil only.
