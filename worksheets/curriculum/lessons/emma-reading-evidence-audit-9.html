Reading Evidence Audit

Goal: copy an exact clue, use a context clue to define a word, compare both sides, and finish exactly five synthesis sentences.

Fresh passage

At the library, Juniper the shy dog stopped at the doorway. Leo knelt sideways instead of reaching over her head. Juniper’s stiff tail loosened, and she stepped toward him. “She is cautious, not unfriendly,” the librarian whispered. By closing time, Juniper rested beside Leo’s chair.

Worked examples

Exact clue: “Juniper’s stiff tail loosened.” This shows her fear is decreasing.

Context word: cautious means careful or hesitant. The doorway pause and gradual approach support that meaning.

Five-sentence map

  1. Claim about change.
  2. Specific detail one.
  3. Specific detail two.
  4. Explain why one detail matters.
  5. Predict the next effect using evidence.

Count sentences by numbering them 1–5. A line is not automatically a sentence.
