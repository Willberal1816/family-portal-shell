Learn First

A schedule tells when a service was planned. A tally tells what was counted. A weather note describes conditions. Even when two events happen together, the records alone may not prove one caused the other.

Keep each date paired with its correct number. A claim about one ferry terminal cannot automatically describe every terminal.

Worked example

Example: The June 2 tally lists 31 passengers and June 3 lists 44. We may say the recorded count increased by 13. We cannot conclude the weather caused the change without stronger evidence.
