Coordinate Clues — Read, Plot & Explain

What you are learning

An ordered pair tells where a point belongs on a coordinate plane. Use the pair in the same order every time: x first, then y. Use the signs to predict the direction before you plot.

Use the coordinate card

x-coordinateMove left or right. Positive = right. Negative = left.
y-coordinateMove up or down. Positive = up. Negative = down.
Final checkDoes the point land in the quadrant or on the axis that the signs predict?

Worked model 1 — read a pair

Point A is (−4, 3).

x = −4 → move left 4. y = 3 → move up 3. Left + up places A in Quadrant II.

Check: a Quadrant II point must have negative x and positive y. The signs match.

Worked model 2 — axis points

Point B is (0, −5).

x = 0 means do not move left or right. y = −5 means move down 5. B lands on the y-axis, not in a quadrant.

If either coordinate is 0, stop and check whether the point belongs on an axis.

Worked model 3 — catch a swap

A point should be (−2, 6), but it is plotted at (6, −2).

The intended pair says left 2, up 6 → Quadrant II. The plotted pair says right 6, down 2 → Quadrant IV. The x and y values were swapped.

Say the pair aloud before plotting: “x: left 2; y: up 6.”

Worked model 4 — read a movement clue

A robot starts at (0,0), moves right 5, then down 3.

Right 5 gives x = 5. Down 3 gives y = −3. Its ordered pair is (5, −3), in Quadrant IV.

Three checks before you finish

  1. Did I move x first?
  2. Did I obey each positive or negative sign?
  3. Do the signs match the quadrant or axis where I ended?
