Bus Route Update: Exact Timing and Final Outcome

Natalie · Reading · Tuesday, October 27, 2026

What you are learning: Timing words anchor events. Separate a report made before a change from an outcome observed after the change. A final outcome states what actually happened at the end, not merely what someone planned.

Fully worked model

“At 7:20 the driver reported a blocked road. At 7:35 the detour began. By 7:50 the bus reached school.” The report, action, and final outcome are different events.

Source and materials

At 7:05 a.m., Route 8 left the depot. At 7:18, the driver reported water across Pine Street. Dispatch checked the road map and called the school. At 7:24, dispatch directed the bus to Oak Avenue. The bus reached school at 7:41, before the 7:45 bell. The next morning, workers reopened Pine Street.
