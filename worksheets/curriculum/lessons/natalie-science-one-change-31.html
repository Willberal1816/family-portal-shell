One Change, Three Trials, One Mean

Natalie · Science · Thursday, October 15, 2026 · Learn First

Goal: identify variables and controls, calculate a mean, and design a safe three-trial fair test. Read this teaching page before opening the separate Independent Work printable.

Worked model

Question: Does ramp height affect block distance? IV: ramp height (4 in vs. 8 in). DV: block distance in inches. Controls: same block, ball, release point, surface, and measuring tool. Trials at 10 in: 3, 4, 5 inches; mean=(3+4+5)÷3=4 inches.

Reliable method

  1. Change exactly one independent variable.
  2. Measure one dependent variable with units.
  3. Keep materials, release, surface, and measurement method fixed.
  4. Run at least three trials per condition; add, divide by the number of trials, compare means, and state only what the data show.
