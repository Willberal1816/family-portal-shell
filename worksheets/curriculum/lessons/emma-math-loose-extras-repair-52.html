Supply Boxes: Inside or Outside?

Emma · Math · Saturday November 21, 2026 · 50 minutes

What you are learning: Distinguish inside-each items from loose once-only extras.

Learn First

Inside EACH repeats in every group; OUTSIDE ALL means add once.

Two worked models

Model 1: Three boxes each hold 4 bookmarks; 2 loose labels: 3 x 4 + 2 = 14.
Model 2: If 2 labels were inside EACH box: 3 x (4 + 2) = 18. Placement changes the result.
Check for this mistake: Draw and label the loose extras outside EVERY group.

Source and facts

  • Record A: 3 reading boxes, 4 bookmarks in EACH, 2 labels OUTSIDE all boxes.
  • Record B: 4 desk trays, 2 pencils and 1 card INSIDE EACH, 3 clips OUTSIDE.
  • Record C: 5 folders, 2 sheets INSIDE EACH, 4 tabs OUTSIDE.

Read the models, then complete your six questions using your source. Write only what the source supports.

All six independent-work questions

These exact questions are visible on screen and appear in the matching four-page PDF. Write on paper, scan all pages and finish this class in the School Portal.

Question 1. Draw Record A with 3 labeled boxes, 4 bookmark marks inside each, and 2 label marks outside. Write and solve its exact expression.

Question 2. Draw Record B. Show its repeated addition, multiplication-plus-addition equation, total and a one-sentence explanation of what repeats.

Question 3. A helper put 2 loose labels in EACH box in Record A and got 18. Correct the picture and equation, then explain in two sentences.

Question 4. Now move the 3 clips in Record B INSIDE EACH tray. Find both original and changed totals; show and explain the numerical difference.

Question 5. Make a table for Record C headed Folders, Sheets per folder, Loose tabs and Total. Use the source to calculate and draw a check.

Question 6. Invent two school-supply stories with 4 bags, 3 cards and 2 ribbons: ribbons once outside or ribbons inside every bag. Draw, calculate and explain both.
