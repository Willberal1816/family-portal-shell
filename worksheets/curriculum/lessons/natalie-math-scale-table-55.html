Scale Legends: Build a Conversion Table

Natalie | Math | Tuesday, November 24, 2026 | 50 minutes

What you are learning: Convert lengths between a scaled drawing and the real world.

Learn First

A scale rule connects drawing units to real units. On our sample map 1 centimeter represents 4 feet; multiply a drawing measurement in centimeters by 4 to find actual feet.

Reverse the conversion by dividing real feet by 4 to get centimeters. Keep your units in a table and use the scale exactly once. A real distance should be larger than its drawing measurement in this example.

Two worked examples

A 6 cm trail represents 24 feet because 6 x 4 = 24.
A 36-foot wall appears 9 centimeters long because 36 / 4 = 9; check 9 x 4 = 36 feet.

Source and materials

MODEL PARK MAP, original data: 1 cm = 4 feet. Gate 2 cm; path 7 cm; garden 5 cm; seating 3 cm. Lengths are straight segments.

All six questions

Question 5. Draw a 4-by-6-cm rectangle and label its two actual dimensions.
