Receipt, Flyer, and Careful Claim

Emma · Language Arts · Thursday, October 15, 2026 · Learn First

Goal: identify what a receipt records, quote exact evidence, name a source-based limitation, and write a bounded two-source claim. Read this teaching page before opening the separate Independent Work printable.

Worked model

Flyer: “Community supper, Oct. 20, 6 p.m.” Receipt: “Oct. 20, 5:42 p.m.; 3 loaves; $12.” Careful claim: Supplies were purchased shortly before the advertised supper. The sources do not prove who attended or whether the bread was served.

Reliable method

  1. Name the source type and its job.
  2. Copy one exact fact from each source.
  3. Say what each source cannot prove.
  4. Join only the facts the sources support; avoid “everyone,” “caused,” or “definitely.”

Before independent work

Explain the goal in your own words. On the printable, answer every numbered part, show the requested evidence or calculation, scan both pages, and then finish class.
