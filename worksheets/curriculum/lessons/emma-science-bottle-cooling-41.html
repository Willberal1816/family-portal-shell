Bottle-Cooling Test: Start Minus End

Emma · Science · Saturday, October 31, 2026 · 45 minutes

What you will be able to do: Calculate two measured decreases, name the changed variable and controls, and write a quantity-based conclusion.

Learn First

  1. A decrease is start − end. Do not subtract in the opposite order.
  2. The changed variable is the one planned difference. Controls are kept the same.
  3. A conclusion reports measured changes, their difference, and a limit such as “in this test.”

Worked examples

If a bottle goes from 31°C to 25°C, the decrease is 31−25 = 6°C.
If two decreases are 6°C and 2°C, their difference is 6−2 = 4°C. Say which decreased more in this test.

Class source

Source/data for this class: Practice test: Two identical bottles started at 30°C and sat for 15 minutes on the same counter. Bottle A had a damp cloth wrap and ended at 24°C. Bottle B had no wrap and ended at 27°C. Same bottle size, water amount, starting temperature, location, thermometer, and time.
