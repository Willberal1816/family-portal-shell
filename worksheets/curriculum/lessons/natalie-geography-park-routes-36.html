Learn First

A map legend tells what symbols mean, and its scale tells how map distances convert to real distances. On this grid, one horizontal or vertical step equals ½ mile. Count route segments, not landmarks. A shortest grid route may have more than one direction order, but its number of horizontal plus vertical steps is fixed.

Two worked examples

Worked example 1. From B1 to D3, travel 2 steps east and 2 south: 4 steps × ½ mile = 2 miles. You may reverse the order and keep the same length.
Worked example 2. A planned route does not prove the road is currently open. The map gives positions and an estimated route distance; it does not report closures, traffic, or travel times.
Common mistake to avoid: Count gaps BETWEEN grid cells. State directions and scale math; do not invent road conditions. Use map addresses as letter then number.
