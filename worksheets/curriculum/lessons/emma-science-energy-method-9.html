Convert feet and inches

3 ft 2 in means 3 × 12 + 2 = 38 in. Always convert all trials to inches before adding them.

Worked average

For 34, 36 and 38 inches, the total is 108; 108 ÷ 3 = 36 in = 3 ft 0 in. Write units in every answer.

Compare two results

If another group has 45, 47 and 49 inches, its average is 47 in. Compare averages: 47 − 36 = 11 in. A conclusion says exactly which group traveled farther and gives BOTH averages.

What happened to energy?

High release stores more gravitational potential energy; downhill it transfers to kinetic energy. Friction and deformation transfer energy mainly to thermal energy, with some sound. The energy does not disappear when the ball stops.
