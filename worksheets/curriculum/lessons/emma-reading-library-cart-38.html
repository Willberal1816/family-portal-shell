The Missing Library Cart: Sequence and Immediate Impact

What you are learning: A strong sequence answer includes every required action in order. An immediate impact is what changed right after an action; a final outcome is what happened at the end. Use exact passage evidence, not a likely guess.

Fully worked model

If Maya checks the return log, calls Room 4, and then finds the cart there, the complete sequence has three actions. The immediate impact of the call is learning the cart's location; the final outcome is recovering it.

Source and materials

At 9:10, Ms. Lee found the return cart missing. Emma checked the checkout desk, but it was not there. She read the delivery log and saw that Room 12 had borrowed the cart. She called Room 12. Mr. Diaz said the cart was beside the science shelves. Emma brought it back by 9:25, and the class returned books on time.
