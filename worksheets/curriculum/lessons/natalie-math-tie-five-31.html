Build the Tie at Five

Natalie · Math · Thursday, October 15, 2026 · Learn First

Goal: prove two linear rules by substitution and construct a pair that ties at a required input. Read this teaching page before opening the separate Independent Work printable.

Worked model

Rules A = 4h + 7 and B = 2h + 17 tie at h = 5. A(5)=4(5)+7=27 and B(5)=2(5)+17=27. The complete substitutions prove the same output.

Reliable method

  1. Keep the start value as the value at h = 0.
  2. Substitute the same h into both rules.
  3. Show multiplication, addition, and the final equality.
  4. To design a tie at 5, choose rates and solve for starts that make both outputs equal.

Before independent work

Explain the goal in your own words. On the printable, answer every numbered part, show the requested evidence or calculation, scan both pages, and then finish class.
