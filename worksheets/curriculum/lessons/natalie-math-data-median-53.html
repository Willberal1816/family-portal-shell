Field Data: Median, Range and Evidence

Natalie | Math | Monday, November 23, 2026 | 50 minutes

Your goal: Find median and range from ordered observations and describe what summaries cannot prove.

Learn First

Sort the data before finding its median, the middle reading. Keep repeated values. Range is maximum minus minimum. A median tells you a central value, not the cause of a change.

Worked models

For 7, 9, 9, 12, 15, the median is 9 (position 3 of 5).
The range of 7, 9, 9, 12, 15 is 15 - 7 = 8. Each 9 remains a separate observation.
