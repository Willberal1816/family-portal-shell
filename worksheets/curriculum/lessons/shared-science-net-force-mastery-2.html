Why Things Keep Moving — Net Force

What you are learning

Net force tells whether velocity changes. Velocity means speed and direction together.

Step 1: Ask whether velocity changes.

If speed and direction stay the same → acceleration is 0 → net force is 0.

If speed or direction changes → acceleration is not 0 → net force is not 0.

Worked example 1: A cart moves east at a steady 2 m/s.

Its speed and direction are not changing. Therefore acceleration = 0 and net force = 0 N. It does not need a forward net force just to keep moving.

Worked example 2: A book rests on a table.

↑ table support   BOOK   ↓ gravity

The upward support force and downward gravity are equal and opposite. Net force = 0 N.

Worked example 3: A sliding box slows down.

Its velocity changes, so net force is not zero. Friction points opposite the motion and causes the change.

Use this order on every printable problem: Did velocity change? → What is the net force? → Name the forces.
