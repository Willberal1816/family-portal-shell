Coordinates in Word Problems

Math · Learn First
What you are learning: Turn movement words into changes in x and y.

Key ideas

  • Right increases x; left decreases x.
  • Up increases y; down decreases y.
  • Start at the given point and change only the coordinate named by the direction.

Example

Example: Start at (3,-2) and move 4 units left. Only x changes: 3-4=-1, so the new point is (-1,-2).
