Start with your book

Read the current book for 25 minutes first. A short new passage on the worksheet gives us practice in quoting a clue and explaining what that clue shows.

Clue and conclusion are different

Example: “The curtain trembled as the backstage door rattled.” Clue = those exact words. Reasonable conclusion = wind may be entering backstage. Not supported = someone deliberately shook the curtain, because the sentence never says that.

Use nearby words to learn vocabulary

If someone “adjusted” a crooked light so it pointed at the stage, adjusted means changed its position. The nearby action “so it pointed at the stage” is the context clue; you must name it.

Five complete sentences

1 what happened; 2 exact clue; 3 explain clue; 4 name cause and result; 5 next effect or remaining question. Count all five before turning in.
