Learn First

Scientists record temperatures with a unit. In our home we use degrees Fahrenheit (°F). To find a change, subtract the earlier reading from the later reading. A negative change means cooling.

A few measurements can describe those specific times but cannot prove what the temperature was all day or what caused a change. Use the supplied data; do not invent new readings.

Worked example

If it was 46°F at 9 AM and 58°F at noon, the temperature rose 58 − 46 = 12°F. A temperature graph labels its vertical axis °F and horizontal axis time.
