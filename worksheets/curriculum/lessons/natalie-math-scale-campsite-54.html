Scale Drawing: Plan a Campsite in Feet

Natalie | Math | Wednesday, November 25, 2026 | 50 minutes

What you are learning: Use customary length, scale, area and perimeter to plan a rectangular site.

Learn First

Warm-up: 1 yard = 3 feet, so 2 yards = 6 feet; check 6 / 3 = 2. A drawing scale of 1 inch for 4 real feet means multiply drawing inches by 4. Rectangle area is length x width in SQUARE feet, while perimeter is length around the edge in feet.

Two worked examples

A 3-by-2-inch drawing means 12-by-8 feet; area 96 square feet, perimeter 40 feet.
A 2-yard clearance is 6 feet, which becomes 1.5 drawing inches; check 1.5 x 4 = 6.

Source and materials

Scale: 1 drawing inch = 4 real feet.
