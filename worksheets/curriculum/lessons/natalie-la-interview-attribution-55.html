Interview Notes: Quote and Paraphrase Fairly

Natalie | Language Arts | Tuesday, November 24, 2026 | 45 minutes

What you are learning: Separate direct quotation, attribution and unverified opinion.

Learn First

A direct quote copies exact spoken words and identifies the speaker. A paraphrase uses your own words but preserves the same meaning and credits the source.

A reported experience or preference is not automatically a universal fact. Keep posted records separate from speakers' recollections, and do not put invented words inside quotation marks.

Two worked examples

Rosa said, 'The first trip leaves at 7:15.' An attributed paraphrase says Rosa reported a 7:15 departure.
Eli says 'I think a later bus will help'; report it as a prediction, not a proven result for all riders.

Source and materials

BUS NOTES: driver Rosa 'The first trip leaves at 7:15.' Rider Leon 'I missed one connection last week.' Rider April 'I would use a later bus.' Posted schedule: route 8 departs at 7:15.
