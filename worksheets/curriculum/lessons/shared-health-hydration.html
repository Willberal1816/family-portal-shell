Hydration & Body Signals

What you are learning: water supports normal body functions, and fluid needs change with activity, heat, food, and individual circumstances. Required planning/reflection is on the printable.

Learn the idea

Your body uses water all day and loses some through normal body functions. Activity and heat can increase that loss. The goal is to have enough fluid for what your body is doing?not to force as much water as possible.

Words you will use

Hydration: having enough water available for normal body functions.

Body signal: a clue such as thirst, dry mouth, fatigue, or urine color that can contribute to a hydration decision. No single clue is perfect by itself.

Fluid loss: water leaving the body through urine, sweat, breathing, and other normal processes.

What water does

JobExample
Temperature regulationSweating helps release heat during activity.
TransportBody fluids help move nutrients and waste products.
Normal cell/body functionWater is part of many chemical and physical processes.

Worked situation — hot active day

On a hot day with outdoor activity, sweat loss is greater than during a cool quiet day. A practical plan is to begin the activity normally hydrated, have fluids available, drink at reasonable opportunities, and pay attention to thirst and how the body feels.

Why “more” is not always “better”

The goal is appropriate hydration, not forcing huge amounts of water. Fluid intake should respond to normal needs and circumstances rather than becoming a contest.
