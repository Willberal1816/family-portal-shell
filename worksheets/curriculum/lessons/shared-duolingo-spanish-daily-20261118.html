Daily Spanish: Your Duolingo Practice

What you are learning

You are building Spanish listening, speaking, reading, and vocabulary through your OWN Duolingo path. Do 60 minutes of ACTIVE practice, not 60 minutes of keeping the app open.

Learn First: What to do

  1. Start today's Spanish class in your School Portal, then open the Duolingo Spanish course linked below in your own family-plan profile.
  2. Work through your next available new lessons. Listen closely to the complete Spanish audio before choosing an answer.
  3. Speak aloud when your course offers a speaking activity. If you make a mistake, replay or repeat the word or sentence and compare it with the example.
  4. Mix new lessons and review as your own path allows. An XP total, streak, or number of questions does NOT replace 60 actual active minutes.
  5. Keep track of how much active Spanish practice you actually do. Pauses, unrelated apps, and breaks do not count toward the 60 minutes.
  6. Return to your SAME open School Portal class. Finish Class when you have really completed the session, and submit the student feedback questions.

Two examples of strong learning

Example 1 — listening: Hear a short Spanish sentence, replay it, and say the words slowly with the audio. If one word is unfamiliar, use the lesson hint and listen again.

Example 2 — a corrected mistake: If your first response is marked incorrect, read the correction, say the correct sentence out loud once, and try the next question using that model.
