Make a Measured Parfait and Scale the Recipe

Natalie · Cooking · Friday, October 30, 2026 · 60 minutes

Today's goal: Make an actual ready-to-eat parfait shown in the video, measure ingredients accurately, and scale the same safe recipe for two people.

Watch the actual demonstration

Exact instructional source: Primrose Schools: Yogurt Granola Parfait — watch the finished snack being made. If playback is blocked, open the original video link and follow the steps shown below. Additional official written safety/recipe reference.

Prepare / parent check: For ONE portion: clean transparent cup, spoon, 1/2 cup chilled yogurt (or safe substitute), 1/2 cup ready-to-eat small fruit, 2 tablespoons granola/cereal; for TWO portions double supplies. 1/2-cup measure, tablespoon, hand soap, clean surface. Adult checks allergens and prepares any fruit requiring a knife; no heat, no raw ingredients.

Learn First — follow in order

  1. The demonstration shows a real yogurt + granola + fruit parfait. Your finished object is one cup with distinct layers, not a worksheet-only plan. Watch the video before building.
  2. Food-safe procedure: wash hands for 20 seconds with soap and water. Adult checks allergies and cold yogurt; use ready-to-eat fruit and clean tools. Keep raw foods elsewhere. Do not use knives or heat.
  3. Base amounts for 1 person: 1/2 cup yogurt, 1/2 cup ready-to-eat fruit, 2 tablespoons granola. Make this single cup first while following the video. Then plan how amounts would double for two persons; prepare a second serving only if family wants it.
  4. Measure by filling the measuring cup level; if a measure is unavailable, use two matching 1/4-cup scoops for one 1/2 cup. Keep the fruit layer visible, finish with granola, and show the complete safe result.
  5. Record observations, scale exact quantities, and complete cleanup. Refrigerate leftovers promptly and let an adult judge whether ingredients were handled safely.

Two examples of the finished work and skills

Finished example: 1/2 cup plain yogurt at bottom, 1/2 cup blueberries in middle, 2 tablespoons granola at top. Three visibly separate layers.
Two-serving scaling: yogurt 1/2 ×2=1 cup; fruit 1/2 ×2=1 cup; granola 2×2=4 tablespoons = 1/4 cup. These are quantities only; do not prepare extra if the food is not needed.
