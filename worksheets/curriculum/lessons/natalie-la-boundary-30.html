Source Roles and Claim Boundaries

Natalie · Language Arts · Wednesday, October 14, 2026 · Learn First

Goal: distinguish record, opinion, and promotion while keeping every claim inside the evidence boundary. Read this teaching page before opening the separate Independent Work printable.

Worked model

A maintenance log says a fountain filter was replaced on May 2. That record supports the replacement event. A survey says 14 of 20 respondents preferred colder water; it supports those respondents’ views, not everyone’s. An advertisement calling the fountain “the city’s best” is promotional language, not proof of quality.

Reliable method

  1. Classify the source’s role.
  2. Quote or paraphrase only what is actually present.
  3. Separate chronology from causation.
  4. Write a synthesis that includes a limitation.

Before independent work
