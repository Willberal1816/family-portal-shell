What Job Does Each Source Do?

Emma · Language Arts · Wednesday, October 14, 2026 · Learn First

Goal: identify the purpose and limits of different sources, then answer in the requested numbered format. Read this teaching page before opening the separate Independent Work printable.

Worked model

A garden log records that 18 seedlings were watered. It can support what happened during that check, but it cannot prove the garden is always well cared for. A flyer invites volunteers; it shows the organizer’s message, not the number who actually attended.

Reliable method

  1. Name the source type.
  2. State what the source directly shows.
  3. State one thing it cannot prove.
  4. Use numbered answers that match every numbered prompt.

Before independent work

Explain the goal in your own words. On the printable, answer every numbered part, show the requested evidence or calculation, and scan both pages before finishing class.
