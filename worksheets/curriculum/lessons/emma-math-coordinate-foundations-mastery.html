Coordinate Plane — Ordered Pairs & Signs

Today stays on coordinate-plane foundations. We are making the x/y order and direction signs automatic before adding a new idea.

1. Read an ordered pair in this order

An ordered pair is written (x, y).

x comes first: move left or right. y comes second: move up or down.

2. Match signs to directions

  • Right = positive x
  • Left = negative x
  • Up = positive y
  • Down = negative y

Worked example: “4 left and 3 up” becomes (-4, 3). Left controls x, so x is -4. Up controls y, so y is +3.

3. Slow down for direction words

Before writing a point, say: “x first: left/right; y second: up/down.” Then write the signs.

Now do your mastery check

  1. Label x and y first.
  2. For each direction, decide the x movement before the y movement.
  3. Check the sign before you write the ordered pair.
  4. Do the short follow-up sheet on your own.

If the order and signs are secure, the next coordinate lesson can move forward.
