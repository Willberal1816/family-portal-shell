Independent Reading: A Change of Plan

Emma · Reading · Thursday, November 12, 2026 · 45 minutes

What you are learning: Read your own book for at least 25 active minutes and explain a change in a character's plan with specific page evidence.
Read your own book for 25 active minutes FIRST. Then begin the response sheets.

Learn First

  1. Read your current book for 25 minutes before working on the response page. Continue from where you last stopped.
  2. A plan is an action a character intends to take. A change of plan occurs when the character chooses a different action.
  3. Look for an event or new piece of information between the first plan and the change. Report the details the text actually gives.
  4. Record page numbers for both the original plan and the later choice. If the cause is not stated, mark it as your possible inference.
  5. Use complete sentences to describe before, after, and what evidence connects them.

Two worked examples

Made-up model A: On page 6 Lila decides to walk home. On page 9 she sees a closed bridge and chooses the longer road. The bridge explicitly changes her route.
Made-up model B: A child takes a bus after hearing a bell. If the page never connects the bell to the decision, the bell might NOT be the reason.
A mistake to avoid: Do not invent a plan that is not in the pages you actually read. It is okay to explain that no plan changed today.
