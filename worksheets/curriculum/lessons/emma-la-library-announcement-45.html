Library Announcement: Facts, Not Guesses

Emma · Language Arts · Thursday, November 12, 2026 · 55 minutes

What you are learning: Write a complete school announcement with exact dates and evidence and without guessing reasons.

Learn First

  1. An announcement tells readers the key facts: who, what, when and where. Copy numbers and dates exactly from the source.
  2. Begin with one complete sentence saying the main event. Each sentence needs a person or thing and an action.
  3. For each supporting sentence, point to a matching line in the source. An idea not on that line is an inference, not a notice fact.
  4. A clear ending can tell readers what to bring or what to expect when the notice says so. Do not claim a reason unless one is supplied.
  5. Read your paragraph again and circle exact dates and numbers; make sure a reader can act on the announcement.

Two worked examples

Worked model A: Notice: 'Library book swap, Thursday at 2:00 p.m.' Exact announcement: 'The library will hold a book swap on Thursday at 2:00 p.m.'
Worked model B: Weak: 'Because last week's party was cancelled, the club will meet Friday.' The notice only says Friday; stronger: 'The club will meet Friday,' without guessing a cause.
A mistake to avoid: 'At the gym at 10:30' is not a full sentence. Change it to 'The craft fair begins at the gym at 10:30.'
