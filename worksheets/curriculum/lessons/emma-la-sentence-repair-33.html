Learn First

A complete sentence has a subject and a verb. A run-on pushes two complete ideas together without the right punctuation. When you repair it, keep both original ideas.

Put a comma after an introductory time phrase such as After the doors opened,. Use a comma before and when it joins two complete sentences.

Worked example

Original: Before the bus arrived Mia packed her bag her brother checked the tickets.

Repair: Before the bus arrived, Mia packed her bag, and her brother checked the tickets. Both actions are still present.
