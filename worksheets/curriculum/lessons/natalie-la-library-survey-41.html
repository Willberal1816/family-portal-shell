Library Exit Survey: Bound Every Claim

Natalie · Language Arts · Friday, October 30, 2026 · 55 minutes

What you will be able to do: Name the respondent group, use numerator and total, and state who was not surveyed.

Learn First

  1. Survey evidence describes the people who answered, not automatically everyone.
  2. Write numerator and total together: “18 of 30 respondents.”
  3. A complete report names place/time, counts or percent, and one unsurveyed-population limit.

Worked examples

Bounded: “Among 30 Saturday library visitors surveyed at the west exit, 18 preferred longer weekend hours.”
Too broad: “All residents want longer hours.” People outside that exit survey were not measured.

Class source

Source/data for this class: Practice survey: Volunteers asked 30 people leaving the west library exit on Saturday from 1–2 p.m. Eighteen chose longer weekend hours, 8 chose more study tables, and 4 chose more computers. No one entering other exits or visiting on other days was surveyed. Reasons were not asked.
