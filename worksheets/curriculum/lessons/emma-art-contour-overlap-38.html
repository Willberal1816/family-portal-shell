Contour and Overlap: Build Depth

Emma · Art · Tuesday, October 27, 2026

What you are learning: A contour line follows an object's visible edge. Overlap creates depth when one shape covers part of another. Size and placement strengthen the effect: nearer objects are often larger and lower on the page. A measurable reflection names an observable change such as “I widened the front leaf by 2 cm.”

Fully worked model

Draw a small circle partly behind a larger leaf. Erase the hidden part of the circle’s contour. The interrupted edge shows that the leaf is in front.

Source and materials

Materials: pencil, eraser, ruler, and one sheet of plain paper. No scissors or paint are needed.

Materials: pencil, eraser, ruler, and one sheet of plain paper. No scissors or paint are needed.
  1. Draw three simple plant forms with clear outer contours.
  2. Make the front form overlap both others; erase every hidden edge.
  3. Use the ruler to make the front form at least 2 cm wider than the farthest form.
