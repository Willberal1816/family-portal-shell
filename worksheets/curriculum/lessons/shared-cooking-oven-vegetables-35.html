Oven Basics: Roast Potatoes & Carrots

Today's real meal: a shared sheet pan of fork-tender roasted potatoes and carrots with browned edges. Both students do the SAME recipe as a team, but each fills in their OWN two-page lab record. This is one Cooking class per student, not two.

Learn First — Oven Safety and Technique

Watch the actual cooking demonstration first: The Busy Baker, How to Make Roasted Potatoes & Carrots. The video demonstrates the same potatoes-and-carrots roasting method with a larger batch and extra herbs; follow OUR smaller measured recipe below.

If video does not play in the portal, open the exact demonstration on YouTube. Adult supervision is required for knife/oven steps.

Mise en place means wash hands, clear and sanitize the work area, read the full recipe, measure ingredients, and set out tools BEFORE using heat. For roasting, cut vegetables into pieces about the same size so they cook evenly. Spread pieces in one layer with gaps rather than piling them up. The adult handles the oven, hot pan and sharp knife when supervision is required.

Worked model 1: even pieces. A 1-inch potato cube and a 1-inch carrot piece are closer in size than a 1-inch cube and a 3-inch chunk. Similar sizes help pieces become tender at similar times. Use a ruler to see what 1 inch looks like; adult checks cutting technique.
Worked model 2: one layer. The drawing shows a sheet pan from above. Pieces should not be stacked: Crowding traps steam; spreading pieces lets their surfaces brown.

Ingredients and tools — ONE shared batch (about four side servings)

IngredientsAmount
Potatoes, washed1 lb
Carrots, scrubbed½ lb
Olive or vegetable oil2 tablespoons
Salt¼ teaspoon (optional)
PepperPinch (optional)

Tools: oven, rimmed sheet pan, large bowl, measuring spoons, cutting board, ruler, knife, oven mitts, timer, fork, two printed individual lab records. Adult must be present for knife use and oven handling. Confirm ingredients/tools before starting; do not begin if required items are missing.

Numbered cooking steps

  1. Wash hands, wipe station, tie back hair, read recipe. Adult confirms knife/oven supervision.
  2. Adult preheats oven to 400°F. Place oven rack in middle. Wash potatoes and carrots; pat dry.
  3. With adult guidance, cut into roughly 1-inch pieces. Keep fingers curled back, use stable board, and stop if unsure.
  4. In bowl, toss pieces with 2 tablespoons oil and optional seasoning. Spread in ONE layer on the rimmed pan.
  5. Adult places pan in oven. Roast about 30–35 minutes; adult helps turn pieces once around 15 minutes. Check earlier if pieces are small. Timing is a guide, not proof of doneness.
  6. Adult removes hot pan. Test one piece carefully with a fork: it should pierce easily and have lightly browned edges. If still firm, adult returns pan briefly and retests. Cool before tasting.
  7. Serve safely, describe texture and flavor, wash tools, clean counters, and put food away according to household guidance. Record ACTUAL times and observations in each student's own lab record.
Stop/ask adult: sharp knife, hot sheet pan, oven rack, smoke, burns, or uncertainty. No student removes the pan or reaches into the oven alone. Never claim a food test happened if it did not.
