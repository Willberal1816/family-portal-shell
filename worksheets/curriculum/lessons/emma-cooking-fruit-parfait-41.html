Make a Fruit-and-Yogurt Parfait

Emma · Cooking · Friday, October 30, 2026 · 60 minutes

Today's goal: Follow a demonstration to build a real layered snack, measure ingredients, and keep a kitchen work area safe.

Watch the actual demonstration

Exact instructional source: Primrose Schools: Yogurt Granola Parfait — watch the finished snack being made. If playback is blocked, open the original video link and follow the steps shown below. Additional official written safety/recipe reference.

Prepare / parent check: For ONE portion: 1 clean transparent cup/bowl, 1 clean spoon, 1/2 cup chilled yogurt (or suitable safe family substitute), 1/2 cup ready-to-eat small fruit, 2 tablespoons granola or breakfast cereal. Adult checks allergies and food labels. If fruit needs a knife, adult prepares it. One measuring cup, tablespoon, soap, running water, towel, two printable sheets.

Learn First — follow in order

  1. WHAT YOU ARE MAKING: one real cup with a pale yogurt layer, a colored fruit layer, and a crunchy granola top. It is a ready-to-eat cold parfait, not a cooking demonstration using heat.
  2. Watch the exact parfait-building video first. Pause after the ingredient display. Assemble everything on a washed surface, with a clean cup and spoon. Do not cut fruit yourself.
  3. WASH hands with soap for 20 seconds; check the yogurt is cold and that the fruit is ready-to-eat; have an adult verify allergies and packaging.
  4. MEASURE 1/2 cup yogurt into the cup. ADD 1/2 cup of safe ready-to-eat small fruit as the next visible layer. TOP with 2 tablespoons granola or breakfast cereal, using a clean spoon. This is one parfait.
  5. SHOW the finished layers to a parent, photograph/draw it if permitted, then clean the area; refrigerate uneaten perishable food. Each student has their own cup and own printable.

Two examples of the finished work and skills

Finished example: bottom = 1/2 cup yogurt; middle = 1/2 cup blueberries; top = 2 tablespoons granola. From the side you can see three clear layers.
Worked measurement: two 1/4-cup scoops of yogurt = 1/2 cup. Two 1-tablespoon scoops of granola = 2 tablespoons.

Picture of what you will make

Video-aligned finished snack: A cold yogurt-and-fruit-and-granola parfait with visible layers. The exact fruit flavor, cereal type and yogurt brand may change to approved available family-safe ingredients; the technique stays the same.
