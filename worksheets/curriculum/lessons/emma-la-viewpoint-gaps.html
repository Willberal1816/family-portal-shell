Viewpoint and Missing Information

Goal: Explain what an account emphasizes, what its narrator can know, and what information is missing.

Learn First

Two honest accounts can differ because each writer has a different position, purpose, audience, and access to information.

Account A: A firefighter describes smoke, alarms, and rescue decisions. Account B: A shop owner describes lost inventory and rebuilding. Both may be accurate, but each selects different details.
Ask three questions: (1) What does this person directly observe? (2) What purpose shapes the details chosen? (3) What important information can this person not know?
Neutral synthesis: “The firefighter account explains the emergency response, while the shop owner account shows the event’s effect on local businesses. Together they show both immediate action and longer-term impact.”
A strong answer cites an exact detail, names the viewpoint, and explains the information gap.
