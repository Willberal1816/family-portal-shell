Pasta Basics: Boil, Drain, Finish

Emma and Natalie · Cooking · Thursday, October 22, 2026 · 70 minutes

Goal: Safely prepare an actual simple pasta dish while demonstrating mise en place, boiling, tasting for doneness, and cleanup.

One class: Read this Learn First lesson, work through both examples, complete all six items on TWO printable pages, scan both pages, and Finish Class. Learn First and Printable are not separate assignments.

Learn First

This is one SHARED cooking lab with a SEPARATE lab record for each student. Ask an adult to supervise boiling water, hot pots, and draining. Mise en place means setting out measured ingredients and tools before starting. A rolling boil has bubbles breaking across the surface. Stir pasta after adding it so pieces do not stick; follow the package's time range and taste a piece when an adult says it is safe.

Two worked models

Model 1. Recipe for TWO servings: 8 oz dry pasta; about 2 quarts water; optional ½ teaspoon salt for water; 1 tablespoon butter OR olive oil; optional 2 tablespoons grated cheese. Use one pot and one colander. Do not use both butter and oil unless the adult approves.
Model 2. Example sequence: wash hands → clear and clean station → measure pasta → boil water with adult → add and stir pasta → check package time and test a cooled piece → adult drains → toss with butter or oil → serve → wash and reset. Record actual observations, not invented times.
Check for mistakes: Safety: no student carries a full hot pot or drains boiling water alone. Keep hands, sleeves, and cords clear of heat. Ask the adult to handle the colander/draining and check safe temperature before tasting.
