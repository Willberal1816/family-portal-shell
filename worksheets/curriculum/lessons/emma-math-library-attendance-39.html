Library Attendance: Mean, Median, and Outliers

Emma · Math · Wednesday, October 28, 2026 · 50 minutes

What you are learning: Compare mean and median, calculate an even-count median by averaging the two middle values, and explain how one outlier changes a data summary.

Learn First

Order values before finding the median. With six values, the median is the average of positions 3 and 4.

The mean uses every value: add all values, then divide by the number of values. An outlier can pull the mean more than the median.

Fully worked model

For 6, 8, 9, 11, 12, 14, the middle values are 9 and 11. Median = (9 + 11) ÷ 2 = 10. Mean = 60 ÷ 6 = 10.
