Paper Bridge Challenge

Project · Learn First
What you are learning: Use a simple engineering cycle to build, test, improve, and explain a paper bridge.
School word: prototype = an early version built so you can test and improve an idea

Key ideas

  • First understand the goal and the limits on materials.
  • Build one design and test it the same way each time.
  • Use what happened in the test to make one purposeful change, then test again.

Example

Example: If a flat sheet bends too easily, folding the paper may change its shape and stiffness. Test the new design instead of assuming it is stronger.
When this makes sense, go back to your School Portal and do the Practice step.
