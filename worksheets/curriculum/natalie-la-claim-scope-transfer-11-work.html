Source / data for these questions:
Source A — Greenhouse log: Tuesday: 12 plants, 8 showed dry leaves. Wednesday after shade cloth: 12 plants, 3 showed dry leaves. Source B — Volunteer: “It felt cooler when we walked in on Wednesday.” Source C — Thursday’s display included a new plant label.
1. Classify Source A, B, and C as strong, partial, or irrelevant for the exact dry-leaf count. Give a source-specific reason for each.
2. Write a careful, precise claim using the Tuesday and Wednesday plant counts.
3. Calculate the difference and explain how matched plant totals matter.
4. Name one plausible alternative explanation for the change; say why the records do not rule it out.
5. Write one extra observation that should be collected for the next seven days; identify who records it and what is measured.
