Corroboration: Check Another Source

Shared Social Studies

Mini Lesson

Historians compare different sources instead of trusting one account alone. Corroboration means checking another independent source to see what agrees, conflicts, or adds detail.

Practice

1. A diary says a flood began before sunrise. Name one different source you could check.
2. What could that second source add that the diary might not?
3. Why is another person’s diary a stronger corroborating source than rereading the same diary?
4. Two sources disagree about the crowd size at an event. Name one reason both could be honest but different.
5. Write one sentence explaining what corroboration helps a historian do.
