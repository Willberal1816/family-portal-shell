Percent Increase & Decrease

Natalie · Math · Independent Work
Directions: For each problem, identify the original amount first. Show the change, divide by the original amount when needed, and label whether the change is an increase or decrease.
1. A concert ticket changes from $24 to $30. Find the amount of increase and the percent increase. Explain why the original price belongs in the denominator.
2. A backpack marked $48 is reduced to $36. Find the amount of decrease and the percent decrease. Show your reasoning.
3. A game has 80 players one week and 92 the next. Estimate the percent increase first, then calculate it exactly. Was your estimate reasonable?
4. A student says a drop from 60 to 45 is a 25% decrease because 15 is 25% of 60. Check the claim and explain each step.
5. A price rises by 20% and is now $54. What was the original price? Use an equation or proportional relationship and verify your answer.
6. Create one realistic percent-increase situation and one percent-decrease situation. Give the original and new amounts and solve both.
