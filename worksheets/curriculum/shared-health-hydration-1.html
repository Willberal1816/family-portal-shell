Hydration & Body Signals

Shared Health

Mini Lesson

Water helps regulate temperature, move nutrients, and support normal body functions. Thirst, activity, heat, and urine color can all give clues about hydration, but no single sign is perfect.

Practice

1. Name two jobs water does in the body.
2. Why might someone need more fluids on a hot active day?
3. What are two body signals that can suggest it is time to drink?
4. Why is forcing huge amounts of water not the goal?
5. Write a practical hydration plan for a school day with one active period.
