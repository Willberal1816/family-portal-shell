Paper Bridge Challenge

Shared · Project
Use two books or cups as supports, paper for the bridge, and small equal weights such as coins. Build, test, change, and test again.
1. Test one flat paper bridge. How many weights did it hold?
2. Change the paper shape without adding more paper. Describe what you changed.
3. Test again. How many weights did it hold now?
4. Which design worked better, and what do you think made it stronger?
