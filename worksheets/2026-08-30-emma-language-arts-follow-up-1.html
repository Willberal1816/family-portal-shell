Language Arts Follow-Up 1

Emma - Short correction practice, not a new placement test
Quick teaching notes
Main idea: what the whole passage is mostly about, not just one detail.
Inference: a conclusion supported by clues in the text plus what you know.
Prefer: to like or choose one option more than another.
Fragment: a group of words that is missing a complete thought.

Read

Maya planted basil, tomatoes, and marigolds in three pots. She watered them every morning. After a week, the tomato plant had grown the most. Maya moved the pots so each plant could get enough sunlight.

1. Main idea

What is the passage mostly about?

2. Supporting detail

Name one thing Maya did to care for the plants.

3. Inference

Why can you infer that sunlight matters to the plants? Use one clue from the passage.

4. Vocabulary

If Maya says, “I prefer tomatoes to basil,” what does prefer mean?

5. Fix the fragment

Because the tomato plant needed sunlight. Rewrite it as one complete sentence.

6. Add a specific detail

“Kids should learn cooking in school because it is an important life skill.” Add one specific supporting sentence.
