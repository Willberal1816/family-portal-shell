Natalie — Sentence structure & organized paragraphs
Why this follow-up: Your reading comprehension was strong. This short practice focuses only on the skills that need another pass: recognizing complex sentences, fixing fragments, and organizing ideas into complete paragraphs.

A. Sentence types

Write simple, compound, or complex for each sentence.
1) The birds rested near the lake.
2) The sun went down, and the flock continued south.
3) When the weather cooled, the birds began to migrate.
4) We turned off the porch light because migrating birds were passing through.
5) The city dimmed unnecessary lights, but safety lights stayed on.
6) Birds use less energy when they can travel without circling bright buildings.

B. Fix the fragments

Rewrite each fragment as a complete sentence. You may add words or combine it with another idea.
7) So that birds can safely migrate.
8) Because bright lights can confuse them.
9) When the flock reaches its destination.

C. Build two organized paragraphs

Use your original idea: Communities should turn off unnecessary lights at night during bird migration. Keep your own position, but develop it more fully.
Paragraph 1 plan — State your position and explain your first reason.
Paragraph 2 plan — Add another supporting reason or example, then finish with a concluding thought.
