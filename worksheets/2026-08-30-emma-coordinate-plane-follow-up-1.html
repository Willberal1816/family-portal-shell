Remember: The horizontal axis is x. The vertical axis is y. Coordinates are written (x, y) by convention. The first number is the x-coordinate and tells the horizontal position. The second number is the y-coordinate and tells the vertical position. Order matters because switching the numbers usually identifies a different point.
