Primary & Secondary Sources - Quick Follow-Up

Emma - Short correction check
History detective rule: A primary source comes from the time/event being studied. A secondary source is created later to explain or analyze it.

1.

A documentary made in 2020 about a 1969 moon mission is Primary or Secondary? Explain why.

2.

A historian's article written later analyzing immigration in 1905 is Primary or Secondary? Explain why.

3.

You have one child's diary from a major flood. Name one different source you would check before making a conclusion, and say what that source could add.

4.

Source A is a diary entry written that morning. Source B is a history book written 80 years later. Which source gives the most direct evidence of what one person personally saw? Explain why.
Goal: classify later analysis correctly, use a different corroborating source, and explain why evidence is direct.
