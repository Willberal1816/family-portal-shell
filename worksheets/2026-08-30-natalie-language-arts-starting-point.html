Language Arts Starting Point

Natalie — Reading, grammar & writing
Starting-point check — not a grade. Work on your own. If you are unsure, write “not sure.” This helps choose what to teach next.

A. Read

Each spring and fall, millions of birds travel long distances between breeding and wintering areas. Many species migrate at night, when the air is cooler and the sky can help them navigate. In cities, however, bright lights from buildings can create problems. Birds may become disoriented by artificial light, circle buildings, or waste energy that they need for the rest of their journey. Researchers and conservation groups have tested a simple idea: during the busiest migration nights, buildings can turn off unnecessary lights. Programs sometimes called “Lights Out” ask offices and building owners to dim decorative lighting and switch off empty floors. The change does not eliminate every danger birds face, but studies of migration routes have found that darker nighttime conditions can reduce collisions and confusion. Supporters also point out that using less unnecessary light saves electricity. Critics sometimes worry about safety or inconvenience, so many programs focus only on lights that are not needed for people to see or move safely. The goal is not to make cities dark. It is to use light more carefully when wildlife is moving through.
1) What is the main idea?
2) Give one detail that supports the main idea.
3) Why might circling a lit building make migration harder for a bird?
4) In the passage, what does disoriented most nearly mean? Explain how you know.
5) What is the author’s main purpose: entertain, persuade only one side, or explain a problem and response? Use evidence.

B. Language

6) Choose the correct verb: The flock of geese (moves / move) across the sky.
7) Rewrite to fix the run-on: The sky was cloudy the birds continued south.
8) Is this sentence simple, compound, or complex? When the sun set, the birds began their journey.
9) Add needed punctuation: Maya said we should turn off the extra lights tonight
10) What does the prefix re- mean in reappear?

C. Writing — choose one

A) Should communities reduce unnecessary nighttime lighting during bird migration? Explain your position.
B) Explain a skill or hobby that takes practice and what helps someone improve.
C) Explain why a topic you care about deserves more attention.
Write about 2 organized paragraphs. Use complete sentences and revise once before you finish.
