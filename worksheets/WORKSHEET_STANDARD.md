# Moran School Worksheet Standard

- Identify the student and lesson in the printed header; do not ask the student to rewrite their name.
- Do not include blank Name or Date fields. Assignment ownership/date already live in the School Portal and print metadata.
- Keep worksheets paper-first, uncluttered, and sized for US Letter printing.
- Provide scaffolds such as grids, number lines, tables, charts, fraction models, or diagrams when using the structure is the skill.
- Require the student to construct the structure only when construction itself is a learning objective.
- Put teaching in the Portal/video; keep worksheet directions short and task-focused.
- Preserve enough working space to show reasoning, not just final answers.
- Future scanner submission metadata may be added later without changing the student-facing layout.
