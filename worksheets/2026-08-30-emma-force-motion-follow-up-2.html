Force and Motion — Follow-Up 2

Emma — Moving objects and balanced forces
Remember: Balanced forces mean the object’s motion does not change. An object may be still, or it may keep moving at the same speed and direction.
1. A hockey puck is sliding straight at the same speed. Its motion is not changing. Are the forces balanced or unbalanced? Explain why.
2. True or false: “If an object is moving, the forces on it must be unbalanced.” Explain in one sentence.
