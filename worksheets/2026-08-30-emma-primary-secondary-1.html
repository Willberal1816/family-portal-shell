Primary & Secondary Sources

Emma — Shared Social Studies
History detective rule: A primary source comes from the time/event being studied. A secondary source was created later to explain or analyze it.

1. Sort the sources

Write Primary or Secondary.
A soldier’s 1863 diary
A 2024 textbook chapter about the Civil War
A photograph taken during a 1969 moon mission
A documentary made in 2020 about that moon mission
A letter written by an immigrant in 1905
A historian’s article analyzing immigration in 1905

2. Think like a historian

You find a diary entry from a child who lived through a major flood.
a) What could this source help you learn?
b) What might this one diary not tell you about the whole flood?
c) Name one other source you would want before making a conclusion.

3. Evidence

Source A: “The town square was crowded before sunrise.” — diary entry written that morning

Source B: “Thousands gathered in the town square that day.” — history book written 80 years later
Which source gives the most direct evidence about what one person personally saw? Why?

4. Emma challenge

If two people saw the same event from different places, why might their stories be different even if neither person is lying?
