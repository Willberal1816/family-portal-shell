Force & Motion Follow-Up

Natalie — Friction and net force
Why this follow-up: You understand pushes, pulls, and balanced vs. unbalanced forces. This short practice focuses only on what makes moving objects slow down and how opposing forces combine.

1. What stopped the book?

A book slides across a table after you stop pushing it. What force causes it to slow down and stop? Explain in one sentence.

2. Net force

One person pushes a box right with 10 N. Another person pushes left with 6 N.
Are the forces balanced or unbalanced? What direction will the box tend to move? Explain why.

3. Changing a rolling ball

A soccer ball is rolling forward. Name two different forces that could change its motion. For each force, explain whether it would speed the ball up, slow it down, stop it, or change its direction.
