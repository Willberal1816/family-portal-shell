Language Arts Starting Point

Emma — Reading, grammar & writing
Starting-point check — not a grade. Some questions may be about things you have not learned yet. If you are unsure, write “not sure.”

A. Read

A school wanted more butterflies and bees in its garden, so students planted flowers that bloom at different times of the year. They also left a shallow dish of water with stones where insects could land safely. At first, the garden looked quiet. After several weeks, students began seeing bees visit the flowers in the morning and butterflies arrive on sunny afternoons. The class kept a simple chart of what they saw. They noticed that different insects seemed to prefer different flowers. The students learned that a pollinator garden does not need to be huge. It needs useful plants, water, and a place where insects can find food without being disturbed too much.
1) What is the main idea?
2) Name two things the students added to help pollinators.
3) What can you infer from the fact that different insects visited different flowers?
4) What does prefer mean in the passage?

B. Language

5) Choose the correct verb: The group of students (works / work) in the garden.
6) Rewrite to fix the fragment: Because the flowers needed water.
7) Rewrite to fix the run-on: The butterfly landed the students watched quietly.
8) Add punctuation and capitalization: emma asked can we plant sunflowers too
9) What does the prefix un- mean in unusual?

C. Writing — choose one

A) Explain how to care for a plant or animal.
B) Should kids learn cooking as part of school? Explain why or why not.
C) Teach the reader about something you know a lot about.
Write one clear paragraph of about 6–8 sentences. You may make a quick list of ideas before you start.
