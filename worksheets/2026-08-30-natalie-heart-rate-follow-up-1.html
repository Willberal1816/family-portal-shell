Heart Rate & Movement - Follow-Up 1

Natalie - Health / Life Skills
Quick reminder: During exercise, working muscles need more oxygen. The heart speeds up to move more oxygen-rich blood around the body.

1. Why does heart rate usually increase during exercise?

Write one complete sentence using the words muscles, oxygen, and blood.

2. Fair-test thinking

You want to compare your heart rate after two different activities. Name one variable you should keep the same so the comparison is fair.
Examples: how long you exercise, how long you rest first, when you measure your pulse, or how long you count your pulse.

3. Apply it

Suppose you compare jumping jacks and fast walking. Write one sentence explaining how you would make the test fair.
