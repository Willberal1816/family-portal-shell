Heart Rate & Movement - Follow-Up 1

Emma - Health / Life Skills
Quick reminder: When you exercise, working muscles need more oxygen. Your heart pumps faster to move more oxygen-rich blood around your body.

1. Why does your heart beat faster during exercise?

Write one complete sentence using the words muscles, oxygen, and blood.

2. Make the comparison fair

If you repeated the same movement tomorrow, name one specific thing you would keep the same.
Examples: the same movement, the same amount of time moving, the same rest time before measuring, or the same way of counting your pulse.
