Quick teaching note
Balanced forces are equal overall, so the object's motion does not change. An object can be still, or it can keep moving at the same speed and direction.

Unbalanced forces are not equal overall, so the object's motion changes — it may start, stop, speed up, slow down, or turn.

1.

Two people push a box from opposite sides with equal force. The box stays in place. Are the forces balanced or unbalanced? Why?

2.

One person pushes harder than the other. Are the forces balanced or unbalanced? What happens to the box?
