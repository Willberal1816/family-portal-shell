Force & Motion Foundations

Emma — Shared Science
After the lesson video: Use a small book, a pencil, and a clear table or floor space. Do not use anything breakable.

1. Pushes and pulls

Write push, pull, or both for each:
Opening a drawer
Kicking a ball
Tug-of-war
Closing a door

2. Mini investigation

Place a small book on a table. Give it a gentle push. Then try a stronger push.
a) What changed when you used more force?
b) What eventually made the book slow down and stop?

3. Balanced or unbalanced?

Two people push a box equally hard from opposite sides. The box does not move.
Balanced or unbalanced? Why?
One person pushes a chair forward and nobody pushes back.
Balanced or unbalanced? What would you expect the chair to do?

4. Explain

In your own words, what is a force?

5. Emma challenge

Draw or describe one example from real life where a force makes something start, one where it makes something stop, and one where it changes direction.
